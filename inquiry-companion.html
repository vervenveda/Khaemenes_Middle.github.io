Khaemenes Academy · Grades 6–8

Investigate, compare and substantiate

Optional offline-friendly instructional companion. No student data is collected, and no quizzes, grades or existing mastery controls are modified.

1 · Explore

Compare two firsthand or reliable sources; identify perspectives, dates and claims.

2 · Make connections

Model a real problem with a table, diagram or equation, then inspect assumptions and limitations.

3 · Demonstrate understanding

Construct an argument with cited evidence, a counterargument and a concise reflection on uncertainty.

Mentor and family guidance

Use scaffolded reading for Grade 6 and increasingly independent inquiry through Grade 8. Existing weekly assessments remain unchanged.

Invite learners to explain how they know, what might change their conclusion, and what they would explore next. Maintain existing course assessments separately.
