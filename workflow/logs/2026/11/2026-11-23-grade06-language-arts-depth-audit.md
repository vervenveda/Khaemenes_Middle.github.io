# Grade 6 Language Arts Deep Curriculum Audit — 2026-11-23

## Scope

All 36 Grade 6 Language Arts week pages were reviewed against the dedicated Middle School Language Arts engine and its High-School-derived evidence/revision workflow.

## Findings

1. **Weeks 01–07 were stronger than the engine adapter recognized.** They already contain Academy-original sustained readings, explicit learning targets, worked examples/think-alouds, student practice, facilitator guidance, and portfolio evidence. The prior engine adapter only read later-week labels such as Focus / Objective / Activity / Evidence, causing these richer early weeks to surface incomplete context in the engine workspace.

2. **Weeks 08–36 have strong writing and revision continuity.** The later curriculum consistently asks learners to explain, argue, report, critique, publish, revise, or defend evidence. This was preserved.

3. **Explicit grammar/mechanics and morphology were uneven.** Revision was strong, but grammar, syntax, morphology, and vocabulary were not named consistently enough across the full year to guarantee a visible Grade 6 progression.

4. **Weeks 08–23 needed more sustained reading before writing.** They contained strong explanatory mini-lessons but several weeks lacked a dedicated source text substantial enough for the engine's Read → Annotate → Analyze sequence.

5. **Weeks 24–36 already form a strong source/research/capstone arc.** Source limits, civic evidence, reliability, interviews, research questions, thesis formation, debate, media analysis, capstone research, oral defense, and Grade 7 readiness are well represented and were retained.

## Upgrades

- Expanded the Language Arts engine adapter to recognize the rich Week 01–07 curriculum labels.
- Added a 36-week Grade 6 literacy spine with explicit mechanics, morphology/vocabulary, reading-evidence, and writing/revision targets.
- Added Academy-original sustained reading-evidence labs for Weeks 08–23, each with three paragraphs and three evidence questions.
- Exposed the Grade 6 skill spine and sustained reading lab in the Language Arts engine and evidence exports.
- Preserved official assessment, mastery, learner-record, and placement authority unchanged.
- Added a dedicated Grade 6 Language Arts depth regression gate.
