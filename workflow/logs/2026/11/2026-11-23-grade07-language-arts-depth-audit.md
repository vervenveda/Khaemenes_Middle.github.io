# Grade 7 Language Arts Deep Curriculum Audit — 2026-11-23

## Findings

- Weeks 01–07 are richly authored Academy-original workshops and already exceed the generic template.
- Weeks 08–36 retained five daily lessons and evidence/revision language, but much of the instructional surface was generic and did not consistently supply a sustained source text.
- Grade 7 needed a visible progression beyond Grade 6 in syntax, morphology, source evaluation, multi-source reasoning, historical/scientific literacy, argument, oral defense, and research synthesis.
- The shared Language Arts engine was already capable of supporting these upgrades once grade-specific curriculum data were supplied.

## Upgrades

- Added a 36-week Grade 7 literacy spine with explicit mechanics, morphology/vocabulary, reading-evidence, and writing/revision targets.
- Added Academy-original sustained reading-evidence labs for Weeks 08–36.
- Readings increase Grade 7 complexity through source purpose, sampling limits, model limitations, multi-source corroboration, systems reasoning, historical sourcing, ethics, counterclaims, and capstone synthesis.
- Generalized the shared Language Arts engine so grade-specific spines/readings can be supplied by Grade 6, Grade 7, and later Grade 8 without creating separate pedagogy engines.
- Preserved the Grade 7 Week 01–07 rich workshop format.
- Preserved official assessments, mastery, placement, learner records, and curriculum unlock authority.
