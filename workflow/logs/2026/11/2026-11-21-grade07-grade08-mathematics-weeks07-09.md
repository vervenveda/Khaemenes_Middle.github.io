## Curriculum authored
- **30** original daily mathematics laboratory sections (2 grades × 3 weeks × 5 days).
- **120** four-part mathematics challenges and **120** corresponding teacher-key entries.
- Every lesson has vocabulary in context, independent worked mathematical model, misconception checkpoint, four practice tasks, formative notes with explicit label, verification reminder and teacher-key disclosure.
- **Grade 7 Week 07:** equations, strict/inclusive inequalities, signed inequality reversals, budgets and solution verification.
- **Grade 7 Week 08:** scaled lengths, area versus perimeter scaling, geometric proportion, scale-model claims and cumulative review.
- **Grade 7 Week 09:** circumference, circle area, semicircle arc and perimeter, design material models, cumulative retrieval.
- **Grade 8 Week 07:** translations, reflections, rotations, congruence under rigid motions, compositions and mathematical congruence evidence.
- **Grade 8 Week 08:** coordinate dilation, scale factors, similarity versus congruence, side/area/volume scaling, corresponding sides.
- **Grade 8 Week 09:** Pythagorean theorem, missing legs, right-triangle checks, coordinate distance and design applications.
