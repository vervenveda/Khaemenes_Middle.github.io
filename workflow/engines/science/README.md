# Science Engine — Dedicated Middle School Instruction Runtime

**Status:** Active on all Grade 6–8 Science week pages.

The Middle School Science curriculum was already deeply authored and remains the content authority. This engine restores the missing dedicated subject-runtime layer using the High School Science instruction-engine contract as the architectural reference.

## Responsibility

The Science engine organizes the existing five-day curriculum into:

1. Observe & Prepare
2. Model & Explain
3. Investigate & Analyze
4. Check & Revise
5. CER & Transfer

The engine exposes instructional stages and curriculum-contract inspection. It does not replace lesson text or generate a parallel curriculum.

## Authority boundaries

The Science engine owns instructional presentation only.

- no storage writes
- no official mastery writes
- no grade writes
- no placement changes
- no assessment authority
- no protected assessment content
- no page chrome, translator, ticker, or toolbar ownership
- no learner-record authority

Official mastery remains with the Middle School assessment engine. Learner records and continuity remain with the established Middle School record systems.
