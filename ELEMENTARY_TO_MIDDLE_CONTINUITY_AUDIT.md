# Khaemenes Academy — Grade 05 Through Grade 08 Continuity Audit

**Scope:** Grade 05 → Grade 06 → Grade 07 → Grade 08  
**Purpose:** confirm academic progression, identity continuity, advanced-learner access, mastery authority, and transition integrity across the Elementary-to-Middle-School boundary.

---

## 1. Canonical learner pathway

```text
Academy Family Registry
        ↓
NAIB intake / resource direction / delegation
        ↓
Khaemenes Academy
        ↓
Archaemenes
        ↓
Grade / course context
        ↓
Assessment evidence
        ↓
Adult verification
        ↓
Learner-scoped formal record
        ↓
Certificate / readiness evidence
```

Formal placement and learning access are intentionally separate concepts.

A learner may preview more advanced material without changing formal grade placement.

## 4. Advanced Grade 05 trajectory

Advanced learners should not be forced to choose between challenge and record integrity.

The approved model is:

```text
Formal Grade 05 placement
        ↓
Advanced preview / enrichment access
        ↓
Selected Grade 06 curriculum and resources
        ↓
Archaemenes remains the Academy mentor
        ↓
Grade 05 formal record remains authoritative
        ↓
Explicit family / Academy promotion when appropriate
```

Advanced preview:

- may expose selected Grade 06 lessons and subject halls;
- may support readiness diagnostics and challenge work;
- may be used before Grade 05 certification is complete;
- does not create Grade 06 mastery records;
- does not unlock Grade 06 certification;
- does not rewrite stage or grade;
- does not auto-promote.

Recommended advanced-preview focus:

1. Grade 06 ratios / rates / percent;
2. formal textual citation and argument analysis;
3. CER and Middle School science systems;
4. World Languages & Global Culture;
5. research notebook and source-management routines;
6. executive-function systems for multi-subject Middle School work.

## 8. Identity and authority audit

### PASS — one learner identity

Grades 05–08 do not create independent competing learner identities in the hardened architecture.

### PASS — formal placement remains upstream

Grade portals consume formal stage / grade placement rather than defining it locally.

### PASS — NAIB delegation

NAIB receives context and delegates to the appropriate Academy or resource destination.

### PASS — Archaemenes continuity

Khaemenes Academy provides Archaemenes across the Grade 05–08 pathway.

Presentation develops from:

- Grade 05: **Young Scholar**;
- Middle School: **Academy Scholar**.

### PASS — mentor is not grader

Archaemenes supports learning but does not award mastery.

### PASS — adult verification

Formal weekly, midterm, final, portfolio, and transition evidence is recorded through adult-facing tools.

### PASS — learner-scoped records

Grades 05–08 use learner-scoped formal records with protected legacy migration.

### PASS — no silent promotion

Every stage / grade transition remains explicit.

## 9. Remaining upstream dependency

The Academy Family Registry still requires its planned `stage + grade` hardening so that formal placement can be represented canonically across all grade portals.

Target examples:

```json
{
  "stage": "elementary",
  "grade": "grade-05"
}
```

```json
{
  "stage": "middle",
  "grade": "grade-06"
}
```

Until that upstream layer is upgraded, hardened grade portals correctly fail closed rather than inventing formal placement.

---

## 10. Overall audit result

**PASS — the Grade 05 through Grade 08 academic progression is coherent and the hardened authority model is internally consistent.**

The principal transition improvement required was not a curriculum rewrite. It was a clearer Grade 05 → Grade 06 bridge that supports advanced learners without conflating enrichment access with formal promotion.

The Middle School landing page should therefore distinguish:

1. formal Grade 06 entry;
2. advanced Grade 05 preview;
3. formal Grade 07 entry;
4. formal Grade 08 entry;
5. family transition planning;
6. High School transition from Grade 08.
