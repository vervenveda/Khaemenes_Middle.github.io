This catalog identifies curriculum topics that would benefit from short, age-appropriate instructional videos. These are requested topics, not approved or currently embedded videos. No external platform or unreviewed video is automatically loaded.

Before a video appears in student lessons, verify factual quality, grade fit, accessibility, transcript or accurate captions, source permissions, availability, and an offline text alternative. The weekly assessment remains independent of optional videos.
