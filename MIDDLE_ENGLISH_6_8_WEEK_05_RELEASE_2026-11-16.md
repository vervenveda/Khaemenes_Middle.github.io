## Verification of the numerical examples

- **Grade 6:** `3/4 ÷ 1/4 = 3` and `2/3 ÷ 1/6 = 4`, confirmed by the bar model and `4 × 1/6 = 4/6 = 2/3`. This models how to explain a procedure and verify a result, not an English replacement for mathematics assessment.
- **Grade 7:** The fictional sequence is `-2.5°C`, `+1.5°C`, then `-0.5°C`. The first change is `+4.0°C`; the second is `-2.0°C`. These signed differences are descriptions of the invented observations and do not establish causes.
- **Grade 8:** The fictional archival constraints are `p + m = 18` folders and `2p + m = 26` caption cards. They give `p = 8` photo folders and `m = 10` manuscript folders. The rival `p = 10, m = 8` interpretation fits the folder total but gives 28 captions, contradicting the second constraint. Numerical consistency alone does not authenticate the fictional inventory or its source assumptions.
