## Numerical and source fidelity

**Grade 6:** XII and decimal 12 express twelve using different writing conventions. Conventional classroom Roman examples include IV for four. Modern opposites -3 and +3 have absolute value 3. The lesson does not claim all historical cultures used the same modern symbols or imply that numeral writing reflects intelligence.

**Grade 7:** Fictional three-kit planning rule `3(x + 4) = 3x + 12` is distinguished from a mistaken publicity rule `3x + 4`. If x = 5 cards, the respective totals are 27 and 19 paper pieces; the hypothetical calculation does **not** establish actual material usage or a historical archive record.

**Grade 8:** Hypothetical `3.2 × 10^6` equals 3,200,000 entries; `4.5 × 10^-4` meters equals 0.00045 meters. Scientific notation does not authenticate values, determine importance or establish scientific accuracy. The automated regression uses tolerance for decimal floating-point arithmetic rather than exact IEEE-754 comparison.
