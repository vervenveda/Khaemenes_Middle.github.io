### Weekly academic progression

- **Monday:** engage with original source material; define the question and cite initial evidence.
- **Tuesday:** close reading and interpretation, including observation vs. inference or theme/central idea.
- **Wednesday:** compare sources, evaluate evidence, and synthesize accurate conclusions.
- **Thursday:** draft a reasoned argument, identify weaknesses, and show substantial revision.
- **Friday:** produce independent comparison or defense and assemble teacher-reviewed portfolio evidence.

The cognitive demands intentionally rise by grade. Grade 6 emphasizes accurate attribution; Grade 7 compares narrative theme, informational claims and proportions; Grade 8 ranks source strength, qualifies trend interpretations, synthesizes three documents and defends its position.
