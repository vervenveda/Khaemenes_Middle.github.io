## Shared five-day teaching architecture

- **Monday:** original reading; define a literary, evidentiary or informational focus.
- **Tuesday:** study structure, narrator perspective, technical terms and relevant examples.
- **Wednesday:** make careful source notes; identify or compare unsupported claims; synthesize.
- **Thursday:** draft a substantive and grade-appropriate explanation or argument.
- **Friday:** revise, reflect, document evidence, and prepare for teacher/family feedback or oral defense.

## Numerical and reading integrity

Grade 06's invented cycling report distinguishes 12 km in 3 h = 4 km/h from 15 km in 3 h = 5 km/h; the comparison cannot establish causes.

Grade 07's invented cart example distinguishes 18 books in 3 trips = 6 books/trip from 20 books in 4 trips = 5 books/trip; the text separates rates, totals and community effects.

Grade 08's entirely fictional cost model defines `A(n) = 6 + 2n` and `B(n) = 4n`. B is lower at one and two sessions, the modeled costs tie at three, and A is lower at four; real accessibility and fairness cannot be inferred from these fictional functions alone.
