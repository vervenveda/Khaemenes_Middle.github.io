Above and Below a Reference Line

During a fictional Earth-systems lesson, students recorded measurements relative to a reference elevation. One station stood 3.5 meters above the reference and another was 4.5 meters below it. A student added 3.5 and −4.5 and incorrectly reported that the separation was one meter. The class placed both numbers on a number line and calculated 3.5 − (−4.5) = 8 meters. Later they examined deviations from typical rainfall, noting that a negative deviation did not imply negative physical rainfall. Their revised report identified each reference and unit before interpreting changes.

Original fictional Academy reading; offline.

1. State the mathematical mistake and correction.

2. Cite two measurements and the correct calculation.

3. Explain why −2 mm departure does not mean negative rainfall.
