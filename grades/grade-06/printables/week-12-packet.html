The Display Box Design Review

At a fictional community arts center, a student team designed an open-top display box. The box needed a base measuring 8 cm by 5 cm and sides 4 cm high. At first, one student calculated surface area as if the box had six faces. Another student drew the net and noticed that the display would have no lid. The team calculated 8×5 + 2(8×4) + 2(5×4) = 144 square centimeters for the base and four walls. They reminded the organizer that cutting and joining the material could require additional allowance for seams and waste. The organizer accepted their design explanation because the team showed a diagram, correct square units, and a clear statement of its assumptions.

Original fictional Khaemenes Academy reading, included for offline study.

1. Explain why the first student's six-face model was inappropriate.

2. Cite two details that strengthen the revised calculation.

3. Describe one practical reason material purchases may exceed the calculated area.
