The Fair Speaking Schedule

A fictional debate club had three minutes for brief contributions. One learner reasoned that half-minute turns meant only one and one-half contributions, because three divided by two equals one and a half. Another drew six half-minute sections and showed that 3 ÷ (1/2) = 6. The students checked by multiplying six turns by one-half minute, recovering three minutes. Later the group compared four minutes divided into half-minute turns with four minutes divided into quarter-minute turns. They found eight and sixteen contributions respectively. The class practiced rebutting a mathematical mistake with respectful reasoning, while recognizing that speaking time alone does not determine the quality of anyone's contribution.

Original fictional Academy reading; works offline.

1. State the mistaken claim and a respectful correction.

2. Cite the quotient and the multiplication check.

3. Explain what time allocation does not tell you about a debate.
