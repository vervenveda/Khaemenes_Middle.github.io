A Survey and the Meaning of Percent

A fictional youth council asked 40 students which improvement they preferred for a shared study room. Sixteen selected additional books, 12 selected more lamps, eight selected flexible seating, and four selected longer hours. One council member said that 16 students meant 16 percent supported books. Another student explained that percent means 'out of one hundred,' so 16 out of 40 is 40 percent. The group calculated the other shares as 30 percent, 20 percent, and 10 percent. Their totals added to 100 percent, which helped them check the arithmetic. They also noted that surveying only 40 volunteers could not establish the views of every student. The council published the sample size and question wording alongside the results so readers could judge the proposal fairly.

Original Khaemenes Academy practice passage. This fictional reading is included in the packet for offline learning.
