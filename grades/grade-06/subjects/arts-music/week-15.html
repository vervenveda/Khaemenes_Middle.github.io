Week 15 · Arts, Music & Media

Thermal Energy and Phase Change

How does energy explain changes in matter?

Monday: Visualize a thermal trend

Focus: Visualize a thermal trend.

Learn: An artist can represent a temperature series with a labeled line and abstract color choices. Color choices are artistic, not measured thermal evidence.

Practice and evidence: Sketch a fictional temperature series 0,2,4,4,6 °C and design a visual key.

Tuesday: Explain a plateau

Focus: Explain a plateau.

Learn: A horizontal line in a graph shows recorded values remaining equal. It can inspire a sustained musical note or repeated visual motif.

Practice and evidence: Make a four-measure graphic score representing rise, rise, hold, rise.

Wednesday: Choose accessible symbols

Focus: Choose accessible symbols.

Learn: Red and blue alone cannot convey temperatures for every reader. Numeric labels, textures and descriptions offer alternatives.

Practice and evidence: Revise a fictional thermal poster to include clear text values and an accessible legend.

Thursday: Defend an interpretation

Focus: Defend an interpretation.

Learn: Artistic metaphors should be distinguished from scientific conclusions. A warm color does not prove a substance is warmer.

Practice and evidence: Write a critique of your score explaining which features represent measured data and which are imaginative.

Friday: Thermal art portfolio

Focus: Thermal art portfolio.

Learn: A successful interpretation combines a valid graph, original composition, transparent legend and artist rationale.

Practice and evidence: Submit graph and score with a 100-word statement. Rubric: data 6, design 5, access 5, reflection 4.
