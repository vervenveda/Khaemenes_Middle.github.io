Week 18 · Arts, Music & Media

Waves, Sound, and Communication

How do waves carry energy and information?

Monday: Represent sound patterns

Focus: Represent sound patterns.

Learn: Sound can be modeled as periodic variation. A visual wave trace illustrates a pattern but its drawn height alone does not measure real loudness.

Practice and evidence: Draw two fictional wave traces and label their period and amplitude as schematic.

Tuesday: Count rhythm cycles

Focus: Count rhythm cycles.

Learn: An original rhythmic pattern containing 12 repeated beats over 3 seconds has frequency 4 beats per second; musical tempo and sound-wave frequency should not be confused.

Practice and evidence: Construct a rhythmic counting chart and explain the distinction.

Wednesday: Explore dynamics

Focus: Explore dynamics.

Learn: Musical dynamics describe expressive intensity; they are not equivalent to the measured pressure amplitude of a specific acoustic signal.

Practice and evidence: Create a four-bar original composition using a dynamic change and annotate the artistic intent.

Thursday: Design accessible performance

Focus: Design accessible performance.

Learn: A score can be explained through text, pattern descriptions and visual cues so it need not be heard to be understood.

Practice and evidence: Create an accessible static caption for a fictional rhythmic performance.

Friday: Make a sound-art portfolio

Focus: Make a sound-art portfolio.

Learn: A strong artistic response distinguishes scientific pattern, composition choices, and accessible presentation.

Practice and evidence: Submit wave sketches, short original score and artist statement. Rubric: modeling 5, creativity 6, access 5, reflection 4.
