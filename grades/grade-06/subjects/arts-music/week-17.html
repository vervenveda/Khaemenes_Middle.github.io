Week 17 · Arts, Music & Media

Midyear Portfolio and Assessment

How can I prove growth across every subject?

Monday: Select original creative work

Focus: Select original creative work.

Learn: A useful portfolio contains sketches, designs or notation plus dates and a clear statement about what each illustrates.

Practice and evidence: Identify two original works, including an early draft and a later version.

Tuesday: Analyze design choices

Focus: Analyze design choices.

Learn: Line, proportion, rhythm and accessible labeling can be compared without ranking personal expression as correct or incorrect.

Practice and evidence: Annotate three observable design choices in a prior work.

Wednesday: Revise an artifact

Focus: Revise an artifact.

Learn: Revision might improve readable captions or a model's numerical labels while keeping the work original.

Practice and evidence: Create a before-and-after sketch and record why two changes help.

Thursday: Prepare an artist statement

Focus: Prepare an artist statement.

Learn: An artist statement explains intention, process, creative choices and a limitation without inventing audience reactions.

Practice and evidence: Draft a 120-word reflection referring to specific evidence.

Friday: Present a midyear exhibit

Focus: Present a midyear exhibit.

Learn: A portfolio exhibit should identify creator, date, title, original work, revisions and accessible descriptions.

Practice and evidence: Submit two works, their annotations and statement. Rubric: artifacts 6, revision 5, interpretation 5, access 4.
