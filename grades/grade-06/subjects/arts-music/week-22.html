Week 22 · Arts, Music & Media

Earth Systems and Water

How do Earth systems interact and affect human life?

Monday: Visualize a water cycle

Focus: Visualize a water cycle.

Learn: A diagram may show evaporation, condensation, precipitation and collection with arrows. Artistic symbolism should remain distinct from physical measurement.

Practice and evidence: Sketch an original water-cycle poster with labeled arrows.

Tuesday: Represent land and water

Focus: Represent land and water.

Learn: Color and shape can distinguish hills, rivers, clouds and soil, but labels must carry essential meaning without depending on color.

Practice and evidence: Design an illustrated fictional watershed with a readable legend.

Wednesday: Compose patterns of movement

Focus: Compose patterns of movement.

Learn: An original rhythm can symbolize rising vapor and falling rain, but a musical pattern is a metaphor rather than recorded weather data.

Practice and evidence: Create a four-measure graphic score and write a scientific-versus-artistic note.

Thursday: Make inclusive environmental art

Focus: Make inclusive environmental art.

Learn: Alt-text-style descriptions, captions and clear line contrast help viewers understand an image without relying on tiny marks.

Practice and evidence: Revise a fictional landscape poster with descriptive text and larger labels.

Friday: Explain an artistic message

Focus: Explain an artistic message.

Learn: An artist statement should identify the intended environmental message and avoid claiming invented images are measured data.

Practice and evidence: Submit water-cycle art, a short graphic score and statement. Rubric: design 6, science 5, accessibility 5, explanation 4.
