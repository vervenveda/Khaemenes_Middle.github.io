Week 05 · Arts, Music & Media

Fraction Division and Procedural Writing

How can models explain division when the numbers are fractions?

Monday: Fractional rhythm values

Focus: Fractional rhythm values.

Learn: In common four-beat notation, a whole note commonly spans four quarter-note beats and a half note spans two. Fractions describe relationships to the whole note; meter and tempo also matter.

Practice and evidence: Create a four-beat rhythm using two half notes and a different four-beat rhythm using quarter notes and rests.

Tuesday: Group beats accurately

Focus: Group beats accurately.

Learn: Four quarter-note beats can be partitioned into halves and quarters. A fraction diagram helps organize events; notation conventions should be explained.

Practice and evidence: Draw a four-beat measure split into eighth-note pairs and describe how eight half-beat units fill it.

Wednesday: Repeated design patterns

Focus: Repeated design patterns.

Learn: A repeating visual motif can be partitioned into equal fractional portions, such as three of four panels using the same shape.

Practice and evidence: Create a four-panel original composition and identify the fractional share occupied by one color or motif.

Thursday: Write reproducible art directions

Focus: Write reproducible art directions.

Learn: A studio guide should specify materials, steps, measurements, and a way to check the result.

Practice and evidence: Write numbered instructions for making a four-panel visual rhythm poster and revise after a test.

Friday: Art and rhythm presentation

Focus: Art and rhythm presentation.

Learn: A portfolio should contain the original design, accurate fractional representation, a clear procedure, and a specific critique.

Practice and evidence: Present an original rhythm measure, fraction diagram, and procedure with reflection. Rubric: notation 6, fraction accuracy 5, process 5, revision 4.
