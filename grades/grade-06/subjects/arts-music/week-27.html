Week 27 · Arts, Music & Media

Trade Routes, Migration, and Exchange

How do movement and exchange transform societies?

Monday: Study patterns crossing regions

Focus: Study patterns crossing regions.

Learn: Styles and techniques can spread through contact, but similarity alone cannot prove a specific historical route.

Practice and evidence: Compare two reliably captioned object examples and record known versus inferred connections.

Tuesday: Trace artistic adaptation

Focus: Trace artistic adaptation.

Learn: Artists may transform imported ideas for local purposes and audiences.

Practice and evidence: Sketch a fictional adaptation sequence while labeling invented elements.

Wednesday: Explore rhythm and exchange

Focus: Explore rhythm and exchange.

Learn: Musical resemblance does not by itself document historical transmission.

Practice and evidence: Compare two teacher-provided rhythm patterns and avoid unsupported origin claims.

Thursday: Design culturally respectful displays

Focus: Design culturally respectful displays.

Learn: Exhibits should credit creators, show provenance and avoid copying sensitive motifs casually.

Practice and evidence: Revise an exhibit label for accuracy, legibility and credit.

Friday: Curate an exchange gallery

Focus: Curate an exchange gallery.

Learn: A gallery should distinguish authentic evidence from original student-inspired work.

Practice and evidence: Submit two sourced comparison cards and one original composition with ethical labels. Rubric: sourcing 6, craft 5, access 5, reflection 4.
