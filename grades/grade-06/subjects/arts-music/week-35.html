Week 35 · Arts, Music & Media

Capstone Studio and Oral Defense

How can I make my evidence clear enough to defend publicly?

Monday: Select a presentation format

Focus: Select a presentation format.

Learn: A capstone exhibit may use a poster, storyboard or original musical sketch with equivalent access options.

Practice and evidence: Choose one format and identify its audience and core message.

Tuesday: Attribute inspiration

Focus: Attribute inspiration.

Learn: Creators must distinguish their work from sources, samples and traditions.

Practice and evidence: Prepare a source card and an original-work label.

Wednesday: Design clear visuals

Focus: Design clear visuals.

Learn: Visual hierarchy, captions and contrast allow audiences to follow complex evidence.

Practice and evidence: Storyboard three panels with explanatory labels.

Thursday: Gather targeted critique

Focus: Gather targeted critique.

Learn: Useful critique asks about message, source integrity and accessibility.

Practice and evidence: Test a fictional presentation with three review questions and revise it.

Friday: Present an artist defense

Focus: Present an artist defense.

Learn: An artist statement should explain original choices and known limits without invented facts.

Practice and evidence: Submit a final mockup, creator statement and feedback log. Rubric: craft 6, evidence 6, access 4, reflection 4.
