Week 12 · Arts, Music & Media

Geometry: Area, Surface Area, and Design

How do measurements help designers plan efficient spaces?

Monday: Geometry in composition

Focus: Geometry in composition.

Learn: A geometric collage can combine rectangles and triangles. The area of a 4 cm by 3 cm rectangle is 12 cm².

Practice and evidence: Sketch an original collage plan and label the dimensions of two shapes.

Tuesday: Decompose a design

Focus: Decompose a design.

Learn: A shape assembled from nonoverlapping regions has total area equal to their individual areas. Overlap must be counted only once.

Practice and evidence: Design a nonoverlapping rectangle-plus-triangle arrangement and calculate both areas.

Wednesday: Fold an original net

Focus: Fold an original net.

Learn: A closed cube net consists of six equal squares, each with side s; its surface area is 6s².

Practice and evidence: Sketch a net with 2 cm squares and explain why its total face area is 24 cm².

Thursday: Evaluate visual proportions

Focus: Evaluate visual proportions.

Learn: Proportion concerns relative sizes. A design may be mathematically valid but still need contrast and readable labels.

Practice and evidence: Write a critique of a fictional geometric poster identifying a strength and an accessibility improvement.

Friday: Artist portfolio

Focus: Artist portfolio.

Learn: Strong geometric art combines original composition, correct square units, craftsmanship and an intentional artistic rationale.

Practice and evidence: Submit a measured collage and cube net with a 100-word artist statement. Rubric: area 6, composition 5, access 5, revision 4.
