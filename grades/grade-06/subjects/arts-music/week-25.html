Week 25 · Arts, Music & Media

Belief Systems, Law, and Culture

How do beliefs and laws shape community life?

Monday: Analyze cultural symbols

Focus: Analyze cultural symbols.

Learn: A civic or religious symbol may have several meanings depending on creator, date, audience and location. Avoid attributing meanings without evidence.

Practice and evidence: Sketch a fictional civic emblem and explain which interpretations are invented.

Tuesday: Read artifacts in context

Focus: Read artifacts in context.

Learn: An image, inscription or musical tradition needs provenance before historical interpretation; creative reconstructions are not original historical objects.

Practice and evidence: Create a source-analysis card for an invented ceremonial object.

Wednesday: Compare expressive choices

Focus: Compare expressive choices.

Learn: Pattern, repetition and materials can express shared identity in art, but no single motif defines a whole community.

Practice and evidence: Create two alternative fictional design motifs with explanations of their intended meanings.

Thursday: Make culturally respectful art

Focus: Make culturally respectful art.

Learn: Original work should not present borrowed sacred imagery as generic decoration or invented details as authentic.

Practice and evidence: Revise an original civic poster to include a context note and accessible caption.

Friday: Present a culture-and-law exhibit

Focus: Present a culture-and-law exhibit.

Learn: An artist statement should explain intention, evidence, respectful framing and source limitations.

Practice and evidence: Submit original emblem and critique. Rubric: design 6, context 5, respect 5, access 4.
