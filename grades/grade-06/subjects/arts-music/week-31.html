Week 31 · Arts, Music & Media

Arts, Media, and Message Design

How do artists and media makers shape meaning?

Monday: Identify artistic intention

Focus: Identify artistic intention.

Learn: The maker's stated purpose and the audience's interpretation may differ.

Practice and evidence: Analyze an original fictional poster and distinguish observable choices from claimed intentions.

Tuesday: Explore composition

Focus: Explore composition.

Learn: Scale, contrast, rhythm and placement guide attention but do not automatically establish truth.

Practice and evidence: Produce two thumbnail compositions for one fictional community message.

Wednesday: Compare sound and imagery

Focus: Compare sound and imagery.

Learn: Audio and visual elements may reinforce or challenge each other's mood.

Practice and evidence: Storyboard a thirty-second silent or softly narrated message with accessible alternatives.

Thursday: Test ethical presentation

Focus: Test ethical presentation.

Learn: Artwork inspired by a tradition needs attribution, context and respect for culturally sensitive symbols.

Practice and evidence: Peer-review captions and revise unsupported cultural claims.

Friday: Create a media exhibit

Focus: Create a media exhibit.

Learn: A finished work should be original, accessible and transparent about evidence.

Practice and evidence: Submit a poster or storyboard, source note and artist statement. Rubric: craft 6, meaning 5, accessibility 5, ethics 4.
