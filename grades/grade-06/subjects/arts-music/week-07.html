Week 07 · Arts, Music & Media

Coordinate Planes and Geography

How can coordinates help us describe place, movement, and patterns?

Monday: Build a coordinate composition

Focus: Build a coordinate composition.

Learn: An art grid can place shapes using ordered pairs. Negative and positive coordinates describe location rather than quality or color.

Practice and evidence: Draw a four-quadrant canvas and place three different shapes at (−3,2), (1,4), and (2,−2).

Tuesday: Scale a visual pattern

Focus: Scale a visual pattern.

Learn: If one grid square represents 2 cm, a horizontal span of four squares represents 8 cm. Scale should be stated in the legend.

Practice and evidence: Design a symmetric motif and calculate two distances using an explicit scale.

Wednesday: Perspective and placement

Focus: Perspective and placement.

Learn: Foreground and background are visual design decisions, not the same as geographic north and south. A good composition uses deliberate size and position.

Practice and evidence: Draw the same fictional street from overhead and eye level, and explain three differences in perspective.

Thursday: Design an accessible map poster

Focus: Design an accessible map poster.

Learn: Labels should remain readable without relying exclusively on colors. Coordinates and icons provide a second way to locate features.

Practice and evidence: Create an original poster-map with text labels, legible icons, a north arrow and a caption describing spatial choices.

Friday: Exhibition and critique

Focus: Exhibition and critique.

Learn: A useful artist statement explains coordinates, scale, symbols and how composition guides the audience.

Practice and evidence: Submit your coordinate artwork and a 100-word artist statement with one revision. Rubric: placement 6, scale 5, readable design 5, reflection 4.
