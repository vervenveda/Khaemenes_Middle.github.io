Week 34 · Arts, Music & Media

A++ Big Question Capstone Research

What serious question can I investigate with evidence, calculation, and design?

Monday: Set a creative inquiry

Focus: Set a creative inquiry.

Learn: Artistic capstones should define an audience, message and research basis.

Practice and evidence: Write an original design question for a fictional community exhibit.

Tuesday: Collect ethically sourced references

Focus: Collect ethically sourced references.

Learn: A reference board must credit artists and distinguish inspiration from appropriation.

Practice and evidence: Make two source cards and mark one uncertain attribution.

Wednesday: Build a prototype

Focus: Build a prototype.

Learn: Composition, form and rhythm can communicate the idea without being presented as historical fact.

Practice and evidence: Sketch an accessible poster, storyboard or musical plan.

Thursday: Invite targeted critique

Focus: Invite targeted critique.

Learn: Feedback should focus on accessibility, evidence and intended message rather than identity judgments.

Practice and evidence: Apply a three-question peer critique rubric.

Friday: Present an artist statement

Focus: Present an artist statement.

Learn: The artifact should identify original work, sources and revisions.

Practice and evidence: Submit final concept, source log and artist statement. Rubric: craft 6, evidence 6, access 4, reflection 4.
