Week 03 · Arts, Music & Media

Rates, Unit Rates, and Informational Text

How can rates and text structures explain change?

Monday: Tempo as a rate

Focus: Tempo as a rate.

Learn: Tempo describes beats per minute (BPM). At 60 BPM there is about one beat each second, while 120 BPM yields about two beats each second.

Practice and evidence: Notate four beats at 60 BPM and 120 BPM using a paper timeline; describe how long each measure lasts.

Tuesday: Calculate musical timing

Focus: Calculate musical timing.

Learn: At 80 BPM, four beats take 4×60÷80 = 3 seconds. At 120 BPM four beats take 2 seconds. A beat's length changes with tempo, not with pitch.

Practice and evidence: Calculate four-beat durations at 60, 90, and 120 BPM. Show division and units.

Wednesday: Create a rhythmic pattern

Focus: Create a rhythmic pattern.

Learn: Rhythm uses notes and rests while tempo sets the pulse. Different rhythms can share an identical tempo.

Practice and evidence: Design two original eight-beat patterns using X for a clap and – for a rest. Label a common BPM and explain the variation.

Thursday: Visualize rate and pattern

Focus: Visualize rate and pattern.

Learn: A repeated motif can form a visual representation of beat groups. A clear caption communicates the units, sequence, and purpose.

Practice and evidence: Draw an eight-beat pictogram with a time axis and a caption giving BPM, seconds per beat, and the motif used.

Friday: Artist statement and revision

Focus: Artist statement and revision.

Learn: A portfolio should show original work and explain how specific choices affect an audience.

Practice and evidence: Create a one-page rhythm study with two tempos, correct calculations, a visual pattern, and a revised caption. Rubric 20: rate math 6, notation 5, clarity 5, revision 4.
