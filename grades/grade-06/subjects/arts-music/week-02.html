Week 02 · Arts, Music & Media

Evidence, Annotation, and Ratios

How do evidence and comparison help us understand complex ideas?

Monday: Patterns and steady beat

Focus: Patterns and steady beat.

Learn: A beat is a regular pulse; rhythm is an arrangement of sounds or silences against that pulse. A melody is a sequence of pitches.

Practice and evidence: Create four beats with claps and rests, notate using X and – signs, and perform or describe two variations.

Tuesday: Pitch and contour

Focus: Pitch and contour.

Learn: Melodic contour describes whether pitches rise, fall, or repeat. A drawn line can represent these changes without requiring an instrument.

Practice and evidence: Draw an original five-note contour, label three directions, and explain how your sketch represents sound.

Wednesday: Ratios in visual patterns

Focus: Ratios in visual patterns.

Learn: A repeating visual pattern may compare colors or shapes using ratios: three circles for every two squares means 3:2.

Practice and evidence: Create a repeated shape composition with at least 15 shapes. Count each type and write the ratio with an explanation.

Thursday: Critique and revision

Focus: Critique and revision.

Learn: An evidence-based critique distinguishes what is visible or audible from feelings or interpretation.

Practice and evidence: Review your composition and rhythm pattern. Record three observations, one interpretation, and an intentional revision.

Friday: Music and design portfolio

Focus: Music and design portfolio.

Learn: Original work demonstrates rhythm, melodic contour, pattern, and visual decisions.

Practice and evidence: Submit a rhythm notation, a contour drawing, and a labeled visual pattern. Rubric: accuracy 6, creativity 5, explanation 5, revision 4.
