Week 28 · Arts, Music & Media

World Languages and Cultural Communication

How does language carry culture and connection?

Monday: Explore scripts as visual forms

Focus: Explore scripts as visual forms.

Learn: Writing systems combine form and function; sacred or culturally important characters need informed treatment.

Practice and evidence: Compare two sourced writing-system samples and identify directionality and readability.

Tuesday: Study rhythm and spoken language

Focus: Study rhythm and spoken language.

Learn: Speech rhythms and musical rhythms may be compared, but neither determines a culture's identity.

Practice and evidence: Create a short original spoken-word rhythm and mark pauses and stressed beats.

Wednesday: Design multilingual signage

Focus: Design multilingual signage.

Learn: Good communication uses readable hierarchy, contrast and accurate translations.

Practice and evidence: Sketch a two-language welcome sign using verified wording and large legible labels.

Thursday: Critique respectfully

Focus: Critique respectfully.

Learn: Design evaluation should address usability, attribution and translation quality rather than stereotypes.

Practice and evidence: Test the sign for legibility and ask for feedback using an invented scenario.

Friday: Publish an inclusive artwork

Focus: Publish an inclusive artwork.

Learn: A final presentation separates original artistic choices from sourced script information.

Practice and evidence: Submit the designed sign, source notes and revision explanation. Rubric: communication 6, accuracy 6, design 4, reflection 4.
