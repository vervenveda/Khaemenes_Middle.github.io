Week 33 · Arts, Music & Media

Technology, Data, and Ethical Creation

How can tools strengthen evidence without replacing judgment?

Monday: Compare digital and handmade composition

Focus: Compare digital and handmade composition.

Learn: Digital tools expand options but do not replace artistic decisions and source accountability.

Practice and evidence: Compare original paper and digital layout sketches.

Tuesday: Study data visualization ethics

Focus: Study data visualization ethics.

Learn: Color, scale and omission can influence impressions without altering raw data.

Practice and evidence: Redesign a fictional misleading infographic with labeled axes.

Wednesday: Track media provenance

Focus: Track media provenance.

Learn: Creators should credit sampled and sourced media and distinguish student inventions.

Practice and evidence: Create a source and permissions log for three invented media assets.

Thursday: Design accessible presentation

Focus: Design accessible presentation.

Learn: Alternative text, optional audio and readable contrast support broader audiences.

Practice and evidence: Storyboard a no-autoplay media exhibit with descriptive captions.

Friday: Publish an ethical artwork

Focus: Publish an ethical artwork.

Learn: An artist statement should explain choices and constraints without fabricated claims.

Practice and evidence: Submit infographic or storyboard, source notes and explanation. Rubric: design 6, integrity 6, access 4, reflection 4.
