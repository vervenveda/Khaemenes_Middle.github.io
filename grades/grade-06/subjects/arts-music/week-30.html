Week 30 · Arts, Music & Media

Argument, Debate, and Public Speaking

How can disagreement become disciplined learning?

Monday: Identify persuasive design

Focus: Identify persuasive design.

Learn: Color, scale, composition and repetition influence attention but do not establish truth.

Practice and evidence: Annotate a fictional public poster, distinguishing visible choices from assumed impact.

Tuesday: Examine media credibility

Focus: Examine media credibility.

Learn: A caption, image crop or soundtrack may influence interpretation and conceal missing context.

Practice and evidence: Compare two fictional captions for the same described photograph.

Wednesday: Create an ethical visual argument

Focus: Create an ethical visual argument.

Learn: Effective design uses a clear claim without inventing evidence or cultural symbols.

Practice and evidence: Sketch a sourced poster supporting a fictional community improvement.

Thursday: Review for audience access

Focus: Review for audience access.

Learn: Legible type, sufficient contrast and a text alternative make persuasion more inclusive.

Practice and evidence: Peer-review the poster and improve its accessible description.

Friday: Exhibit a transparent message

Focus: Exhibit a transparent message.

Learn: A presentation should label artwork as original and evidence as sourced.

Practice and evidence: Submit a poster and a one-minute artist statement. Rubric: evidence 6, clarity 5, access 5, reflection 4.
