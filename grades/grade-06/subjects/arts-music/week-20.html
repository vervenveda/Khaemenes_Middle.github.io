Week 20 · Arts, Music & Media

Cells, Systems, and Scientific Models

How do small structures support living systems?

Monday: Explore organic shapes

Focus: Explore organic shapes.

Learn: Cell-inspired art can borrow rounded membranes or repeating internal forms; such imagery remains an artistic interpretation rather than microscopy.

Practice and evidence: Create three original abstract shape sketches inspired by fictional cell diagrams.

Tuesday: Distinguish image from model

Focus: Distinguish image from model.

Learn: A scientific cell diagram simplifies relative scale and placement. Artistic color choices need not indicate actual cell colors.

Practice and evidence: Annotate a model-inspired collage identifying two artistic liberties.

Wednesday: Design a structure-function poster

Focus: Design a structure-function poster.

Learn: A scientifically labeled poster can connect a membrane to selective exchange and ribosomes to protein synthesis.

Practice and evidence: Plan an accessible two-part visual poster with accurate captions.

Thursday: Revise for access

Focus: Revise for access.

Learn: Distinct outlines, readable labels and text descriptions make a biology-themed art piece understandable without color alone.

Practice and evidence: Create an original poster revision with alt-text and visual hierarchy.

Friday: Present a cell-art exhibit

Focus: Present a cell-art exhibit.

Learn: An artist statement describes creative decisions and identifies where the work departs from science.

Practice and evidence: Submit collage, accurate reference labels, and reflection. Rubric: originality 6, science 5, access 5, explanation 4.
