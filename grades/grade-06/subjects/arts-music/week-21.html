Week 21 · Arts, Music & Media

Ecosystems and Energy Flow

How do energy and matter move through ecosystems?

Monday: Observe ecosystem patterns

Focus: Observe ecosystem patterns.

Learn: Art can depict producers, consumers and decomposers with repeating shapes; artistic symbols are not themselves measured field data.

Practice and evidence: Sketch an original fictional pond ecosystem mural with a visual key.

Tuesday: Represent energy flow

Focus: Represent energy flow.

Learn: A food-web mural should depict arrows from eaten organisms toward consumers, not treat energy as infinitely recycled.

Practice and evidence: Draw an accurately labeled grass-rabbit-fox chain as part of a collage.

Wednesday: Explore rhythm and cycles

Focus: Explore rhythm and cycles.

Learn: Repeated rhythmic motifs can represent ecological cycles artistically, but energy flow and matter cycling are different scientific ideas.

Practice and evidence: Draft a short four-measure graphic rhythm score and caption its intended metaphor.

Thursday: Design accessible ecological art

Focus: Design accessible ecological art.

Learn: Labels, contrast, alt-text-style descriptions and an uncluttered legend support multiple ways of experiencing art.

Practice and evidence: Revise a fictional habitat mural for readable symbols and descriptions.

Friday: Create an ecosystem art portfolio

Focus: Create an ecosystem art portfolio.

Learn: A complete artistic record combines original composition, accurate diagrams and a clear distinction between science and metaphor.

Practice and evidence: Submit a mural sketch, energy-flow captions and artist statement. Rubric: creativity 6, science 5, access 5, reflection 4.
