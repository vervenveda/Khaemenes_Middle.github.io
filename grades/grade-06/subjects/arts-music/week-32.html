Week 32 · Arts, Music & Media

Health, Identity, and Responsible Choices

How do healthy routines support learning and identity?

Monday: Analyze wellness messages

Focus: Analyze wellness messages.

Learn: Visual and audio design choices can influence feelings but do not prove medical effects.

Practice and evidence: Compare fictional school wellness posters, marking claims and emotional cues.

Tuesday: Use inclusive visual language

Focus: Use inclusive visual language.

Learn: Icons and captions need enough contrast and cannot depend on one culture's symbolism.

Practice and evidence: Draft a low-pressure poster offering varied rest and movement options.

Wednesday: Plan a calming composition

Focus: Plan a calming composition.

Learn: An artwork can invite reflection without claiming to treat anxiety or illness.

Practice and evidence: Create a silent storyboard or optional gentle rhythm pattern with captions.

Thursday: Peer-review attribution and access

Focus: Peer-review attribution and access.

Learn: Creators must label original artwork, check quotations and provide alternatives to sound.

Practice and evidence: Revise the composition using accessibility feedback.

Friday: Present responsible art

Focus: Present responsible art.

Learn: A school campaign should be original, evidence-aware and nonstigmatizing.

Practice and evidence: Submit a poster or storyboard with artist statement and accessible description. Rubric: craft 6, clarity 5, access 5, ethics 4.
