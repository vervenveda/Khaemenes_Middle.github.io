Week 36 · Arts, Music & Media

Final Review and Grade 7 Readiness

How am I ready for seventh grade?

Monday: Revisit source and interpretation

Focus: Revisit source and interpretation.

Learn: A work's observable features differ from unsupported claims about its maker's intent.

Practice and evidence: Compare two sourced artwork descriptions and mark facts versus interpretation.

Tuesday: Apply design principles

Focus: Apply design principles.

Learn: Contrast, composition, rhythm and legibility help communicate meaning.

Practice and evidence: Draft an accessible visual or musical storyboard with descriptive captions.

Wednesday: Explain ethical attribution

Focus: Explain ethical attribution.

Learn: Student-created and sourced cultural materials should be clearly distinguished.

Practice and evidence: Prepare creator and source labels for a small showcase.

Thursday: Critique constructively

Focus: Critique constructively.

Learn: Useful feedback tests message, access and integrity, not a learner's worth.

Practice and evidence: Revise a fictional exhibition after peer-review notes.

Friday: Curate a final showcase

Focus: Curate a final showcase.

Learn: Artistic readiness includes original expression, sourcing and reflection.

Practice and evidence: Submit one original design, an artist statement and source card. Rubric: craft 6, ethics 6, access 4, reflection 4.
