Week 11 · Arts, Music & Media

Statistics: Questions, Data, and Distribution

How can data answer questions while still requiring judgment?

Monday: Collect artistic data

Focus: Collect artistic data.

Learn: An artist can gather data about a fictional rhythm performance. Recorded beat counts 4,4,6,6,8 form a distribution rather than a single fixed value.

Practice and evidence: Construct a dot plot of the five beat counts and name the units.

Tuesday: Find center and spread

Focus: Find center and spread.

Learn: For 4,4,6,6,8 the ordered median is 6 and the range is 4. A measure of center alone does not describe variation.

Practice and evidence: Compute median and range, then describe what each misses.

Wednesday: Study tempo responsibly

Focus: Study tempo responsibly.

Learn: Beats per minute can be counted or simulated, but artistic quality cannot be inferred from tempo alone.

Practice and evidence: Create a fictional tempo table with 60,72,72,84 and 96 bpm and identify its mode.

Thursday: Design an understandable chart

Focus: Design an understandable chart.

Learn: A readable chart includes title, axis scale, units and count. Color alone must not determine what a symbol means.

Practice and evidence: Draft a poster comparing two simulated rhythm distributions using labels and readable icons.

Friday: Music-data portfolio

Focus: Music-data portfolio.

Learn: An effective explanation connects a question, accurate data graphic, and artistic interpretation without overstating evidence.

Practice and evidence: Submit a dot plot, median/range calculations and 100-word artist statement. Rubric: statistics 6, graphics 5, reflection 5, access 4.
