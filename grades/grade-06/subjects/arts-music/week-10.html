Week 10 · Arts, Music & Media

Inequalities and Decision Rules

How do constraints shape solutions and decisions?

Monday: Create within a limit

Focus: Create within a limit.

Learn: Constraints can spark creative choices. A fictional poster must use no more than four shapes: s≤4 allows four, but not five.

Practice and evidence: Draw two different original compositions meeting s≤4 and count each shape.

Tuesday: Beat boundaries

Focus: Beat boundaries.

Learn: A fictional rhythm exercise allows at most eight beats per phrase, b≤8. An exactly eight-beat pattern is accepted.

Practice and evidence: Create three short rhythms of six, eight and nine beats, and label which meet the invented practice rule.

Wednesday: Contrast criteria

Focus: Contrast criteria.

Learn: An accessible poster needs readable labels and symbols, not only a numerical color limit. Numeric and descriptive criteria work together.

Practice and evidence: Revise a fictional poster with tiny text to satisfy s≤4 and clear readability expectations.

Thursday: Defend choices

Focus: Defend choices.

Learn: A critique can test whether a design meets its stated limits and why the chosen arrangement is effective.

Practice and evidence: Make a rubric listing the shape cap, clear focal point, text legibility and evidence of revision.

Friday: Constraint studio

Focus: Constraint studio.

Learn: Artistic independence remains possible inside shared rules. Present both the calculated compliance and an original artistic rationale.

Practice and evidence: Submit two original designs, an eight-beat score and artist statement. Rubric: constraints 6, composition 5, access 5, revision 4.
