Week 10 · Health, PE & SEL

Inequalities and Decision Rules

How do constraints shape solutions and decisions?

Monday: Personal boundaries

Focus: Personal boundaries.

Learn: A boundary can be expressed respectfully without revealing private information. 'I prefer the seated option' is enough for a fictional activity choice.

Practice and evidence: Write a two-line self-advocacy dialogue and a supportive response that respects consent.

Tuesday: Optional time limits

Focus: Optional time limits.

Learn: If a fictional activity slot lasts at most 12 minutes, t≤12 includes t=12. A learner may choose a shorter duration or abstain.

Practice and evidence: Plot t≤12 on a number line and propose three optional activity durations that fit.

Wednesday: Decision rules and safety

Focus: Decision rules and safety.

Learn: Generic numbers are not universal health prescriptions. A responsible plan checks permission, environment, discomfort and accessible alternatives.

Practice and evidence: Draft an if-then decision guide that offers a seated task, a rest option and stopping when uncomfortable.

Thursday: Compare choices

Focus: Compare choices.

Learn: Rules can prioritize safety while preserving agency. A plan requiring everyone to complete the same movement ignores differences in abilities and preferences.

Practice and evidence: Rewrite an inflexible fictional group routine to allow equivalent learning evidence without physical performance.

Friday: Boundary reflection

Focus: Boundary reflection.

Learn: Wellbeing evidence should evaluate sound choices and inclusion, not measure students' bodies or infer medical suitability.

Practice and evidence: Submit a fictional t≤12 plan, a respectful script and an accessible decision flow. Rubric: safety 6, math 5, inclusion 5, reflection 4.
