Week 03 · Health, PE & SEL

Rates, Unit Rates, and Informational Text

How can rates and text structures explain change?

Monday: Activity pacing

Focus: Activity pacing.

Learn: Pacing means choosing a sustainable effort level. Time and movement counts can describe activities, but different learners need different alternatives and safe rest.

Practice and evidence: Design a flexible six-minute fictional activity plan with two rest intervals and a seated option. Explain safe adjustments.

Tuesday: Reading activity-rate data

Focus: Reading activity-rate data.

Learn: A rate such as steps per minute describes observed motion; it does not measure someone's worth or health. Fictional records of 120 steps over 3 minutes yield 40 steps/minute.

Practice and evidence: Calculate two fictional rates (120 steps/3 min and 150 steps/5 min), then explain why comparisons require context.

Wednesday: Pulse and privacy

Focus: Pulse and privacy.

Learn: Heart rate is typically expressed in beats per minute, but personal data should be private and not required for portfolio grades. Anyone with symptoms or health concerns should use appropriate adult or professional guidance.

Practice and evidence: Use a simulated table of 70 and 90 beats/minute to practice reading units. Do not collect or disclose classmates' measurements.

Thursday: Inclusive wellness decisions

Focus: Inclusive wellness decisions.

Learn: A responsible movement plan uses safe options and recognizes fatigue, rest, hydration, and access needs.

Practice and evidence: Compare two fictional pacing plans for a mixed-ability group and recommend modifications without ranking people's bodies.

Friday: Wellness reflection

Focus: Wellness reflection.

Learn: Data are helpful only when interpreted with safety, context, and respect.

Practice and evidence: Submit an accessible activity plan, two fictional rate calculations, and a reflection on limitations. Rubric 20: safe planning 6, computation 5, inclusion 5, evidence 4.
