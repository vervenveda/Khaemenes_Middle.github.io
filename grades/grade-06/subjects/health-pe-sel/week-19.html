Week 19 · Health, PE & SEL

Light, Vision, and Media Interpretation

How does light shape what we see and how we interpret media?

Monday: Respect differences in perception

Focus: Respect differences in perception.

Learn: Visual activities should offer written descriptions and flexible participation rather than require disclosure of vision conditions.

Practice and evidence: Rewrite a hypothetical exercise to include verbal and tactile-free paper options.

Tuesday: Read visual safety symbols

Focus: Read visual safety symbols.

Learn: A label should combine wording and a symbol when practical, and avoid relying solely on color.

Practice and evidence: Make a fictional safety sign using an icon and large readable text.

Wednesday: Use safe observation

Focus: Use safe observation.

Learn: Never look toward the Sun or into lasers to investigate vision. Paper ray diagrams are sufficient for the lesson.

Practice and evidence: Create a safe paper investigation about a mirror with no bright-light exposure.

Thursday: Support inclusive communication

Focus: Support inclusive communication.

Learn: A diagram should have a text description, a clear reading order and a way to ask for clarification.

Practice and evidence: Create an accessible explanation of a fictional instruction poster.

Friday: Reflect on design care

Focus: Reflect on design care.

Learn: Learning evidence should focus on safe decisions and access, not personal vision measurements.

Practice and evidence: Submit a safety poster and paper model with reflection. Rubric: safety 6, inclusion 6, clarity 4, reflection 4.
