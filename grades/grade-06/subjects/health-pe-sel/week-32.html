Week 32 · Health, PE & SEL

Health, Identity, and Responsible Choices

How do healthy routines support learning and identity?

Monday: Understand balanced routines

Focus: Understand balanced routines.

Learn: Sleep, food, movement and connection support daily well-being, while individual needs vary.

Practice and evidence: Create a fictional student routine that includes flexible options and rest.

Tuesday: Practice emotional self-awareness

Focus: Practice emotional self-awareness.

Learn: Recognizing an emotion and selecting a constructive response differs from judging the emotion.

Practice and evidence: Role-play a fictional busy-school-day scenario and write two coping choices.

Wednesday: Offer accessible movement

Focus: Offer accessible movement.

Learn: Movement activities should allow different mobility levels and avoid competitive health disclosure.

Practice and evidence: Design a safe five-minute seated or standing coordination circuit with breaks.

Thursday: Build digital boundaries

Focus: Build digital boundaries.

Learn: Screen use can be balanced with offline activities without a rigid rule for everyone.

Practice and evidence: Create a personal-choice checklist for an invented device-use situation.

Friday: Make a self-advocacy plan

Focus: Make a self-advocacy plan.

Learn: Asking for support is a communication skill; no student must reveal private conditions.

Practice and evidence: Submit a fictional routine plan with support requests and accommodations. Rubric: safety 6, inclusion 6, choices 4, reflection 4.
