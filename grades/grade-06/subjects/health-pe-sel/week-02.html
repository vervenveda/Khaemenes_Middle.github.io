Week 02 · Health, PE & SEL

Evidence, Annotation, and Ratios

How do evidence and comparison help us understand complex ideas?

Monday: Safe combinations of movement

Focus: Safe combinations of movement.

Learn: A movement sequence may include warm-up, balance, coordination, and cool-down. Adapt intensity and posture to individual needs.

Practice and evidence: Design a six-minute sequence with a seated or low-impact option, and explain safety considerations.

Tuesday: Coordination and rhythm

Focus: Coordination and rhythm.

Learn: Combining movement with a steady beat can support coordination. Activities should be optional and adaptable, not judged by body shape.

Practice and evidence: Write a four-count movement pattern and an accessible hand-motion variant. Describe how to adjust pace.

Wednesday: Noticing exertion

Focus: Noticing exertion.

Learn: Perceived effort varies by person. A talk test can help describe moderate effort, but discomfort, pain, or dizziness are reasons to stop.

Practice and evidence: Create a private or fictional movement log with activity, duration, effort description, and safe modification.

Thursday: Cooperation and consent

Focus: Cooperation and consent.

Learn: Good group activities include shared rules, role rotation, consent, and respectful feedback.

Practice and evidence: Design a partner or solo coordination challenge with three explicit inclusion and safety rules.

Friday: Flexible activity portfolio

Focus: Flexible activity portfolio.

Learn: Personal progress evidence concerns planning, skill, and reflection rather than speed or physical traits.

Practice and evidence: Submit a safe movement sequence, inclusion adjustments, and a reflection. Rubric: safety 6, inclusion 5, explanation 5, revision 4.
