Week 14 · Health, PE & SEL

Matter: Particles, Properties, and Models

How can invisible particle models explain visible properties?

Monday: Observe without assumptions

Focus: Observe without assumptions.

Learn: Wellbeing activities can explore objects' textures and measured properties without collecting private health or bodily data.

Practice and evidence: Prepare a fictional classroom material-observation table with non-sensitive properties.

Tuesday: Plan safe handling

Focus: Plan safe handling.

Learn: A material may have sharp edges, dust or breakage risks. A learning plan should use harmless representations and provide seated alternatives.

Practice and evidence: Rewrite a fictional unsafe sorting activity into a paper-based comparison.

Wednesday: Understand measured density

Focus: Understand measured density.

Learn: Density is mass per volume, not an instruction about what a learner can lift. For simulated mass 10 g and volume 5 cm³, density is 2 g/cm³.

Practice and evidence: Complete the calculation and explain why it does not determine safe handling for a person.

Thursday: Make responsible choices

Focus: Make responsible choices.

Learn: An inclusive design considers clear labels, non-contact alternatives, safe materials and permission to opt out.

Practice and evidence: Create a choose-your-option decision flow for an original materials lesson.

Friday: Safety reflection

Focus: Safety reflection.

Learn: Evidence can show mathematical reasoning and responsible choices without physical performance or sensitive personal records.

Practice and evidence: Submit a fictional property table, density example and accessible procedure. Rubric: calculation 5, safety 6, accessibility 5, explanation 4.
