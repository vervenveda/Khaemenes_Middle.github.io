Week 12 · Health, PE & SEL

Geometry: Area, Surface Area, and Design

How do measurements help designers plan efficient spaces?

Monday: Accessible space planning

Focus: Accessible space planning.

Learn: A floor plan can reserve clear routes for movement and rest. Classroom drawings cannot establish real accessibility-code compliance.

Practice and evidence: Sketch a fictional 6 m by 4 m movement room and calculate its area of 24 m².

Tuesday: Understand spatial needs

Focus: Understand spatial needs.

Learn: A 2 m by 4 m fictional quiet zone covers 8 m². Remaining floor area is 16 m² only if the zones do not overlap.

Practice and evidence: Draw two clearly labeled nonoverlapping areas and check the subtraction.

Wednesday: Choose inclusive layouts

Focus: Choose inclusive layouts.

Learn: A route that looks wide on paper may still have obstacles in practice. Safe planning needs consent, room checks and seated alternatives.

Practice and evidence: Mark clear pathways, a seated option and two potential obstacles on your fictional plan.

Thursday: Reflect on boundaries

Focus: Reflect on boundaries.

Learn: A person's participation should not be determined by measured space alone; comfort, accessibility, local conditions and personal choice matter.

Practice and evidence: Revise a fictional group plan to offer equivalent nonmovement learning evidence.

Friday: Explain an inclusive design

Focus: Explain an inclusive design.

Learn: Evidence should demonstrate geometric reasoning and respect for learner autonomy rather than physical performance.

Practice and evidence: Submit a scale sketch, area calculations and design reflection. Rubric: math 6, layout 5, safety 5, explanation 4.
