Week 35 · Health, PE & SEL

Capstone Studio and Oral Defense

How can I make my evidence clear enough to defend publicly?

Monday: Assign inclusive roles

Focus: Assign inclusive roles.

Learn: Capstone teamwork should offer genuine choice without requiring private disclosures.

Practice and evidence: Draft roles for presenter, writer, checker and accessibility reviewer.

Tuesday: Prepare for questions

Focus: Prepare for questions.

Learn: A pause, restatement and respectful answer can make a defense more constructive.

Practice and evidence: Rehearse a fictional challenging question without personal attacks.

Wednesday: Design accessible participation

Focus: Design accessible participation.

Learn: Written, spoken and other accessible responses can demonstrate the same reasoning.

Practice and evidence: Plan participation alternatives and optional breaks.

Thursday: Evaluate constructive feedback

Focus: Evaluate constructive feedback.

Learn: Critique should focus on project evidence, not the worth or identity of the learner.

Practice and evidence: Write two helpful and two unhelpful fictional feedback statements, then revise them.

Friday: Reflect on self-advocacy

Focus: Reflect on self-advocacy.

Learn: A successful defense includes respectful requests for tools, help or time.

Practice and evidence: Submit a presentation-access plan and reflective guide. Rubric: inclusion 6, respect 6, planning 4, reflection 4.
