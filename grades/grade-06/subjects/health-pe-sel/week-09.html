Week 09 · Health, PE & SEL

Equations and Balanced Arguments

How do balance and evidence make arguments stronger?

Monday: Weigh choices

Focus: Weigh choices.

Learn: Balanced decision making examines benefits, costs, safety and alternatives. A fictional group may choose either quiet stretching or an optional coordination game; access differs by participant.

Practice and evidence: Draw a two-column comparison of the options with safety and accessibility criteria.

Tuesday: Self-advocacy

Focus: Self-advocacy.

Learn: A person can respectfully state a need or boundary without disclosing private health information. A reasoned request offers an accessible alternative.

Practice and evidence: Write a short fictional script requesting a seated role in a cooperative game, and a supportive response.

Wednesday: Model time fairly

Focus: Model time fairly.

Learn: An equality can express a fixed schedule: x+5=15 means ten minutes remain for an optional activity after five minutes of setup.

Practice and evidence: Solve and check x+5=15; design two safe uses of the remaining time.

Thursday: Consider consequences

Focus: Consider consequences.

Learn: A preferred activity can have unintended consequences for people with different access needs. Consent and room for rest are essential.

Practice and evidence: Compare two fictional group activity plans and revise the one lacking accessible choices.

Friday: Decision reflection

Focus: Decision reflection.

Learn: A strong decision record supports safe options with reasons and recognizes uncertainty and personal boundaries.

Practice and evidence: Submit an inclusive 15-minute plan with the equation, balanced decision table and scripted accommodation. Rubric 20: safety 6, inclusivity 6, math 4, reasoning 4.
