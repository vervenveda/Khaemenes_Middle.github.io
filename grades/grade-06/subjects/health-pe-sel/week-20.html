Week 20 · Health, PE & SEL

Cells, Systems, and Scientific Models

How do small structures support living systems?

Monday: Respect privacy

Focus: Respect privacy.

Learn: Cells and body systems can be taught using standard diagrams without collecting anyone's medical history or specimens.

Practice and evidence: Design a privacy-respecting fictional classroom cell activity.

Tuesday: Use safe models

Focus: Use safe models.

Learn: Paper models avoid biological samples, unknown fluids and unsafe preparation. A model needs a label showing what it represents.

Practice and evidence: Draft a safety checklist for a paper-based cell lesson.

Wednesday: Connect systems thoughtfully

Focus: Connect systems thoughtfully.

Learn: Organisms depend on interacting systems, but a simplified picture cannot diagnose an individual learner's health.

Practice and evidence: Annotate an imagined cell-system map and label unsupported health conclusions.

Thursday: Make participation inclusive

Focus: Make participation inclusive.

Learn: A student may submit text, labeled drawings or a verbal-equivalent written explanation without providing health details.

Practice and evidence: Create two equivalent evidence options for a fictional assignment.

Friday: Reflect on respectful learning

Focus: Reflect on respectful learning.

Learn: The portfolio rewards scientific understanding, accessibility and safety rather than personal biometrics.

Practice and evidence: Submit a safe learning plan, model sketch and reflection. Rubric: science 5, safety 6, access 5, reflection 4.
