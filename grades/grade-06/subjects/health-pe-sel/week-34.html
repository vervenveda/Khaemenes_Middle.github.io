Week 34 · Health, PE & SEL

A++ Big Question Capstone Research

What serious question can I investigate with evidence, calculation, and design?

Monday: Plan team roles responsibly

Focus: Plan team roles responsibly.

Learn: Leadership includes listening, consent and equivalent ways to participate.

Practice and evidence: Draft a fictional capstone team agreement with accessible roles.

Tuesday: Consider safe activity needs

Focus: Consider safe activity needs.

Learn: Wellness-related projects must respect privacy and individual differences.

Practice and evidence: Create non-identifying participation options for a hypothetical school activity.

Wednesday: Practice constructive feedback

Focus: Practice constructive feedback.

Learn: Specific, respectful critique improves a project without shaming learners.

Practice and evidence: Use an invented team disagreement to practice restatement and negotiation.

Thursday: Manage timelines flexibly

Focus: Manage timelines flexibly.

Learn: Milestones and rest periods help teams adapt without demanding private health explanations.

Practice and evidence: Create a three-stage project schedule with alternative participation methods.

Friday: Reflect on teamwork

Focus: Reflect on teamwork.

Learn: Ethical collaboration documents choices, adjustments and contributions.

Practice and evidence: Submit a teamwork and accessibility plan. Rubric: safety 6, inclusion 6, planning 4, reflection 4.
