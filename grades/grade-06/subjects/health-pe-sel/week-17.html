Week 17 · Health, PE & SEL

Midyear Portfolio and Assessment

How can I prove growth across every subject?

Monday: Reflect on safe choices

Focus: Reflect on safe choices.

Learn: Midyear learning evidence may document safety planning, inclusive activity adaptations and understanding of wellbeing without personal medical information.

Practice and evidence: Select one fictional safety plan and explain its purpose.

Tuesday: Review inclusive participation

Focus: Review inclusive participation.

Learn: Equivalent participation can include seated demonstrations, written analysis or paper models rather than compulsory exertion.

Practice and evidence: Revise a hypothetical group task to make participation optional and accessible.

Wednesday: Examine collaboration

Focus: Examine collaboration.

Learn: An evidence-based reflection names specific respectful communication practices rather than ranking classmates.

Practice and evidence: Write a fictional team scenario and identify two better choices.

Thursday: Set a realistic goal

Focus: Set a realistic goal.

Learn: A learning goal should identify a controllable academic or teamwork behavior and a way to review progress, not require disclosure of health details.

Practice and evidence: Draft a two-week self-management plan with learner choice.

Friday: Compile a confidential-safe portfolio

Focus: Compile a confidential-safe portfolio.

Learn: A printable family record can show skill evidence, reflections and optional reviewer notes without private medical or biometric data.

Practice and evidence: Submit two safe evidence artifacts and reflection. Rubric: reasoning 6, inclusion 5, safety 5, clarity 4.
