Week 28 · Health, PE & SEL

World Languages and Cultural Communication

How does language carry culture and connection?

Monday: Build inclusive greetings

Focus: Build inclusive greetings.

Learn: A welcoming classroom allows students to choose how they participate without public disclosure of home languages.

Practice and evidence: Write a respectful welcome protocol with opt-in choices and accessible alternatives.

Tuesday: Clarify misunderstandings

Focus: Clarify misunderstandings.

Learn: Tone, idiom and context can produce unintended confusion across languages.

Practice and evidence: Role-play a fictional misunderstanding and practice clarification before responding.

Wednesday: Communicate movement instructions

Focus: Communicate movement instructions.

Learn: Simple directions, visuals and demonstrations support safe participation.

Practice and evidence: Design a low-impact movement sequence with plain-language instructions and seated options.

Thursday: Advocate for access

Focus: Advocate for access.

Learn: Asking for repetition, interpretation or accessible instructions is a normal learning strategy.

Practice and evidence: Draft three respectful phrases for requesting clarification and help.

Friday: Reflect on communication equity

Focus: Reflect on communication equity.

Learn: Inclusive activities preserve privacy and allow different ways to demonstrate understanding.

Practice and evidence: Submit a communication-and-movement access plan. Rubric: safety 6, clarity 5, inclusion 5, reflection 4.
