Week 36 · Health, PE & SEL

Final Review and Grade 7 Readiness

How am I ready for seventh grade?

Monday: Reflect on flexible routines

Focus: Reflect on flexible routines.

Learn: Sleep, food, activity and rest needs differ; students should not be ranked by private wellness details.

Practice and evidence: Plan a fictional balanced week with accessible choices.

Tuesday: Practice self-advocacy

Focus: Practice self-advocacy.

Learn: Requesting help, clarification or an accommodation is a useful school skill.

Practice and evidence: Rehearse two respectful requests in a fictional scenario.

Wednesday: Support constructive collaboration

Focus: Support constructive collaboration.

Learn: Teams benefit from listening, consent and fair role choices.

Practice and evidence: Write a four-step response to a simulated group disagreement.

Thursday: Prepare for school transition

Focus: Prepare for school transition.

Learn: New routines and classmates may require organization and support networks.

Practice and evidence: Draft a non-identifying Grade 7 transition plan with optional assistance.

Friday: Celebrate growth with privacy

Focus: Celebrate growth with privacy.

Learn: Reflection can document useful habits without sharing sensitive information.

Practice and evidence: Submit a fictional wellness and teamwork reflection. Rubric: safety 6, access 6, planning 4, reflection 4.
