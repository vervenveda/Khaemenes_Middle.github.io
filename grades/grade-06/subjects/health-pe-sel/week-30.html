Week 30 · Health, PE & SEL

Argument, Debate, and Public Speaking

How can disagreement become disciplined learning?

Monday: Regulate debate emotions

Focus: Regulate debate emotions.

Learn: A disagreement about an idea need not become a judgment about someone's worth.

Practice and evidence: Practice a scripted pause-and-restate response to a fictional dispute.

Tuesday: Listen to understand

Focus: Listen to understand.

Learn: Active listening includes summarizing another person's view before arguing against it.

Practice and evidence: Role-play a safe classroom disagreement and record both viewpoints.

Wednesday: Practice inclusive participation

Focus: Practice inclusive participation.

Learn: Students can join discussion through speech, writing or other accessible means.

Practice and evidence: Design three equivalent participation options for a class debate.

Thursday: Resolve conflict constructively

Focus: Resolve conflict constructively.

Learn: A repair process identifies harm, needs, feasible choices and follow-up.

Practice and evidence: Write a mediation plan for a fictional team conflict.

Friday: Reflect on respectful advocacy

Focus: Reflect on respectful advocacy.

Learn: Good advocacy combines clear requests with safety, inclusion and dignity.

Practice and evidence: Submit a student-led participation agreement and reflection. Rubric: respect 6, inclusion 6, safety 4, reflection 4.
