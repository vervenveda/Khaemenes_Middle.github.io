Week 07 · Health, PE & SEL

Coordinate Planes and Geography

How can coordinates help us describe place, movement, and patterns?

Monday: Safe spatial pathways

Focus: Safe spatial pathways.

Learn: A coordinate path is a planning diagram; classroom activities must preserve safe clearances, consent and seated alternatives.

Practice and evidence: Draw a fictional 5×5 movement space and designate obstacles and an accessible route without requiring physical performance.

Tuesday: Read movement directions

Focus: Read movement directions.

Learn: An ordered pair names a destination relative to an origin. Moving from (0,0) to (2,0) means two units in the positive horizontal direction, not necessarily two steps in a real room.

Practice and evidence: Plot an optional route (0,0) to (2,0) to (2,2) and describe each segment with units.

Wednesday: Allow accessible alternatives

Focus: Allow accessible alternatives.

Learn: Not every learner should be required to traverse a floor grid. A finger-tracing or seated paper version can demonstrate the same spatial understanding.

Practice and evidence: Redesign a fictional movement game to include a seated version, visible markers and permission to stop.

Thursday: Plan safe navigation

Focus: Plan safe navigation.

Learn: Safe procedures identify boundaries, destination, obstacles and what to do if a route becomes inaccessible.

Practice and evidence: Annotate a school-garden simulation grid with two possible routes and explain which is clearer, not which person moves fastest.

Friday: Spatial awareness portfolio

Focus: Spatial awareness portfolio.

Learn: Evidence can show planning and inclusive directions rather than movement speed or sensitive personal data.

Practice and evidence: Submit a clearly labeled grid, two routes and a reflection on accessibility. Rubric: plotting 5, safety 6, inclusion 5, explanation 4.
