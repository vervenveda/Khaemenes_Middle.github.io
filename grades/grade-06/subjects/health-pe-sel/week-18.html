Week 18 · Health, PE & SEL

Waves, Sound, and Communication

How do waves carry energy and information?

Monday: Communicate with consent

Focus: Communicate with consent.

Learn: Effective collaboration considers listening needs, language and personal boundaries. No student must disclose hearing or medical information.

Practice and evidence: Write a fictional group agreement with options for spoken or written participation.

Tuesday: Respect safe sound practices

Focus: Respect safe sound practices.

Learn: A learning task need not use loud sound to teach waves. Paper graphs and low-volume, optional demonstrations avoid unnecessary exposure.

Practice and evidence: Replace a hypothetical loud-volume experiment with a written simulation.

Wednesday: Use clarification skills

Focus: Use clarification skills.

Learn: A respectful learner can request repetition, captions, a transcript or clarification without needing to explain personal reasons.

Practice and evidence: Draft a short dialogue requesting a written version of instructions.

Thursday: Make group messages accessible

Focus: Make group messages accessible.

Learn: A plain-language announcement may use headings, readable text, captions and clear next actions rather than relying on audio only.

Practice and evidence: Revise a fictional classroom announcement into an accessible written format.

Friday: Reflect on inclusive communication

Focus: Reflect on inclusive communication.

Learn: Portfolio evidence should reward thoughtful choices, accurate models and collaboration without sensitive personal records.

Practice and evidence: Submit a safety plan, accessible announcement and reflection. Rubric: safety 6, inclusion 6, clarity 4, reflection 4.
