Week 06 · Health, PE & SEL

Rational Numbers and Ancient Number Systems

How have cultures represented numbers and meaning across time?

Monday: Compare a neutral baseline

Focus: Compare a neutral baseline.

Learn: Signed values indicate change relative to a reference. A fictional reading schedule of +10 minutes means 10 minutes above a chosen baseline; −5 means 5 below. A negative deviation is not a judgment of a learner.

Practice and evidence: Make a fictional weekly optional-activity table with baseline 20 minutes and deviations of +5, −5 and 0 minutes.

Tuesday: Number-line planning

Focus: Number-line planning.

Learn: A number line helps depict gains and reductions in planned time. Changes describe a schedule, not health or athletic ability.

Practice and evidence: Plot the fictional deviations +5, −5, 0 and +10, and reconstruct actual times relative to the 20-minute baseline.

Wednesday: Interpret data kindly

Focus: Interpret data kindly.

Learn: Group averages may conceal different access, interests or individual needs. No one must share personal physical-health information to complete a math activity.

Practice and evidence: Rewrite three fictional group-progress comments to avoid shame and unsupported comparisons.

Thursday: Safe routine adjustments

Focus: Safe routine adjustments.

Learn: An effective plan allows rest, choice and accessibility. A change from 15 to 10 minutes is −5 minutes, which can be a thoughtful adjustment rather than a failure.

Practice and evidence: Design a flexible fictional two-activity routine and note when a participant could adapt the plan or stop.

Friday: Wellness reflection portfolio

Focus: Wellness reflection portfolio.

Learn: Reflections focus on appropriate planning, signed-number accuracy, safe choices and respectful language.

Practice and evidence: Submit your baseline chart, signed-number line, and accessible routine with two revisions. Rubric: arithmetic 5, clarity 5, inclusion 6, reflection 4.
