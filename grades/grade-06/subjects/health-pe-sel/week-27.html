Week 27 · Health, PE & SEL

Trade Routes, Migration, and Exchange

How do movement and exchange transform societies?

Monday: Respect stories of movement

Focus: Respect stories of movement.

Learn: Students should never be pressured to disclose personal migration or family history.

Practice and evidence: Use fictional journeys to identify ways peers can welcome newcomers.

Tuesday: Practice supportive communication

Focus: Practice supportive communication.

Learn: Active listening and nonjudgmental questions can reduce misunderstandings.

Practice and evidence: Role-play a fictional school welcome with optional language support.

Wednesday: Plan accessible active routes

Focus: Plan accessible active routes.

Learn: Travel activities should include safe movement options and rest choices.

Practice and evidence: Design a fictional walking or seated movement challenge with two accommodations.

Thursday: Resolve cultural misunderstandings

Focus: Resolve cultural misunderstandings.

Learn: Clarification, consent and respect are more useful than guessing someone's intent.

Practice and evidence: Write a four-step repair plan for an invented misunderstanding.

Friday: Build a welcoming community

Focus: Build a welcoming community.

Learn: Shared rules should protect privacy and permit varied participation.

Practice and evidence: Submit a newcomer-support guide and inclusive movement plan. Rubric: respect 6, access 6, safety 4, reflection 4.
