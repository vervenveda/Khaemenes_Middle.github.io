Week 16 · Health, PE & SEL

Forces, Motion, and Systems

How do forces and systems explain motion?

Monday: Movement choices

Focus: Movement choices.

Learn: Motion learning can be completed through a graph, seated discussion, or paper simulation without requiring personal movement.

Practice and evidence: Create a fictional route map that offers several equivalent participation options.

Tuesday: Calculate a safe model

Focus: Calculate a safe model.

Learn: A simulated object covering 8 meters in four seconds has an average speed of 2 m/s; this is not a measure of learner fitness.

Practice and evidence: Graph the invented time-distance data and label the units.

Wednesday: Recognize physical boundaries

Focus: Recognize physical boundaries.

Learn: Movement activities should avoid mandatory exertion, competition pressure or sharing sensitive measurements.

Practice and evidence: Rewrite a hypothetical group assignment as an inclusive paper-based investigation.

Thursday: Communication in a group

Focus: Communication in a group.

Learn: A systems design exercise benefits from shared roles, agreed boundaries and opportunities to opt out of physical demonstrations.

Practice and evidence: Write a respectful fictional group protocol with accessible reporting.

Friday: Reflect on inclusive design

Focus: Reflect on inclusive design.

Learn: Learner evidence should document the model, calculations and teamwork choices, not physical performance.

Practice and evidence: Submit a route sketch and reflection. Rubric: measurement 6, inclusion 6, communication 4, reflection 4.
