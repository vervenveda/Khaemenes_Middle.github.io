Week 31 · Health, PE & SEL

Arts, Media, and Message Design

How do artists and media makers shape meaning?

Monday: Recognize emotional framing

Focus: Recognize emotional framing.

Learn: Media can trigger strong feelings; noticing reactions helps learners evaluate claims without shame.

Practice and evidence: Annotate emotional language in a fictional wellness advertisement.

Tuesday: Evaluate safety messages

Focus: Evaluate safety messages.

Learn: Health or movement claims should be proportional to evidence and should not promise universal results.

Practice and evidence: Compare two invented activity messages, marking unsupported claims.

Wednesday: Practice media boundaries

Focus: Practice media boundaries.

Learn: Personal information and body images should not be required in student demonstrations.

Practice and evidence: Draft a privacy-respecting participation policy for classroom media creation.

Thursday: Design an inclusive movement message

Focus: Design an inclusive movement message.

Learn: Movement instructions need clear steps, accessible alternatives and safe pacing.

Practice and evidence: Storyboard a seated-or-standing coordination activity with text description.

Friday: Reflect on respectful messaging

Focus: Reflect on respectful messaging.

Learn: Helpful advocacy avoids stigmatizing individuals and welcomes varied participation.

Practice and evidence: Submit an accessible wellness-media critique and revised message. Rubric: safety 6, respect 6, clarity 4, reflection 4.
