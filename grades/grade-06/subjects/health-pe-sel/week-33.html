Week 33 · Health, PE & SEL

Technology, Data, and Ethical Creation

How can tools strengthen evidence without replacing judgment?

Monday: Practice digital decision-making

Focus: Practice digital decision-making.

Learn: Apps offer choices but should not secretly rank students or require sensitive disclosures.

Practice and evidence: Critique a fictional habit tracker for privacy and fairness.

Tuesday: Set balanced media routines

Focus: Set balanced media routines.

Learn: Well-being routines vary; device use choices should be flexible and nonjudgmental.

Practice and evidence: Develop a fictional screen-and-offline schedule with rest options.

Wednesday: Recognize manipulative design

Focus: Recognize manipulative design.

Learn: Dark patterns can pressure a user to share data or remain online.

Practice and evidence: Identify three coercive choices in a simulated app interface.

Thursday: Advocate for accessible options

Focus: Advocate for accessible options.

Learn: Learners can request readable text, quiet settings and offline alternatives.

Practice and evidence: Draft respectful support requests for two fictional situations.

Friday: Create a safe-use charter

Focus: Create a safe-use charter.

Learn: Student technology agreements should center consent, privacy and inclusion.

Practice and evidence: Submit a one-page school technology charter. Rubric: privacy 6, safety 6, access 4, reflection 4.
