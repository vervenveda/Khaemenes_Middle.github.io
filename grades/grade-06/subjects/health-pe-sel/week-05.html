Week 05 · Health, PE & SEL

Fraction Division and Procedural Writing

How can models explain division when the numbers are fractions?

Monday: Stepwise safe routines

Focus: Stepwise safe routines.

Learn: Clear safety procedures state the location, accessibility choices, warm-up, optional movement, rest, and cool-down. They avoid demanding unsafe or uncomfortable participation.

Practice and evidence: Draft a fictional 12-minute inclusive routine with a warm-up, activity, optional rests, and seated version.

Tuesday: Fractions of planned time

Focus: Fractions of planned time.

Learn: A fraction of time can help organize routines. One-quarter of 12 minutes is 3 minutes; half is 6 minutes. These are planning quantities, not fitness scores.

Practice and evidence: Create a 12-minute schedule divided into four equal 3-minute intervals and explain what happens in each.

Wednesday: Respect choice and boundaries

Focus: Respect choice and boundaries.

Learn: Effective wellness instructions explain safe options, consent, and when a person should stop and seek help. Private medical information should not be collected for class exercises.

Practice and evidence: Revise a fictional exercise instruction that forces participation, replacing it with accessible safe choices.

Thursday: Practice and feedback

Focus: Practice and feedback.

Learn: A procedure can be tested with paper scenarios rather than physical performance. Feedback should address clarity and inclusion, not body traits.

Practice and evidence: Ask a peer or trusted adult to review your written plan or use a checklist; log two improvements.

Friday: Inclusive wellness procedure

Focus: Inclusive wellness procedure.

Learn: Students demonstrate safe planning, fractional scheduling, and respectful adjustments, using fictional data when appropriate.

Practice and evidence: Submit a clear 12-minute optional movement plan, a fractional time diagram, and two revisions. Rubric: safety 6, fraction accuracy 5, access 5, clarity 4.
