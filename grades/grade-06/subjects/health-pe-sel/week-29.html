Week 29 · Health, PE & SEL

Research Writing: From Question to Thesis

How do researchers build trustworthy explanations?

Monday: Ask respectful wellness questions

Focus: Ask respectful wellness questions.

Learn: Health inquiry should rely on non-identifying information and must not demand classmates disclose conditions.

Practice and evidence: Write a fictional question about accessible activity instructions, not private student health.

Tuesday: Evaluate activity claims

Focus: Evaluate activity claims.

Learn: Anecdote differs from a controlled study and broad promises may ignore individual differences.

Practice and evidence: Compare two invented fitness claims, noting which evidence would be needed.

Wednesday: Design a safe movement observation

Focus: Design a safe movement observation.

Learn: Low-impact choices, rests and consent support participation.

Practice and evidence: Plan a seated or standing balance sequence with voluntary participation and no personal health tracking.

Thursday: Communicate findings compassionately

Focus: Communicate findings compassionately.

Learn: Research about people should avoid deficit language, stigmatization and identifying details.

Practice and evidence: Revise a fictional class report to remove stereotypes and private identifiers.

Friday: Publish an ethical participation guide

Focus: Publish an ethical participation guide.

Learn: A guide should explain access options, safety boundaries and evidence limits.

Practice and evidence: Submit an accessible activity guide and ethical research reflection. Rubric: safety 6, evidence 5, access 5, reflection 4.
