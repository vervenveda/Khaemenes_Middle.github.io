Week 21 · Health, PE & SEL

Ecosystems and Energy Flow

How do energy and matter move through ecosystems?

Monday: Engage safely with ecosystems

Focus: Engage safely with ecosystems.

Learn: Environmental observation can occur through pictures, paper maps or optional outdoor experiences without requiring strenuous activity.

Practice and evidence: Write a paper-based fictional habitat observation activity with learner-choice options.

Tuesday: Practice responsible care

Focus: Practice responsible care.

Learn: Learning about stewardship should avoid handling wild animals, unknown plants or unsafe water.

Practice and evidence: Create a safety checklist for a hypothetical pond study.

Wednesday: Explain food-web connections

Focus: Explain food-web connections.

Learn: A grass-rabbit-fox chain represents feeding relationships but does not imply a single change always produces a specific population outcome.

Practice and evidence: Make a simple diagram and identify one uncertainty.

Thursday: Collaborate respectfully

Focus: Collaborate respectfully.

Learn: Group habitat projects can assign research, illustration and writing roles without requiring disclosure of personal information.

Practice and evidence: Draft an inclusive team agreement and alternate solo evidence path.

Friday: Reflect on stewardship

Focus: Reflect on stewardship.

Learn: An evidence portfolio rewards informed care, accurate ecology and learner autonomy instead of physical exposure.

Practice and evidence: Submit a safe observation plan and food-web reflection. Rubric: science 5, safety 6, inclusion 5, clarity 4.
