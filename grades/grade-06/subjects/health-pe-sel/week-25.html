Week 25 · Health, PE & SEL

Belief Systems, Law, and Culture

How do beliefs and laws shape community life?

Monday: Discuss fair rules

Focus: Discuss fair rules.

Learn: Group norms should support participation and protect people without demanding disclosure of personal beliefs or identity.

Practice and evidence: Review a fictional classroom rule for fairness, privacy and accessibility.

Tuesday: Practice respectful disagreement

Focus: Practice respectful disagreement.

Learn: People can disagree about a tradition while treating each other with dignity. A good response asks for context before judging.

Practice and evidence: Write a two-person fictional dialogue resolving a disagreement without stereotypes.

Wednesday: Consider equal access

Focus: Consider equal access.

Learn: A shared activity rule can unintentionally exclude learners unless equivalent participation methods are available.

Practice and evidence: Revise a fictional group practice to include a written or seated participation alternative.

Thursday: Identify peaceful remedies

Focus: Identify peaceful remedies.

Learn: Fairness requires an understandable way to raise a concern and correct a mistake, not only enforcement.

Practice and evidence: Create a step-by-step fictional class concern-and-review process.

Friday: Reflect on community responsibility

Focus: Reflect on community responsibility.

Learn: Evidence can show empathetic reasoning, listening and problem solving without sensitive personal disclosures.

Practice and evidence: Submit a fair-rules proposal and reflection. Rubric: empathy 6, fairness 6, access 4, clarity 4.
