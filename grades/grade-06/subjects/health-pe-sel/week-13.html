Week 13 · Health, PE & SEL

Volume and Three-Dimensional Modeling

How can 3D models solve real problems?

Monday: Plan storage inclusively

Focus: Plan storage inclusively.

Learn: A fictional equipment cabinet 2×1×1 m has external volume 2 m³; that does not prove usable interior capacity.

Practice and evidence: Create a labeled paper storage plan with an accessible alternative to moving equipment.

Tuesday: Compare equipment boxes

Focus: Compare equipment boxes.

Learn: Two fictional boxes 1×1×2 m and 2×1×1 m both occupy 2 m³ but can differ in how items fit.

Practice and evidence: Calculate both volumes and note why arrangement and access also matter.

Wednesday: Respect safe handling

Focus: Respect safe handling.

Learn: Storage design should consider path clearance and the need for assistance; mathematical capacity does not determine safe lifting or individual ability.

Practice and evidence: Write an inclusive fictional equipment-access procedure without prescribing weight limits.

Thursday: Evaluate a layout

Focus: Evaluate a layout.

Learn: A shelf volume formula does not account for walls, obstructions or learner preferences. Adaptations should be planned rather than assumed.

Practice and evidence: Annotate a fictional storage area with a clear route and seated planning alternative.

Friday: Wellbeing design reflection

Focus: Wellbeing design reflection.

Learn: Portfolio evidence should reward spatial reasoning, safe choices and accessible options, not physical strength.

Practice and evidence: Submit drawings, two calculations and a reflection. Rubric: volume 6, safety 6, access 4, explanation 4.
