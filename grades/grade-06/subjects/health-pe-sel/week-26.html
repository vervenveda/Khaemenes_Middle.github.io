Week 26 · Health, PE & SEL

Classical Civilizations and Civic Ideas

How did classical societies influence civic ideas and public life?

Monday: Discuss participation safely

Focus: Discuss participation safely.

Learn: Classical athletic traditions differed by place and period; modern PE must welcome everyone regardless of historical exclusions.

Practice and evidence: Develop three rules for a low-impact movement game that accommodates varied mobility and comfort.

Tuesday: Explore disagreement respectfully

Focus: Explore disagreement respectfully.

Learn: Disagreement about community rules can be managed with active listening and nonpersonal language.

Practice and evidence: Role-play two students proposing different game rules; restate each view before replying.

Wednesday: Practice cooperative movement

Focus: Practice cooperative movement.

Learn: A fair activity adjusts roles, pacing and equipment without assigning worth to physical ability.

Practice and evidence: Run a five-minute optional walking, seated or stationary coordination activity and log perceived effort without ranking students.

Thursday: Resolve a group conflict

Focus: Resolve a group conflict.

Learn: A useful agreement names the problem, affected people, workable alternatives and a review point.

Practice and evidence: Use an invented dispute over playing space to write a four-step mediation plan.

Friday: Reflect on equitable participation

Focus: Reflect on equitable participation.

Learn: A good rule is understandable, safe, flexible and revisable based on feedback.

Practice and evidence: Submit an inclusive game rulebook and reflection describing two accommodations. Rubric: safety 6, equity 6, collaboration 4, reflection 4.
