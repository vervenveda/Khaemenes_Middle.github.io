Week 22 · Health, PE & SEL

Earth Systems and Water

How do Earth systems interact and affect human life?

Monday: Understand water safety

Focus: Understand water safety.

Learn: Water-related learning should avoid entering unfamiliar bodies of water or handling unsafe samples. A paper simulation is an appropriate alternative.

Practice and evidence: Draft safety rules for an optional fictional watershed study.

Tuesday: Respect hydration privacy

Focus: Respect hydration privacy.

Learn: Hydration and wellbeing can be discussed generally without collecting private health data or prescribing an individual's fluid intake.

Practice and evidence: Create a fictional wellbeing poster with no personal health measurements.

Wednesday: Explore environmental access

Focus: Explore environmental access.

Learn: Safe drinking-water access is a community issue; classroom exercises should avoid making unsupported claims about classmates or families.

Practice and evidence: Compare two invented community water-delivery scenarios and name evidence gaps.

Thursday: Plan inclusive participation

Focus: Plan inclusive participation.

Learn: Provide seated, written and diagram-based options for water-cycle learning, with a clear opt-out from outdoor demonstrations.

Practice and evidence: Revise a fictional group activity so every learner can document equivalent understanding.

Friday: Reflect on informed care

Focus: Reflect on informed care.

Learn: A safe portfolio shows accurate science, responsible decisions, accessibility and careful treatment of uncertainty.

Practice and evidence: Submit a paper watershed safety plan and reflection. Rubric: safety 6, science 5, inclusion 5, clarity 4.
