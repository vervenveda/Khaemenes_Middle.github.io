Week 05 · Integrated Research Projects

Fraction Division and Procedural Writing

How can models explain division when the numbers are fractions?

Monday: Plan a reproducible model

Focus: Plan a reproducible model.

Learn: An integrated investigation can combine fractional measurement, writing, and design. Every step should be sufficiently precise for another learner to reproduce it.

Practice and evidence: Define a paper model showing how many 1/6-length segments fit in 2/3 of a whole; list materials and assumptions.

Tuesday: Calculate with visual proof

Focus: Calculate with visual proof.

Learn: Since 2/3 equals 4/6, dividing 2/3 by 1/6 gives four units. A strip partitioned into sixths makes the quotient visible.

Practice and evidence: Draw the original fraction strip, shade four of six pieces, and write both the equation and interpretation.

Wednesday: Collect test evidence

Focus: Collect test evidence.

Learn: An evidence log should separate instructions, observed output, simulated data, and corrections. A single valid example does not test every possible procedure.

Practice and evidence: Build a table with instructions, expected count, actual paper result or hypothetical check, and revision notes.

Thursday: Revise for diverse learners

Focus: Revise for diverse learners.

Learn: Accessible models use visible boundaries, labeled segments, optional tactile methods, and readable directions.

Practice and evidence: Redesign your procedure to work on paper with no internet, with a color-independent alternative and an explicit safety note.

Friday: Defend a finished design

Focus: Defend a finished design.

Learn: A final defense explains the math, procedural choices, observed evidence, limitations, and what was changed after review.

Practice and evidence: Submit a fraction-strip model, five-step guide, test log and one-page reflection. Rubric: mathematical reasoning 6, reproducibility 5, accessibility 5, reflection 4.
