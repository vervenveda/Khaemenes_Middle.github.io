Week 27 · Integrated Research Projects

Trade Routes, Migration, and Exchange

How do movement and exchange transform societies?

Monday: Define an exchange-network question

Focus: Define an exchange-network question.

Learn: Comparisons need clear scope, stakeholders and evidence limitations.

Practice and evidence: Choose a fictional three-market network and record what you hope to learn.

Tuesday: Calculate meaningful statistics

Focus: Calculate meaningful statistics.

Learn: Mean, median and range can describe route-time samples without proving how real travelers moved.

Practice and evidence: For invented travel times 3, 5, 5, 7 and 10 days, calculate mean, median and range.

Wednesday: Compare evidence and perspectives

Focus: Compare evidence and perspectives.

Learn: Statistics, historical sources and cultural interpretation offer different kinds of evidence.

Practice and evidence: Prepare a matrix with two sourced contextual facts and two dataset observations.

Thursday: Design and critique an exhibit

Focus: Design and critique an exhibit.

Learn: A clear exhibit distinguishes simulation, documented history and reasonable inference.

Practice and evidence: Build a paper route infographic with an accessible text description and peer-review notes.

Friday: Defend the final synthesis

Focus: Defend the final synthesis.

Learn: Good interdisciplinary work communicates uncertainty and meaningful conclusions.

Practice and evidence: Present a one-page exchange atlas with chart, historical notes, inclusion statement and revision. Rubric: statistics 6, sources 6, clarity 4, ethics 4.
