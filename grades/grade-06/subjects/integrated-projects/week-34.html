Week 34 · Integrated Research Projects

A++ Big Question Capstone Research

What serious question can I investigate with evidence, calculation, and design?

Monday: Define a feasible inquiry

Focus: Define a feasible inquiry.

Learn: An interdisciplinary capstone needs a meaningful question and bounded deliverables.

Practice and evidence: Select a fictional school-library resource question and list evidence needs.

Tuesday: Build a mathematical constraint

Focus: Build a mathematical constraint.

Learn: Equations and inequalities can model costs, limits and feasible choices.

Practice and evidence: Use a fictional $30 budget with $4 per kit and $6 fixed cost; solve 4x+6≤30.

Wednesday: Connect disciplines

Focus: Connect disciplines.

Learn: Math, science and civic context answer different parts of a proposal.

Practice and evidence: Prepare a matrix with one verified equation, one model and two contextual source notes.

Thursday: Prototype and revise

Focus: Prototype and revise.

Learn: A proposal should be tested with simulated users and a documented revision.

Practice and evidence: Create an accessible one-page mockup and record two critique changes.

Friday: Defend the capstone plan

Focus: Defend the capstone plan.

Learn: Strong projects identify evidence limits, feasible budgets and next steps.

Practice and evidence: Submit a mini-capstone proposal with constraints, prototype, sources and reflection. Rubric: math 6, evidence 6, access 4, revision 4.
