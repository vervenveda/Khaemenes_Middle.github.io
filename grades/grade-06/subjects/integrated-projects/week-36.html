Week 36 · Integrated Research Projects

Final Review and Grade 7 Readiness

How am I ready for seventh grade?

Monday: Organize the learning portfolio

Focus: Organize the learning portfolio.

Learn: A final portfolio should identify independent work and evidence from all nine subjects.

Practice and evidence: Create a checklist of nine example artifacts and their learning goals.

Tuesday: Review mathematical evidence

Focus: Review mathematical evidence.

Learn: Statistics, ratios, equations and geometry help quantify claims when units and assumptions are clear.

Practice and evidence: For fictional data 2, 4, 4, 6, 9 calculate median and range; explain what the sample cannot prove.

Wednesday: Defend interdisciplinary reasoning

Focus: Defend interdisciplinary reasoning.

Learn: A conclusion should connect calculations, context and scientific or design evidence.

Practice and evidence: Build a source-to-claim matrix for a fictional school-improvement proposal.

Thursday: Prepare the readiness presentation

Focus: Prepare the readiness presentation.

Learn: A good summary includes an honest reflection and accessible participation options.

Practice and evidence: Plan a short capstone defense with questions and alternatives to live speech.

Friday: Build the Grade 7 bridge

Focus: Build the Grade 7 bridge.

Learn: Completion evidence documents readiness, but promotion depends on actual assessment records.

Practice and evidence: Submit a nine-subject portfolio index, critique log and next-year goals. Rubric: evidence 6, mathematics 6, access 4, reflection 4.
