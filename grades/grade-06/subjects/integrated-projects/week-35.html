Week 35 · Integrated Research Projects

Capstone Studio and Oral Defense

How can I make my evidence clear enough to defend publicly?

Monday: Choose final deliverables

Focus: Choose final deliverables.

Learn: A capstone defense requires question, calculation, evidence, prototype and stated limitations.

Practice and evidence: Create a checklist for a fictional accessible library design.

Tuesday: Explain measurements

Focus: Explain measurements.

Learn: Geometry can justify material estimates only when units and assumptions are correct.

Practice and evidence: For a 4 m by 3 m by 2 m hall model, calculate rectangular-prism volume and label m³.

Wednesday: Compare possible solutions

Focus: Compare possible solutions.

Learn: A fair proposal assesses resources, access and tradeoffs.

Practice and evidence: Develop two design options with calculations and stakeholder notes.

Thursday: Practice evidence questions

Focus: Practice evidence questions.

Learn: A presenter should explain how the model was checked and what it cannot establish.

Practice and evidence: Write three skeptical questions and honest responses.

Friday: Deliver a final defense

Focus: Deliver a final defense.

Learn: An interdisciplinary defense connects sourced context, math, ethics and revisions.

Practice and evidence: Submit a presentation outline, area or volume evidence and critique log. Rubric: math 6, sources 6, accessibility 4, revision 4.
