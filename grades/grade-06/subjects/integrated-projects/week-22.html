Week 22 · Integrated Research Projects

Earth Systems and Water

How do Earth systems interact and affect human life?

Monday: Define a watershed question

Focus: Define a watershed question.

Learn: A fictional community may ask how rainfall and settlement affect a river, but an invented map cannot establish real hydrological impacts.

Practice and evidence: Write a research question and label a fictional watershed map.

Tuesday: Create a cycle model

Focus: Create a cycle model.

Learn: A water-cycle model shows linked atmosphere, land, living things and waterways through precipitation, evaporation and runoff.

Practice and evidence: Draw a clearly labeled system diagram and note two model limits.

Wednesday: Interpret simulated measurements

Focus: Interpret simulated measurements.

Learn: Rainfall of 2,4,6 mm over three equal observation periods totals 12 mm; these numbers do not establish real flood risk.

Practice and evidence: Graph the invented series and write one supported conclusion and two unknowns.

Thursday: Weigh community choices

Focus: Weigh community choices.

Learn: Fictional water management may compare access, ecosystem protection, cost and resilience without assuming one universal solution.

Practice and evidence: Build a fictional decision matrix for two water-use approaches.

Friday: Present a water-systems capstone

Focus: Present a water-systems capstone.

Learn: A sound final proposal combines a cycle diagram, graph, responsible source notes and an inclusive civic recommendation.

Practice and evidence: Submit a fictional watershed report and revision. Rubric: science 6, data 5, fairness 5, reflection 4.
