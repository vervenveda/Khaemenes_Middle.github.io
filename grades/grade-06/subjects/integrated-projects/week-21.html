Week 21 · Integrated Research Projects

Ecosystems and Energy Flow

How do energy and matter move through ecosystems?

Monday: Frame a watershed study

Focus: Frame a watershed study.

Learn: A fictional pond ecosystem supports producers, consumers and decomposers interacting with water, light and nutrients.

Practice and evidence: Write a clear hypothetical question and draw a simple labeled food web.

Tuesday: Analyze population data

Focus: Analyze population data.

Learn: Simulated producer counts of 40,35,20 across three seasons show decline but cannot establish its cause.

Practice and evidence: Graph the values and write one supported conclusion and two plausible competing explanations.

Wednesday: Consider land use

Focus: Consider land use.

Learn: Fictional farm, housing and protected-habitat options may serve different human and ecological needs.

Practice and evidence: Build a decision matrix with two benefits and limitations for each option.

Thursday: Develop a conservation proposal

Focus: Develop a conservation proposal.

Learn: An accountable proposal should distinguish classroom simulation from verified local conditions and identify data to collect.

Practice and evidence: Write a source-verification plan and a brief inclusive community explanation.

Friday: Present the ecosystem capstone

Focus: Present the ecosystem capstone.

Learn: A coherent final artifact links a food web, population graph, qualified civic argument and explicit uncertainty.

Practice and evidence: Submit original watershed proposal and revision. Rubric: ecology 6, data 5, fairness 5, explanation 4.
