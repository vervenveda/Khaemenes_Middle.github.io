Week 32 · Integrated Research Projects

Health, Identity, and Responsible Choices

How do healthy routines support learning and identity?

Monday: Choose an inclusive design need

Focus: Choose an inclusive design need.

Learn: A school wellness-space proposal should begin with accessibility and voluntary use.

Practice and evidence: Identify three fictional users' needs without inventing medical diagnoses.

Tuesday: Model coordinates accurately

Focus: Model coordinates accurately.

Learn: Signed coordinates can map locations, not personal value or health.

Practice and evidence: Plot A(-3,2), B(4,2), and C(4,-1) on a fictional school garden plan.

Wednesday: Evaluate route distance

Focus: Evaluate route distance.

Learn: Horizontal and vertical grid distances require labeled units and clear assumptions.

Practice and evidence: Find the horizontal distance A to B and vertical distance B to C on the model.

Thursday: Critique the plan

Focus: Critique the plan.

Learn: Evidence and stakeholder perspectives can identify accessibility or safety concerns.

Practice and evidence: Revise the imaginary plan after two fictional user reviews.

Friday: Defend a design recommendation

Focus: Defend a design recommendation.

Learn: A proposal should explain its model, limits, inclusive choices and supporting rationale.

Practice and evidence: Submit a labeled coordinate plan and one-page proposal. Rubric: math 6, evidence 6, access 4, revision 4.
