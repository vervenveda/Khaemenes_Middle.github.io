Week 17 · Integrated Research Projects

Midyear Portfolio and Assessment

How can I prove growth across every subject?

Monday: Choose interdisciplinary evidence

Focus: Choose interdisciplinary evidence.

Learn: Select work demonstrating a question, calculations, written argument and a creative or technical product.

Practice and evidence: Create a dated evidence index with three artifacts from different subjects.

Tuesday: Trace claims to evidence

Focus: Trace claims to evidence.

Learn: A diagram or table supports only the assertions it actually measures; invented classroom scenarios must be labeled.

Practice and evidence: Annotate a fictional transit graph and identify a claim it supports and one it cannot.

Wednesday: Revise with peer criteria

Focus: Revise with peer criteria.

Learn: Review can be constructive without requiring peer disclosure. A checklist should identify clarity, units, sources and accessibility.

Practice and evidence: Apply a four-part checklist to an original draft and record the revision.

Thursday: Prepare a presentation

Focus: Prepare a presentation.

Learn: A brief written or optional oral defense explains design decisions and uncertainties in an accessible format.

Practice and evidence: Draft a 90-second equivalent written script naming a calculation, tradeoff and limitation.

Friday: Complete the midyear capstone

Focus: Complete the midyear capstone.

Learn: The portfolio joins evidence index, corrected artifact, model reasoning and a respectful learner reflection.

Practice and evidence: Submit a three-artifact capstone and reflection. Rubric: evidence 6, synthesis 6, accessibility 4, reflection 4.
