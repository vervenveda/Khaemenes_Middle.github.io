Week 15 · Integrated Research Projects

Thermal Energy and Phase Change

How does energy explain changes in matter?

Monday: Set a thermal evidence question

Focus: Set a thermal evidence question.

Learn: A fictional community center wants to compare two modeled room-warming patterns without claiming measured actual energy use.

Practice and evidence: Write a statistical question and label two invented time-temperature datasets.

Tuesday: Plot simulated trends

Focus: Plot simulated trends.

Learn: Room A might show 16,18,20,20 °C while room B shows 16,17,18,19 °C at four equally spaced times.

Practice and evidence: Graph both with readable axis labels and a legend, and describe the final temperatures.

Wednesday: Calculate differences

Focus: Calculate differences.

Learn: Room A increases by 4 °C over three intervals and B by 3 °C. This alone does not establish which heating method is efficient or affordable.

Practice and evidence: Compute changes and distinguish numerical evidence from an unsupported efficiency claim.

Thursday: Weigh competing constraints

Focus: Weigh competing constraints.

Learn: A public design choice involves comfort, accessibility, energy resources and reliable measurements rather than temperature alone.

Practice and evidence: Create a fictional decision matrix with three nonnumeric criteria and unanswered questions.

Friday: Defend a measured proposal

Focus: Defend a measured proposal.

Learn: A meaningful final report combines charts, correct comparisons, accessible presentation and limitations.

Practice and evidence: Submit an invented community-center report and revision. Rubric: calculations 6, graph 5, tradeoffs 5, caution 4.
