Week 31 · Integrated Research Projects

Arts, Media, and Message Design

How do artists and media makers shape meaning?

Monday: Define a public-message goal

Focus: Define a public-message goal.

Learn: A design project starts with a precise audience, action and evidence-based message.

Practice and evidence: Choose a fictional community-library campaign and identify stakeholders.

Tuesday: Model signed design changes

Focus: Model signed design changes.

Learn: Signed quantities show differences above and below a baseline, not moral quality.

Practice and evidence: For hypothetical brightness offsets -2, +3 and -1, compute their sum and mean.

Wednesday: Build a source-and-design matrix

Focus: Build a source-and-design matrix.

Learn: A sound proposal distinguishes quantitative models, sourced facts and artistic choices.

Practice and evidence: List two supported claims, one signed-number computation and one accessibility goal.

Thursday: Conduct an inclusive critique

Focus: Conduct an inclusive critique.

Learn: Audience tests reveal legibility and interpretation problems before release.

Practice and evidence: Run three fictional review scenarios and record revisions without collecting personal data.

Friday: Defend the final campaign

Focus: Defend the final campaign.

Learn: A responsible recommendation explains tradeoffs, sources and uncertainties.

Practice and evidence: Submit a campaign storyboard, signed-number calculations, citations and reflection. Rubric: math 6, evidence 6, access 4, revision 4.
