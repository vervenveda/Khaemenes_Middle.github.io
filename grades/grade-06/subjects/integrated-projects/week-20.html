Week 20 · Integrated Research Projects

Cells, Systems, and Scientific Models

How do small structures support living systems?

Monday: Frame a cell-system inquiry

Focus: Frame a cell-system inquiry.

Learn: A clear project question asks how selected structures help an invented model cell operate and notes that the model is simplified.

Practice and evidence: Identify three structures and formulate a testable explanatory question.

Tuesday: Compare two cell types

Focus: Compare two cell types.

Learn: Plant cells typically possess walls and chloroplasts whereas animal cells lack these, while both have membranes.

Practice and evidence: Draw a two-column comparison table and carefully qualify which structures are typical.

Wednesday: Explain scaled illustrations

Focus: Explain scaled illustrations.

Learn: A 2 cm paper drawing of a cell cannot represent its actual size without a stated scale. Scientific models may intentionally distort proportions.

Practice and evidence: Write an honest diagram caption and a brief scale disclaimer.

Thursday: Review interpretation

Focus: Review interpretation.

Learn: Structure-function claims should cite trusted instructional knowledge rather than invent microscope observations.

Practice and evidence: Write a CER-style interpretation with two evidence statements and one limitation.

Friday: Present a systems capstone

Focus: Present a systems capstone.

Learn: A finished report combines labeled models, a comparison table, accessible descriptions and an explanation of scientific limits.

Practice and evidence: Submit the two-model packet and 150-word reflection. Rubric: content 6, model 6, access 4, reflection 4.
