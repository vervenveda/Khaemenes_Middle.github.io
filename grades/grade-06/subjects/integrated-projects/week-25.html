Week 25 · Integrated Research Projects

Belief Systems, Law, and Culture

How do beliefs and laws shape community life?

Monday: Define a community-rules inquiry

Focus: Define a community-rules inquiry.

Learn: A fictional town needs a clear, fair rule for sharing a common space. The inquiry should consider access, obligations and review.

Practice and evidence: Write an invented proposed rule and identify the people affected.

Tuesday: Quantify a fairness scenario

Focus: Quantify a fairness scenario.

Learn: If 24 fictional library hours are divided into 3 equal blocks, each lasts 8 hours. Equal quantity may not mean equally usable time.

Practice and evidence: Calculate allocations and explain one accessibility tradeoff.

Wednesday: Compare perspectives

Focus: Compare perspectives.

Learn: A learner, librarian and caregiver may experience the proposed rule differently; avoid treating any imagined viewpoint as a documented fact.

Practice and evidence: Develop a stakeholder table with benefits, burdens and uncertainties.

Thursday: Record source integrity

Focus: Record source integrity.

Learn: A class-created rule is not a real statute; design notes and fictional interviews must be marked as inventions.

Practice and evidence: Build a source and revision log distinguishing created and researched material.

Friday: Defend a civic proposal

Focus: Defend a civic proposal.

Learn: A good capstone connects clear language, mathematics, empathy, cultural respect and a fair appeals process.

Practice and evidence: Submit a fictional town-rule proposal with calculation, diagram and reflection. Rubric: reasoning 6, fairness 6, evidence 4, revision 4.
