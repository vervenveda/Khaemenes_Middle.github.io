Week 16 · Integrated Research Projects

Forces, Motion, and Systems

How do forces and systems explain motion?

Monday: Define a transport challenge

Focus: Define a transport challenge.

Learn: A fictional community compares two travel routes without treating simulation as a real municipal survey.

Practice and evidence: Draw a fictional route map and define two testable travel-time questions.

Tuesday: Compare journeys

Focus: Compare journeys.

Learn: Route A covers 12 km in 3 hours for 4 km/h; Route B covers 12 km in 2 hours for 6 km/h.

Practice and evidence: Compute both average speeds and create a labeled comparison table.

Wednesday: Graph patterns

Focus: Graph patterns.

Learn: A distance-time line depicts modeled progress while variations can reflect pauses or changes in speed.

Practice and evidence: Plot points (0,0), (1,4), (2,8), (3,12) and explain its constant modeled rate.

Thursday: Evaluate access

Focus: Evaluate access.

Learn: The fastest trip does not always offer the safest or most inclusive route. Plans need information on cost, access and actual conditions.

Practice and evidence: Create a decision matrix with three missing real-world facts.

Friday: Publish a transport report

Focus: Publish a transport report.

Learn: A balanced proposal uses measured quantities, a readable map and justified caution about simulated evidence.

Practice and evidence: Submit a fictional transit design brief and revised recommendation. Rubric: math 6, graphics 5, community needs 5, limits 4.
