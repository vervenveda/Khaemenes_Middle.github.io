Week 33 · Integrated Research Projects

Technology, Data, and Ethical Creation

How can tools strengthen evidence without replacing judgment?

Monday: Choose a measurable problem

Focus: Choose a measurable problem.

Learn: A school data-tool proposal needs clear users, constraints and fictional non-sensitive data.

Practice and evidence: Define a fictional classroom supply-count tool.

Tuesday: Translate steps into expressions

Focus: Translate steps into expressions.

Learn: An algebraic model uses variables for counts and constants for fixed rules.

Practice and evidence: Write and evaluate 3x+2 for x=4 sample items.

Wednesday: Compare model outputs

Focus: Compare model outputs.

Learn: A tool should be tested against independently calculated expected values.

Practice and evidence: Prepare three input-output cases and spot a deliberate error.

Thursday: Consider bias and access

Focus: Consider bias and access.

Learn: A model must not infer personal identity or capability from classroom activity counts.

Practice and evidence: Add inclusive design rules and transparent data assumptions to a prototype.

Friday: Present a verified proposal

Focus: Present a verified proposal.

Learn: A responsible capstone combines calculation, testing, sources and limitations.

Practice and evidence: Submit a one-page tool proposal, expression tests and revision. Rubric: math 6, accuracy 6, usability 4, ethics 4.
