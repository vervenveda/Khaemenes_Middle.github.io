Week 24 · Language Arts & Literacy

Ancient River Civilizations

How did rivers support early civilizations and complex societies?

Monday: Read a river-civilization source

Focus: Read a river-civilization source.

Learn: An informational account about early river settlements should identify its time, place, author and evidence. Fertile floodplains could support farming, but individual communities differed.

Practice and evidence: Annotate a short original overview of the Nile and Tigris-Euphrates regions, marking observations versus historical inferences.

Tuesday: Explain geography vocabulary

Focus: Explain geography vocabulary.

Learn: Irrigation means supplying water to fields through planned methods; floodplain refers to land prone to flooding. A source describing agriculture needs precise terms.

Practice and evidence: Write definitions of irrigation, silt, settlement, artifact and primary source, with context sentences.

Wednesday: Compare two river settings

Focus: Compare two river settings.

Learn: Ancient Egyptian settlements along the Nile and Mesopotamian settlements around the Tigris and Euphrates developed in different political and environmental contexts.

Practice and evidence: Make a similarities-and-differences table, marking any unsupported claim for verification.

Thursday: Evaluate source limits

Focus: Evaluate source limits.

Learn: A surviving inscription reflects its creator's perspective and should not automatically represent every person. Archaeological remains offer different evidence.

Practice and evidence: Write an evidence paragraph distinguishing an imagined inscription from a real, citable primary source.

Friday: Write a sourced comparison

Focus: Write a sourced comparison.

Learn: An organized comparison introduces both places, uses evidence fairly and identifies uncertainty where a source cannot support a claim.

Practice and evidence: Submit a 180-word comparison of two ancient river regions, a source checklist and revision. Rubric: context 6, evidence 6, organization 4, revision 4.
