Week 36 · Language Arts & Literacy

Final Review and Grade 7 Readiness

How am I ready for seventh grade?

Monday: Review source-based reading

Focus: Review source-based reading.

Learn: Strong readers distinguish a text's claim, perspective, supporting evidence and context.

Practice and evidence: Compare two fictional school-community accounts and cite their differences.

Tuesday: Revisit argument writing

Focus: Revisit argument writing.

Learn: Evidence-based writing needs a bounded thesis, organized paragraphs and honest citations.

Practice and evidence: Revise a capstone paragraph with a claim, evidence and fair counterpoint.

Wednesday: Practice attentive speaking and listening

Focus: Practice attentive speaking and listening.

Learn: A thoughtful listener summarizes another view before responding.

Practice and evidence: Complete a scripted discussion and record two precise listening notes.

Thursday: Build a literacy portfolio

Focus: Build a literacy portfolio.

Learn: A portfolio should show growth through original samples and specific feedback.

Practice and evidence: Select two invented writing examples and identify revision evidence.

Friday: Defend readiness

Focus: Defend readiness.

Learn: Grade 7 preparation means explaining a skill and naming a next learning goal.

Practice and evidence: Present a sourced 180-word reflection with one strength and one growth target. Rubric: evidence 6, reasoning 6, clarity 4, reflection 4.
