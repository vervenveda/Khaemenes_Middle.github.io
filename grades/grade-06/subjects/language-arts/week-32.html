Week 32 · Language Arts & Literacy

Health, Identity, and Responsible Choices

How do healthy routines support learning and identity?

Monday: Analyze wellness claims

Focus: Analyze wellness claims.

Learn: Claims about health routines need evidence and should avoid presenting one experience as universal.

Practice and evidence: Annotate two invented wellness messages, identifying assertion, source and missing context.

Tuesday: Distinguish persuasion from evidence

Focus: Distinguish persuasion from evidence.

Learn: Emotional language can attract attention without establishing a claim's truth.

Practice and evidence: Compare the evidence and word choice in two fictional school notices.

Wednesday: Write inclusive informational text

Focus: Write inclusive informational text.

Learn: Clear language offers practical choices without blaming individual students.

Practice and evidence: Draft a privacy-respecting guide to requesting help with school routines.

Thursday: Revise for audience needs

Focus: Revise for audience needs.

Learn: Accessible writing includes short instructions, understandable terms and alternative participation methods.

Practice and evidence: Peer-review an invented digital-wellness notice for clarity and respectful tone.

Friday: Publish an evidence-led guide

Focus: Publish an evidence-led guide.

Learn: A guide should cite checked information, identify limitations and welcome diverse needs.

Practice and evidence: Submit a 180-word fictional school wellness guide with source notes and revision. Rubric: evidence 6, accuracy 6, access 4, reflection 4.
