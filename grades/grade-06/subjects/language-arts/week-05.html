Week 05 · Language Arts & Literacy

Fraction Division and Procedural Writing

How can models explain division when the numbers are fractions?

Monday: Identify a procedural text

Focus: Identify a procedural text.

Learn: A procedure tells a reader how to complete a task in a reproducible sequence. Good steps identify materials, units, order, and a test of success. For example, describe how to label a ratio table before calculating its values.

Practice and evidence: Read a fictional set of directions for making a fraction-strip model. Identify its purpose, materials, sequence and one missing detail.

Tuesday: Transitions and precision

Focus: Transitions and precision.

Learn: Words such as first, next, after, and finally signal the order of instructions. A vague command like 'measure some paper' should specify a measurement and unit.

Practice and evidence: Rewrite five vague model-building directions into an ordered list using exact measurements and transitions.

Wednesday: Explain a fraction model

Focus: Explain a fraction model.

Learn: Dividing 3/4 by 1/4 asks how many quarter-units fit into three quarters. A paper strip partitioned into fourths demonstrates three groups, making the quotient 3.

Practice and evidence: Write a step-by-step explanation for a paper strip diagram of 3/4 ÷ 1/4 = 3, including an evidence check.

Thursday: Peer-test a procedure

Focus: Peer-test a procedure.

Learn: Readers reveal unclear directions when they independently try the steps. A revision should record what failed rather than silently changing the original.

Practice and evidence: Ask a peer or adult to follow your fictional fraction-strip procedure, or self-test using a checklist. Record two ambiguities and repairs.

Friday: Final procedural guide

Focus: Final procedural guide.

Learn: An accurate process guide includes a title, numbered steps, materials, units, diagrams where useful, and a verification step.

Practice and evidence: Prepare a one-page original procedure for representing 2/3 ÷ 1/6, correctly obtaining 4. Rubric 20: sequence 5, mathematical clarity 5, specificity 5, revision 5.
