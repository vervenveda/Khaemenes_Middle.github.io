Week 21 · Language Arts & Literacy

Ecosystems and Energy Flow

How do energy and matter move through ecosystems?

Monday: Explain an ecosystem

Focus: Explain an ecosystem.

Learn: An ecosystem includes living organisms and nonliving environmental components that interact. An explanatory paragraph should define producers, consumers, and decomposers before interpreting their relationships.

Practice and evidence: Write a five-term glossary and an original paragraph describing a fictional pond ecosystem.

Tuesday: Read a food-web diagram

Focus: Read a food-web diagram.

Learn: Arrows in a typical food web point from a food source to the organism obtaining energy from it. A model simplifies complex feeding relationships.

Practice and evidence: Caption a fictional grass-rabbit-fox sequence and identify one limitation.

Wednesday: Distinguish energy and matter

Focus: Distinguish energy and matter.

Learn: Energy flows through feeding relationships while matter cycles through living things and the environment. Conflating both makes a scientific explanation misleading.

Practice and evidence: Revise a fictional paragraph that incorrectly says energy is recycled indefinitely.

Thursday: Assess environmental claims

Focus: Assess environmental claims.

Learn: A change in one population can influence others, but the size and direction of real effects require evidence. A single fictional food web cannot establish ecosystem-wide outcomes.

Practice and evidence: Create a claim-evidence-reasoning organizer for a simulated habitat-change scenario.

Friday: Publish an environmental argument

Focus: Publish an environmental argument.

Learn: A sound argument names its ecosystem, references appropriate data, recognizes alternative explanations and avoids unwarranted certainty.

Practice and evidence: Submit a 160-word evidence-based conservation argument and diagram. Rubric: science 6, evidence 6, clarity 4, revision 4.
