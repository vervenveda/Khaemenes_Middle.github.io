Week 22 · Language Arts & Literacy

Earth Systems and Water

How do Earth systems interact and affect human life?

Monday: Explain water as a system

Focus: Explain water as a system.

Learn: The water cycle involves evaporation, condensation, precipitation and movement or storage of water. An explanatory paragraph should not imply that water always follows one fixed circular path.

Practice and evidence: Write a glossary of five water-cycle terms and a qualified introductory paragraph.

Tuesday: Sequence a water-cycle account

Focus: Sequence a water-cycle account.

Learn: Solar energy can drive evaporation, and cooling can cause condensation; precipitation returns water to Earth's surface. Water also moves underground and through organisms.

Practice and evidence: Write a numbered account of a fictional water droplet while explaining where the story simplifies real processes.

Wednesday: Read an Earth-system diagram

Focus: Read an Earth-system diagram.

Learn: A hydrosphere-atmosphere diagram can represent water movement, but arrows alone do not measure how much water moved.

Practice and evidence: Create a text caption for a fictional labeled map showing clouds, a river, soil and groundwater.

Thursday: Evaluate cause-and-effect claims

Focus: Evaluate cause-and-effect claims.

Learn: Rainfall affects river levels alongside soil, land cover and drainage. A single invented observation cannot establish the full cause of flooding.

Practice and evidence: Revise a misleading fictional news paragraph, replacing absolute claims with evidence-based qualifications.

Friday: Publish a water-systems explanation

Focus: Publish a water-systems explanation.

Learn: A strong explanation connects diagrams, sequence, mechanisms, source honesty and a clear limitation statement.

Practice and evidence: Submit a 160-word water-cycle explanation and accessible diagram description. Rubric: science 6, evidence 6, clarity 4, revision 4.
