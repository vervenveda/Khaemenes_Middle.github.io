Week 03 · Language Arts & Literacy

Rates, Unit Rates, and Informational Text

How can rates and text structures explain change?

Monday: Identify informational text

Focus: Identify informational text.

Learn: Informational texts explain facts and ideas. Original passage: 'A neighborhood cycling club recorded distance and time. On Monday it traveled 12 kilometers in 3 hours. On Tuesday it traveled 15 kilometers in 3 hours. Members compared rates rather than distance alone.'

Practice and evidence: Underline the main idea and two numerical details. Define distance, duration, and rate using context.

Tuesday: Use text structure

Focus: Use text structure.

Learn: A comparison text places evidence side by side. A cause-and-effect text describes why an event leads to a consequence. In the cycling passage, Monday and Tuesday are compared, but no cause of the difference has been established.

Practice and evidence: Make a comparison chart with the two trips. Identify an unsupported causal claim and explain why the passage cannot prove it.

Wednesday: Take accurate notes

Focus: Take accurate notes.

Learn: Notes should record essential facts without copying entire paragraphs. A Cornell-style page can place questions on the left and evidence on the right. Precise figures keep a summary trustworthy.

Practice and evidence: Prepare a two-column note sheet: What was measured? What do the values show? Include 12 km/3 h and 15 km/3 h.

Thursday: Explain technical vocabulary

Focus: Explain technical vocabulary.

Learn: A unit rate expresses a quantity for one unit of another. Monday: 12÷3 = 4 km/h; Tuesday: 15÷3 = 5 km/h. Technical explanations define terms and include units.

Practice and evidence: Write a 90-word explanation comparing the club's rates; cite the original passage and distinguish numerical evidence from speculation.

Friday: Synthesize and revise

Focus: Synthesize and revise.

Learn: An evidence paragraph introduces a claim, includes details and reasoning, and acknowledges what remains unknown.

Practice and evidence: Write 150 words answering which day had the greater average rate. Add a note on a missing variable and revise for exact numbers and attribution. Rubric: claim 4, accurate evidence 6, explanation 6, revision 4.
