Week 27 · Language Arts & Literacy

Trade Routes, Migration, and Exchange

How do movement and exchange transform societies?

Monday: Read competing travel accounts

Focus: Read competing travel accounts.

Learn: Travel narratives record selected experiences, not every migrant's viewpoint.

Practice and evidence: Annotate two fictional accounts of one market journey, identifying audience, perspective and omission.

Tuesday: Identify source reliability

Focus: Identify source reliability.

Learn: A diary, trade record and modern summary answer different questions.

Practice and evidence: Compare claims from three invented documents and flag uncertain details.

Wednesday: Practice respectful interviews

Focus: Practice respectful interviews.

Learn: Open questions invite detail without assuming a person's origins or experiences.

Practice and evidence: Draft six nonintrusive interview questions about a fictional relocation.

Thursday: Synthesize divergent voices

Focus: Synthesize divergent voices.

Learn: Responsible synthesis preserves differences instead of turning multiple perspectives into one voice.

Practice and evidence: Build a claim-and-evidence chart from two conflicting fictional testimonies.

Friday: Present an evidence-led account

Focus: Present an evidence-led account.

Learn: Presentations should separate documented details, inference and imaginative reconstruction.

Practice and evidence: Deliver a 180-word comparative report with citations, two viewpoints and a revision note. Rubric: evidence 7, context 5, clarity 4, ethics 4.
