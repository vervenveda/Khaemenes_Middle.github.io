Week 34 · Language Arts & Literacy

A++ Big Question Capstone Research

What serious question can I investigate with evidence, calculation, and design?

Monday: Define a big research question

Focus: Define a big research question.

Learn: A capstone question should be specific, consequential and answerable from available evidence.

Practice and evidence: Narrow an invented broad school-resource issue to a bounded research question.

Tuesday: Create an annotated source log

Focus: Create an annotated source log.

Learn: Record author, date, context and relevance; unknown details must remain unknown.

Practice and evidence: Annotate two fictional source cards and identify one evidence gap.

Wednesday: Plan an argument

Focus: Plan an argument.

Learn: A thesis should be defensible and revised if evidence conflicts.

Practice and evidence: Draft a thesis, two reasons and a fair counterclaim.

Thursday: Write a coherent proposal

Focus: Write a coherent proposal.

Learn: Transitions, citation and plain language help audiences test a proposal.

Practice and evidence: Compose a 220-word mini-proposal with marked evidence and limitations.

Friday: Present and revise

Focus: Present and revise.

Learn: A complete research plan records feedback and changes, not just a final claim.

Practice and evidence: Submit question, thesis, annotated bibliography and revision. Rubric: question 5, evidence 7, reasoning 4, clarity 4.
