Week 28 · Language Arts & Literacy

World Languages and Cultural Communication

How does language carry culture and connection?

Monday: Distinguish translation from interpretation

Focus: Distinguish translation from interpretation.

Learn: A translation conveys meaning through choices shaped by audience, context and language structure.

Practice and evidence: Compare two fictional translations of a greeting and annotate differences in formality and tone.

Tuesday: Read multilingual narratives closely

Focus: Read multilingual narratives closely.

Learn: A narrator's word choices and perspective affect how cultural experiences are represented.

Practice and evidence: Annotate a fictional bilingual community notice and identify two claims and supporting details.

Wednesday: Evaluate cognates responsibly

Focus: Evaluate cognates responsibly.

Learn: Words that look similar across languages may share origins, meanings, or misleading resemblances.

Practice and evidence: Make a chart with three suggested cognates and verify each using classroom references.

Thursday: Revise for an audience

Focus: Revise for an audience.

Learn: Plain wording, context and accurate interpretation make a message more useful to multilingual readers.

Practice and evidence: Revise an invented school notice for clarity and identify where a translator's judgment is needed.

Friday: Present evidence-based language analysis

Focus: Present evidence-based language analysis.

Learn: Responsible analysis distinguishes direct textual support from assumptions about speakers.

Practice and evidence: Submit a 180-word comparison with cited details, audience analysis and revision. Rubric: evidence 7, reasoning 5, clarity 4, reflection 4.
