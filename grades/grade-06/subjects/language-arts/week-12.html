Week 12 · Language Arts & Literacy

Geometry: Area, Surface Area, and Design

How do measurements help designers plan efficient spaces?

Monday: Use measurement vocabulary

Focus: Use measurement vocabulary.

Learn: Length measures one dimension in units such as centimeters; area describes a two-dimensional region in square units, while surface area totals exposed faces of a three-dimensional object.

Practice and evidence: Write a five-entry glossary and distinguish cm from cm² in three clear sentences.

Tuesday: Describe a composite design

Focus: Describe a composite design.

Learn: A rectangular reading mat measuring 4 m by 3 m has area 12 m². An explanatory description states dimensions, units and the formula used.

Practice and evidence: Draft a paragraph describing an original fictional reading-mat design with dimensions and area.

Wednesday: Explain procedures sequentially

Focus: Explain procedures sequentially.

Learn: A rectangle's area is length times width, whereas a triangle's area is one-half its base times perpendicular height. Steps must specify which measurement represents height.

Practice and evidence: Write ordered instructions for finding the area of a triangle with base 6 cm and height 4 cm, including the 12 cm² result.

Thursday: Explain a box net

Focus: Explain a box net.

Learn: A net shows the flat faces of a solid. A closed cube of side 2 cm has six square faces, each area 4 cm², so total surface area is 24 cm².

Practice and evidence: Annotate a six-square cube net, naming each face, and check the total without confusing area with volume.

Friday: Defend a design in writing

Focus: Defend a design in writing.

Learn: Technical writing combines a labeled diagram, consistent square units, accurate sequence and rationale for choices.

Practice and evidence: Submit an original measured design and a 150-word how-to explanation with one corrected ambiguity. Rubric: accuracy 6, sequencing 5, vocabulary 5, revision 4.
