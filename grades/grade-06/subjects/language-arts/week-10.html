Week 10 · Language Arts & Literacy

Inequalities and Decision Rules

How do constraints shape solutions and decisions?

Monday: Read conditional language

Focus: Read conditional language.

Learn: A condition states when a rule applies. 'If a room holds no more than 20 students, entry stops at 20' defines a maximum; 'at least 20' defines a minimum. Precision matters.

Practice and evidence: Write four original sentences using if, unless, at least, and no more than, and explain the mathematical meaning of each.

Tuesday: Distinguish strict and inclusive limits

Focus: Distinguish strict and inclusive limits.

Learn: Fewer than 12 means x<12, while 12 or fewer means x≤12. A single word changes whether the boundary value qualifies.

Practice and evidence: Annotate six fictional club rules and rewrite them as unambiguous statements with example qualifying and nonqualifying values.

Wednesday: Evaluate ambiguous rules

Focus: Evaluate ambiguous rules.

Learn: 'Participants should arrive early' gives no measurable threshold. A precise rule might specify arrival before 9:00, while exceptions and accessibility considerations still need explanation.

Practice and evidence: Revise an invented school event notice to clarify start time, capacity, and accommodation requests without inventing legal requirements.

Thursday: Explain an inequality in prose

Focus: Explain an inequality in prose.

Learn: A sentence can be translated into symbols and back. 'The paper budget b is at most 15 units' means b≤15, including b=15.

Practice and evidence: Make a three-column chart of five sample constraints: words, inequality, and example that tests the boundary.

Friday: Procedural argument with qualifications

Focus: Procedural argument with qualifications.

Learn: Good policies use precise thresholds, explain their purposes, acknowledge exceptions and identify who decides.

Practice and evidence: Write a 150-word fictional library room-capacity policy including one inequality, a worked boundary example and a fair revision. Rubric: wording 6, symbols 5, fairness 5, editing 4.
