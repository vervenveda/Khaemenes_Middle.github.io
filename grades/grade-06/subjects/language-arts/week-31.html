Week 31 · Language Arts & Literacy

Arts, Media, and Message Design

How do artists and media makers shape meaning?

Monday: Identify point of view

Focus: Identify point of view.

Learn: Every media text has a creator, audience and purpose; point of view affects the story told.

Practice and evidence: Annotate an invented public-service poster, marking its message and omitted viewpoint.

Tuesday: Analyze rhetorical choices

Focus: Analyze rhetorical choices.

Learn: Word order, imagery and repetition influence how audiences interpret a message.

Practice and evidence: Compare two fictional headlines and explain how emphasis changes the implied message.

Wednesday: Verify captions

Focus: Verify captions.

Learn: Captions can provide necessary context or introduce misleading claims.

Practice and evidence: Rewrite an invented image caption to distinguish visible evidence from inference.

Thursday: Draft a media critique

Focus: Draft a media critique.

Learn: A strong critique identifies a claim, supports interpretations and acknowledges alternative readings.

Practice and evidence: Create a claim-evidence-reasoning chart from two fictional public messages.

Friday: Publish an evidence-based review

Focus: Publish an evidence-based review.

Learn: A credible media review separates quoted material, inference and personal response.

Practice and evidence: Submit a 180-word comparison with two specific examples, alternative interpretation and revision. Rubric: context 5, evidence 7, reasoning 4, reflection 4.
