Week 18 · Language Arts & Literacy

Waves, Sound, and Communication

How do waves carry energy and information?

Monday: Model a communication pathway

Focus: Model a communication pathway.

Learn: A message can travel from sender through a channel to a receiver; noise or missing context may affect understanding. This is a communication model, not a rule that every message is understood.

Practice and evidence: Draw a sender-channel-receiver diagram for an original fictional school announcement.

Tuesday: Explain sound vocabulary

Focus: Explain sound vocabulary.

Learn: Sound waves are vibrations passing through a medium; pitch relates to frequency, while loudness is related to amplitude and other factors.

Practice and evidence: Write a five-term glossary including frequency, amplitude, vibration, channel and feedback.

Wednesday: Interpret a media message

Focus: Interpret a media message.

Learn: A credible announcement should identify its sender, intended audience, evidence and purpose. A repeated message is not necessarily accurate.

Practice and evidence: Annotate a fictional community alert and identify two missing verification details.

Thursday: Repair misunderstanding

Focus: Repair misunderstanding.

Learn: Communication breakdowns can be addressed with respectful clarification, accessible formats and a check that the receiver understood.

Practice and evidence: Rewrite an ambiguous announcement and give an alternate plain-text version.

Friday: Publish a communication brief

Focus: Publish a communication brief.

Learn: A strong technical explanation combines a wave-model caption, qualified science vocabulary and a clear discussion of communication limits.

Practice and evidence: Submit a 160-word explanation, revised alert and model diagram. Rubric: science 5, evidence 5, clarity 6, revision 4.
