Week 35 · Language Arts & Literacy

Capstone Studio and Oral Defense

How can I make my evidence clear enough to defend publicly?

Monday: Revise the research thesis

Focus: Revise the research thesis.

Learn: A strong capstone defense states a bounded claim and explains why the evidence supports it.

Practice and evidence: Mark thesis, two citations, and an uncertainty in an invented proposal.

Tuesday: Organize the speech

Focus: Organize the speech.

Learn: An introduction, evidence sequence and conclusion help an audience evaluate reasoning.

Practice and evidence: Outline a two-minute explanation with clear transitions.

Wednesday: Anticipate counterquestions

Focus: Anticipate counterquestions.

Learn: Thoughtful questions test assumptions rather than attack the presenter.

Practice and evidence: Prepare three questions and evidence-based answers for a fictional defense.

Thursday: Practice clear delivery

Focus: Practice clear delivery.

Learn: Pacing, readable notes and equivalent written options support accessible presentations.

Practice and evidence: Rehearse and revise a brief speech using a peer-review checklist.

Friday: Defend and reflect

Focus: Defend and reflect.

Learn: Effective defense acknowledges limits, credits sources and documents revisions.

Practice and evidence: Submit a speech transcript, cited sources and Q&A reflection. Rubric: evidence 7, reasoning 5, clarity 4, response 4.
