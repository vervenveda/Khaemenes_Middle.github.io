Week 21 · Science & Engineering

Ecosystems and Energy Flow

How do energy and matter move through ecosystems?

Monday: Construct a food chain

Focus: Construct a food chain.

Learn: Producers such as grasses capture energy from sunlight; consumers obtain energy from food. Decomposers break down organic material and help return nutrients to ecosystems.

Practice and evidence: Draw a fictional grass-rabbit-fox chain with correctly oriented energy-transfer arrows.

Tuesday: Model a food web

Focus: Model a food web.

Learn: A food web connects multiple feeding relationships. A single species may have different prey or predators, and diagrams rarely capture every interaction.

Practice and evidence: Expand the fictional chain with a second consumer and annotate two omitted complexities.

Wednesday: Separate energy from matter

Focus: Separate energy from matter.

Learn: Energy entering ecosystems, often through sunlight, flows among organisms and dissipates as heat, while matter can cycle through the system.

Practice and evidence: Make a two-column explanation contrasting energy flow with nutrient cycling.

Thursday: Investigate limiting factors

Focus: Investigate limiting factors.

Learn: Water, light, nutrients, space and interactions can limit populations. A hypothetical decline does not automatically demonstrate which factor caused it.

Practice and evidence: Interpret a simulated producer count of 40,35,20 over three seasons and state two alternative hypotheses.

Friday: Present an ecosystem CER

Focus: Present an ecosystem CER.

Learn: A scientific explanation should tie a specific claim to observations or a labeled simulation, describe uncertainty and avoid assuming a diagram is a field survey.

Practice and evidence: Submit food web, population graph, and CER response. Rubric: model 6, data 5, reasoning 5, limitations 4.
