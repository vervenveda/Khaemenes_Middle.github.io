Week 33 · Science & Engineering

Technology, Data, and Ethical Creation

How can tools strengthen evidence without replacing judgment?

Monday: Model data collection

Focus: Model data collection.

Learn: Observations become more useful when variables, units and procedures are specified.

Practice and evidence: Plan a nonmedical classroom seed-growth observation using fictional sample data.

Tuesday: Separate data from inference

Focus: Separate data from inference.

Learn: A chart summarizes measurements, but a pattern does not alone establish its cause.

Practice and evidence: Identify an unsupported causal claim in a fictional growth chart.

Wednesday: Inspect sampling bias

Focus: Inspect sampling bias.

Learn: A small or selectively chosen sample can distort a conclusion.

Practice and evidence: Compare two fictional plant samples and note how selection affects claims.

Thursday: Check model predictions

Focus: Check model predictions.

Learn: Predictions should be compared with observations and errors recorded honestly.

Practice and evidence: Calculate a simple model's deviation from three simulated measurements.

Friday: Report reproducible findings

Focus: Report reproducible findings.

Learn: Scientific communication needs methods, labeled data, uncertainty and responsible limits.

Practice and evidence: Submit a chart, method and claim-evidence-reasoning report. Rubric: method 6, evidence 6, reasoning 5, limits 3.
