Week 07 · Science & Engineering

Coordinate Planes and Geography

How can coordinates help us describe place, movement, and patterns?

Monday: Record spatial observations

Focus: Record spatial observations.

Learn: Scientists may plot observations on a grid to describe patterns. A point at (−2,1) can represent a sample location, but its x and y axes must have stated units and origin.

Practice and evidence: Create a fictional sampling grid with three observations at (−2,1), (0,2), (3,−1), labeling all axes and data as simulated.

Tuesday: Plot and compare sites

Focus: Plot and compare sites.

Learn: Observations collected from different places can be tabulated alongside coordinates. A greater count at one location does not prove why the difference exists.

Practice and evidence: Assign simulated counts of 4, 7 and 2 to three sampling sites. Make a table with x, y and count and explain the highest recorded count.

Wednesday: Describe spatial patterns

Focus: Describe spatial patterns.

Learn: A cluster consists of nearby locations in the defined map scale. A visual grouping can be descriptive evidence, but needs enough observations for reliable interpretation.

Practice and evidence: Plot five invented points and circle two that are near one another. State one observation and a plausible but unproven inference.

Thursday: Evaluate coordinate precision

Focus: Evaluate coordinate precision.

Learn: Grid spacing and point placement affect accuracy. Without a scale, a gap of two squares cannot be assumed to mean two meters.

Practice and evidence: Propose a 1-square-to-2-meter scale and explain how measurement precision limits your interpretation.

Friday: Spatial evidence report

Focus: Spatial evidence report.

Learn: A scientific explanation cites plotted observations, distinguishes data and inference, and identifies what further sampling would help.

Practice and evidence: Submit a simulated sampling map, table, and CER paragraph describing the distribution and two limitations. Rubric: plotting 6, units 5, interpretation 5, caution 4.
