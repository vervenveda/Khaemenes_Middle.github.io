Week 31 · Science & Engineering

Arts, Media, and Message Design

How do artists and media makers shape meaning?

Monday: Model sound waves

Focus: Model sound waves.

Learn: Vibrating sources cause sound waves; loudness and pitch are different characteristics.

Practice and evidence: Draw a sound-wave model with frequency and amplitude labels.

Tuesday: Investigate light and color

Focus: Investigate light and color.

Learn: Pigments absorb and reflect different wavelengths; screen colors use emitted light.

Practice and evidence: Compare hypothetical painted and digital color-mixing examples, explaining why their processes differ.

Wednesday: Plan a fair observation

Focus: Plan a fair observation.

Learn: Scientific comparisons use consistent conditions and account for measurement limitations.

Practice and evidence: Design a safe, non-flashing color-contrast observation with fixed lighting conditions.

Thursday: Challenge unsupported claims

Focus: Challenge unsupported claims.

Learn: Media demonstrations are not reliable scientific evidence unless methods and measures are clear.

Practice and evidence: Identify the missing controls in a fictional sound-amplification advertisement.

Friday: Explain a media-science finding

Focus: Explain a media-science finding.

Learn: Scientific explanation must distinguish measured outcomes from artistic preferences.

Practice and evidence: Submit a diagram, observation table and claim-evidence-reasoning report. Rubric: model 6, method 5, evidence 5, limits 4.
