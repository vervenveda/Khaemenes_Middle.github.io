Week 25 · Science & Engineering

Belief Systems, Law, and Culture

How do beliefs and laws shape community life?

Monday: Separate empirical from cultural claims

Focus: Separate empirical from cultural claims.

Learn: Science investigates testable natural phenomena; beliefs and cultural meanings can be studied respectfully as historical or social evidence without labeling them scientific findings.

Practice and evidence: Sort six fictional statements into measurable environmental conditions, cultural interpretations or mixed claims.

Tuesday: Observe materials

Focus: Observe materials.

Learn: A settlement's building choices may respond to rainfall, temperature and locally available materials, but a hypothetical example does not establish historical cause.

Practice and evidence: Construct a fictional two-material durability comparison and label untested assumptions.

Wednesday: Plan fair comparisons

Focus: Plan fair comparisons.

Learn: If comparing model roof materials, change one material at a time while holding size and simulated rainfall constant.

Practice and evidence: Draft a paper-only fair-test table listing independent, dependent and controlled variables.

Thursday: Interpret limited evidence

Focus: Interpret limited evidence.

Learn: A model material retaining 8 of 10 simulated water units differs from one retaining 4; no claim about actual ancient construction follows automatically.

Practice and evidence: Calculate the quantities and list two explanations needing testing.

Friday: Explain evidence boundaries

Focus: Explain evidence boundaries.

Learn: A responsible report describes the observed or simulated property, its measurement assumptions and the limits of inference about cultural practices.

Practice and evidence: Submit a fictional experiment plan and CER paragraph. Rubric: method 6, data 5, reasoning 5, limitations 4.
