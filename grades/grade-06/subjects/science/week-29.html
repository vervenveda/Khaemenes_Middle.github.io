Week 29 · Science & Engineering

Research Writing: From Question to Thesis

How do researchers build trustworthy explanations?

Monday: Ask a testable question

Focus: Ask a testable question.

Learn: Science questions identify measurable variables and distinguish observation from personal preference.

Practice and evidence: Write a question comparing cooling rates of identical safe room-temperature containers.

Tuesday: Plan the investigation

Focus: Plan the investigation.

Learn: A reproducible procedure identifies variables, controls, repeated trials and safety limits.

Practice and evidence: Create a three-trial temperature-observation protocol without heating or unsafe substances.

Wednesday: Document empirical results

Focus: Document empirical results.

Learn: Record measurements consistently; invented values must be labeled simulated.

Practice and evidence: Prepare a measurement table with units, timestamps and source or instrument details.

Thursday: Interpret uncertainty

Focus: Interpret uncertainty.

Learn: A trend in a small sample does not establish every possible result or a historical scientific claim.

Practice and evidence: Graph example data and describe one alternative explanation and two limitations.

Friday: Report evidence faithfully

Focus: Report evidence faithfully.

Learn: A report separates the method, measurements, inference and unanswered questions.

Practice and evidence: Submit a one-page research report with question, method, table and conclusion. Rubric: method 6, records 6, reasoning 5, limitations 3.
