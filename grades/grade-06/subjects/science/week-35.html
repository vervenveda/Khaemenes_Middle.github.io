Week 35 · Science & Engineering

Capstone Studio and Oral Defense

How can I make my evidence clear enough to defend publicly?

Monday: Choose the strongest claim

Focus: Choose the strongest claim.

Learn: A scientific defense relies on observed data and explicit model assumptions.

Practice and evidence: Select a simulated experiment and distinguish measurements from predictions.

Tuesday: Document methods

Focus: Document methods.

Learn: Controls, repeatable steps and units allow another researcher to evaluate a finding.

Practice and evidence: Prepare a method card and three-trial table.

Wednesday: Explain limitations

Focus: Explain limitations.

Learn: Scientific uncertainty belongs in a conclusion rather than being concealed.

Practice and evidence: Identify two uncontrolled variables in a fictional model.

Thursday: Answer evidence questions

Focus: Answer evidence questions.

Learn: Researchers may revise explanations when justified objections arise.

Practice and evidence: Role-play questions about data quality and show one possible correction.

Friday: Deliver a scientific defense

Focus: Deliver a scientific defense.

Learn: A model should be defended without exaggerating its real-world reach.

Practice and evidence: Present a graph, claim-evidence-reasoning summary and limitations. Rubric: method 5, evidence 7, logic 4, limits 4.
