Week 36 · Science & Engineering

Final Review and Grade 7 Readiness

How am I ready for seventh grade?

Monday: Review scientific models

Focus: Review scientific models.

Learn: Models explain selected features of systems and have limits.

Practice and evidence: Compare a simple weather model and a safe classroom material experiment.

Tuesday: Audit data collection

Focus: Audit data collection.

Learn: Reliable scientific records include methods, units, controls and uncertainty.

Practice and evidence: Repair a fictional table with missing units and an uncontrolled variable.

Wednesday: Explain ecosystems and Earth systems

Focus: Explain ecosystems and Earth systems.

Learn: Systems involve interactions; a single observation rarely establishes causation.

Practice and evidence: Build a labeled interaction diagram and describe one limitation.

Thursday: Practice claim-evidence-reasoning

Focus: Practice claim-evidence-reasoning.

Learn: A conclusion must match the investigation rather than exaggerate its reach.

Practice and evidence: Write a bounded explanation of simulated results and one follow-up test.

Friday: Prepare Grade 7 inquiry

Focus: Prepare Grade 7 inquiry.

Learn: Scientific readiness includes safe planning and honest interpretation.

Practice and evidence: Submit a methods card, graph, diagram and reflection. Rubric: method 6, evidence 6, reasoning 5, limits 3.
