Week 17 · Science & Engineering

Midyear Portfolio and Assessment

How can I prove growth across every subject?

Monday: Review scientific reasoning

Focus: Review scientific reasoning.

Learn: Midyear science work should distinguish observations, models and explanations. A diagram of particles is a model rather than a microscopic photograph.

Practice and evidence: Select an earlier model and annotate two strengths and one limitation.

Tuesday: Revisit matter and density

Focus: Revisit matter and density.

Learn: For a fictional sample with mass 12 g and volume 4 cm³, density is 3 g/cm³; a number without units cannot stand as complete scientific evidence.

Practice and evidence: Recalculate two prior simulated density examples and check the units.

Wednesday: Revisit thermal and force models

Focus: Revisit thermal and force models.

Learn: A heating plateau does not automatically prove a phase change, and average speed alone does not establish applied force.

Practice and evidence: Correct two fictional overclaims using evidence-versus-inference language.

Thursday: Practice cumulative CER

Focus: Practice cumulative CER.

Learn: A claim-evidence-reasoning explanation should use appropriate observations, calculations and context and state the limits of a model.

Practice and evidence: Write a CER response comparing simulated motion data for 12 meters in 4 seconds with an alternate pattern.

Friday: Certify science portfolio evidence

Focus: Certify science portfolio evidence.

Learn: A thorough midyear science portfolio includes data table, model, calculation, CER, date, source honesty and a reflection.

Practice and evidence: Submit three revised artifacts with a 20-point rubric: evidence 6, reasoning 6, models 4, reflection 4.
