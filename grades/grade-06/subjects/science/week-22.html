Week 22 · Science & Engineering

Earth Systems and Water

How do Earth systems interact and affect human life?

Monday: Identify Earth's interacting spheres

Focus: Identify Earth's interacting spheres.

Learn: The hydrosphere includes Earth's water; the atmosphere contains gases; the geosphere includes rock and soil; the biosphere comprises living systems. These categories interact.

Practice and evidence: Draw a fictional watershed cross-section labeling all four spheres.

Tuesday: Explain phase changes in the water cycle

Focus: Explain phase changes in the water cycle.

Learn: Evaporation transfers liquid water to vapor, while condensation turns water vapor into liquid droplets under suitable conditions. Water vapor itself is invisible.

Practice and evidence: Label evaporation, condensation and precipitation on a simplified model; explain one model limitation.

Wednesday: Trace surface and groundwater

Focus: Trace surface and groundwater.

Learn: Some precipitation runs off into streams; some infiltrates soil and may recharge groundwater. Local geology, vegetation and weather affect these paths.

Practice and evidence: Create two plausible arrow paths on a fictional watershed model without assigning invented measured rates.

Thursday: Interpret simulated rainfall

Focus: Interpret simulated rainfall.

Learn: Fictional monthly rainfall totals of 30,50,20 mm have a combined total of 100 mm. A three-point record does not establish climate trends.

Practice and evidence: Make a bar chart and distinguish one supported quantity from two unsupported inferences.

Friday: Write an Earth-systems CER

Focus: Write an Earth-systems CER.

Learn: System explanations connect an observation, a mechanism and uncertainty without confusing diagrams with field data.

Practice and evidence: Submit a four-sphere model, rainfall chart and CER paragraph. Rubric: model 6, quantities 5, explanation 5, limits 4.
