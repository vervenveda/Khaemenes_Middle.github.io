Week 32 · Science & Engineering

Health, Identity, and Responsible Choices

How do healthy routines support learning and identity?

Monday: Explore light and vision

Focus: Explore light and vision.

Learn: Light enters the eye and is processed by the visual system; classroom models do not diagnose vision.

Practice and evidence: Sketch a ray diagram for a safe non-laser light source and label its limitations.

Tuesday: Investigate reflection

Focus: Investigate reflection.

Learn: Light reflects from surfaces, but perceived brightness depends on several conditions.

Practice and evidence: Plan a safe paper-and-flashlight observation without shining light at anyone's eyes.

Wednesday: Control an observation

Focus: Control an observation.

Learn: A fair comparison keeps distance, ambient light and measurement procedure consistent.

Practice and evidence: Design a table for three repeated comparisons of paper surfaces.

Thursday: Assess scientific claims

Focus: Assess scientific claims.

Learn: A single classroom observation cannot establish medical conclusions.

Practice and evidence: Interpret fictional observations and identify two confounding factors.

Friday: Present an accurate model

Focus: Present an accurate model.

Learn: Scientific explanations separate the physical process from individual experiences.

Practice and evidence: Submit a labeled reflection model, observation plan and evidence-limits paragraph. Rubric: model 6, method 6, reasoning 4, limits 4.
