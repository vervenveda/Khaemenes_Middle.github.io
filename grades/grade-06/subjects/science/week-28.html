Week 28 · Science & Engineering

World Languages and Cultural Communication

How does language carry culture and connection?

Monday: Investigate how sound carries speech

Focus: Investigate how sound carries speech.

Learn: Speech produces vibrations that travel through a medium; sound is not transmitted through empty space.

Practice and evidence: Draw a labeled sound-source-to-listener model and identify its assumptions.

Tuesday: Explore pitch and frequency

Focus: Explore pitch and frequency.

Learn: Pitch relates to vibration frequency, while loudness relates broadly to vibration amplitude.

Practice and evidence: Use a teacher-led safe rubber-band demonstration; describe relative pitch without measuring people.

Wednesday: Make an observation plan

Focus: Make an observation plan.

Learn: Fair tests change one variable while keeping others, such as distance and background noise, consistent.

Practice and evidence: Plan an accessible classroom test using a quiet mechanical sound source and repeatable distances.

Thursday: Separate measurement from interpretation

Focus: Separate measurement from interpretation.

Learn: Sound intensity, hearing perception and comprehensibility are different ideas.

Practice and evidence: Create a data table using classroom-safe observations and state two limitations.

Friday: Explain a communication system

Focus: Explain a communication system.

Learn: A scientific model explains sound transmission but cannot establish cultural meaning on its own.

Practice and evidence: Submit a diagram, controlled-test plan and claim-evidence-reasoning paragraph. Rubric: model 6, method 5, evidence 5, limitations 4.
