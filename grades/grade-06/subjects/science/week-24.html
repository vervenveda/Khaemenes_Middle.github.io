Week 24 · Science & Engineering

Ancient River Civilizations

How did rivers support early civilizations and complex societies?

Monday: Model river and floodplain

Focus: Model river and floodplain.

Learn: A river moves water and sediment through a watershed. Flooding can redistribute water and sediments, while impacts depend on the landscape.

Practice and evidence: Draw a fictional watershed showing upstream/downstream, floodplain and sediment movement.

Tuesday: Explore sediment deposition

Focus: Explore sediment deposition.

Learn: When moving water slows, some suspended sediment may settle. Particle size and flow conditions influence transport; a picture alone cannot prove deposited mass.

Practice and evidence: Annotate a safe paper diagram of fast and slow river segments, identifying a prediction and a missing measurement.

Wednesday: Consider agricultural conditions

Focus: Consider agricultural conditions.

Learn: Crop growth can depend on water, soil characteristics, nutrients and climate. Flooding can benefit or damage agriculture depending on timing and intensity.

Practice and evidence: Make a fictional two-season comparison table of water availability and agricultural outcomes, labeling assumptions.

Thursday: Read a flood dataset

Focus: Read a flood dataset.

Learn: A fictional sequence of water heights 2,3,5,3 meters documents a peak in a simulated model, not an observed ancient flood.

Practice and evidence: Plot the simulated values and explain one supported claim and two limitations.

Friday: Present a river-systems CER

Focus: Present a river-systems CER.

Learn: A valid scientific explanation uses defined mechanisms, labeled simulations, and distinguishes environmental processes from speculative historical outcomes.

Practice and evidence: Submit a river diagram, height graph and CER response. Rubric: model 6, data 5, explanation 5, limitations 4.
