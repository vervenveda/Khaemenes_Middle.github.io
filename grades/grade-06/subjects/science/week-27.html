Week 27 · Science & Engineering

Trade Routes, Migration, and Exchange

How do movement and exchange transform societies?

Monday: Explain constraints on travel

Focus: Explain constraints on travel.

Learn: Weather, terrain and seasonal water access can influence movement, but no single factor explains all migration.

Practice and evidence: Map hypothetical journey hazards and separate physical constraints from human decisions.

Tuesday: Compare insulation materials

Focus: Compare insulation materials.

Learn: Thermal properties of materials can be tested using safe room-temperature models.

Practice and evidence: Plan a fair wrapped-container temperature observation with consistent intervals and no heating.

Wednesday: Collect repeatable evidence

Focus: Collect repeatable evidence.

Learn: Control starting conditions and record units to compare models fairly.

Practice and evidence: Create a three-trial data table for mock transport containers.

Thursday: Distinguish weather and climate

Focus: Distinguish weather and climate.

Learn: Weather is short-term atmospheric conditions; climate describes longer-term patterns.

Practice and evidence: Explain why a single rainy day cannot prove a region's historical climate.

Friday: Defend a transport recommendation

Focus: Defend a transport recommendation.

Learn: Test results support limited model conclusions, not undocumented historical claims.

Practice and evidence: Submit a labeled graph and claim-evidence-reasoning conclusion with two limitations. Rubric: method 5, data 6, reasoning 5, limits 4.
