Week 25 · Technology, Design & Tools

Belief Systems, Law, and Culture

How do beliefs and laws shape community life?

Monday: Represent a rules record

Focus: Represent a rules record.

Learn: A learning archive can store an invented rule's title, source, date, audience, version and translation notes.

Practice and evidence: Design a small offline structured table with clear fictional labels.

Tuesday: Preserve provenance

Focus: Preserve provenance.

Learn: Digital descriptions should distinguish original source, modern translation and student interpretation rather than overwrite one with another.

Practice and evidence: Write pseudocode that stores each evidence type separately.

Wednesday: Validate cultural records

Focus: Validate cultural records.

Learn: A record missing its source or using a false date should trigger an accessible review notice; do not invent historical credentials.

Practice and evidence: Develop six validation tests for source, date, duplicate ID and missing translation notes.

Thursday: Design responsive access

Focus: Design responsive access.

Learn: A 255px-wide record viewer needs readable labels, keyboard navigation, print output and clear long-word wrapping.

Practice and evidence: Sketch a narrow-screen bilingual archive interface with a plain-text export.

Friday: Document ethical choices

Focus: Document ethical choices.

Learn: Archives should avoid collecting private belief information and keep attribution transparent.

Practice and evidence: Submit schema, wireframe and test log. Rubric: provenance 6, testing 6, access 5, notes 3.
