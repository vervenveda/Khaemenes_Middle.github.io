Week 21 · Technology, Design & Tools

Ecosystems and Energy Flow

How do energy and matter move through ecosystems?

Monday: Structure food-web data

Focus: Structure food-web data.

Learn: An offline model can store organism names and feeding relationships, recording arrows from food sources toward eaters.

Practice and evidence: Design a fictional table of producer, consumer and feeding-link records.

Tuesday: Validate graph links

Focus: Validate graph links.

Learn: A food-web editor should flag missing organisms and self-links according to its clearly stated simplified rules.

Practice and evidence: Write pseudocode for adding and validating a labeled food-web edge.

Wednesday: Handle energy labels

Focus: Handle energy labels.

Learn: An arrow indicates a modeled transfer, not a verified measurement of calories; any quantities must be explicitly hypothetical.

Practice and evidence: Prepare four tests for arrow directions, missing labels and fictional-data indicators.

Thursday: Make accessible diagrams

Focus: Make accessible diagrams.

Learn: A 255px view needs wrapped organism labels, readable controls, keyboard-friendly navigation and a plain-text adjacency list.

Practice and evidence: Sketch a narrow-screen food-web display with an equivalent text table.

Friday: Document a model prototype

Focus: Document a model prototype.

Learn: A good learning app records its simplifications and can export or print data without depending on a service.

Practice and evidence: Submit pseudocode, six tests and a 255px interface sketch. Rubric: logic 6, tests 6, access 5, docs 3.
