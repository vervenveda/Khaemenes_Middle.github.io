Week 36 · Technology, Design & Tools

Final Review and Grade 7 Readiness

How am I ready for seventh grade?

Monday: Review data quality

Focus: Review data quality.

Learn: Accurate datasets need units, provenance, missing-value markers and non-sensitive examples.

Practice and evidence: Correct a fictional four-row spreadsheet with one duplicate and missing date.

Tuesday: Explain basic algorithms

Focus: Explain basic algorithms.

Learn: Repeatable steps have defined input, action and output.

Practice and evidence: Write pseudocode for finding a maximum from three invented measurements.

Wednesday: Audit usability

Focus: Audit usability.

Learn: Tools should work offline with keyboard navigation and narrow layouts.

Practice and evidence: Sketch a 255px interface with labels, wrapping and accessible choices.

Thursday: Protect records

Focus: Protect records.

Learn: Sharing sample work must not expose learner identities or overwrite verified progress.

Practice and evidence: Draft an import/export preview and reset-confirmation test.

Friday: Document a reliable tool

Focus: Document a reliable tool.

Learn: Technical readiness is demonstrated by tests, honesty about bugs and revision.

Practice and evidence: Submit a three-case test log and prototype specification. Rubric: correctness 6, documentation 6, access 4, privacy 4.
