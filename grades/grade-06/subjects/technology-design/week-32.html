Week 32 · Technology, Design & Tools

Health, Identity, and Responsible Choices

How do healthy routines support learning and identity?

Monday: Examine privacy risks

Focus: Examine privacy risks.

Learn: Wellness apps can collect sensitive information; classroom prototypes should avoid personal health records.

Practice and evidence: Draft a data-minimization plan for a fictional wellness planner.

Tuesday: Design accessible reminders

Focus: Design accessible reminders.

Learn: Clear labels, adjustable text and optional notifications support different users.

Practice and evidence: Sketch a 255px offline reminder interface with no mandatory health inputs.

Wednesday: Create nontracking visuals

Focus: Create nontracking visuals.

Learn: A coordinate grid can organize fictional activities without ranking people or tracking identity.

Practice and evidence: Place fictional activity locations on a coordinate plane and label axes.

Thursday: Test safe storage behavior

Focus: Test safe storage behavior.

Learn: Local-only sample data still requires transparent reset, export and identity boundaries.

Practice and evidence: Write test cases for empty fields, device sharing, import and reset confirmation.

Friday: Deliver an ethical prototype

Focus: Deliver an ethical prototype.

Learn: A wellness interface should present options, not diagnose students or assign hidden grades.

Practice and evidence: Submit a three-screen offline mockup and privacy/accessibility checklist. Rubric: privacy 6, usability 6, evidence 4, review 4.
