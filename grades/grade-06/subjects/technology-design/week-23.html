Week 23 · Technology, Design & Tools

Weather, Climate, and Human Patterns

How can patterns help communities prepare for change?

Monday: Seminar Launch and Academic Vocabulary

Focus: spreadsheets

Objective: Students strengthen technology, design & tools through spreadsheets while investigating 'Weather, Climate, and Human Patterns'.

Activity: Introduce the subject focus, connect the essential question, model middle-school thinking, and define academic vocabulary.

Workshop: Technology, Design & Tools A++ workshop: create, solve, read, test, discuss, calculate, revise, or present evidence about weather, climate, and human patterns.

Teacher script: Name the subject: Technology, Design & Tools. Connect to the essential question: How can patterns help communities prepare for change? Model the focus skill: spreadsheets. Require evidence, calculation, model, vocabulary, citation note, or clear explanation. Close with a defense or reflection: The strongest evidence in my work is ____.

Evidence: Student work sample, teacher observation, oral explanation, correction/revision, and weekly subject mastery evidence.
