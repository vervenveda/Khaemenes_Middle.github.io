Week 04 · Technology, Design & Tools

Percent, Proportion, and Civic Decisions

How can percentages help a community make informed decisions?

Monday: Seminar Launch and Academic Vocabulary

Focus: source notes

Objective: Students strengthen technology, design & tools through source notes while investigating 'Percent, Proportion, and Civic Decisions'.

Activity: Introduce the subject focus, connect the essential question, model middle-school thinking, and define academic vocabulary.

Workshop: Technology, Design & Tools A++ workshop: create, solve, read, test, discuss, calculate, revise, or present evidence about percent, proportion, and civic decisions.

Teacher script: Name the subject: Technology, Design & Tools. Connect to the essential question: How can percentages help a community make informed decisions? Model the focus skill: source notes. Require evidence, calculation, model, vocabulary, citation note, or clear explanation. Close with a defense or reflection: The strongest evidence in my work is ____.

Evidence: Student work sample, teacher observation, oral explanation, correction/revision, and weekly subject mastery evidence.
