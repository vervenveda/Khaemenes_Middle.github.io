Week 22 · Technology, Design & Tools

Earth Systems and Water

How do Earth systems interact and affect human life?

Monday: Organize water observations

Focus: Organize water observations.

Learn: An offline table can contain fictional rainfall, river flow and water use; each measure needs units, date and source type.

Practice and evidence: Draft a clearly simulated rainfall table for three days with values 2,4,6 mm.

Tuesday: Compute water totals

Focus: Compute water totals.

Learn: For fictional rainfall amounts 2,4,6 mm, the total is 12 mm and mean is 4 mm, assuming comparable observation periods.

Practice and evidence: Write simple pseudocode to calculate total and mean, checking all three numeric entries.

Wednesday: Validate water data

Focus: Validate water data.

Learn: Missing data, inconsistent measurement units and negative rainfall entries should be identified rather than silently converted into facts.

Practice and evidence: List six tests for the fictional rainfall calculator.

Thursday: Design an accessible mobile chart

Focus: Design an accessible mobile chart.

Learn: A 255px display should offer wrapped unit labels, a keyboard-accessible table and text summaries of values.

Practice and evidence: Sketch an offline narrow-screen rainfall chart and equivalent text table.

Friday: Document the prototype

Focus: Document the prototype.

Learn: A trustworthy data tool records expected results, test outcomes, accessible exports and limitations of invented measurements.

Practice and evidence: Submit pseudocode, six tests and print-ready design. Rubric: computations 6, tests 6, access 5, documentation 3.
