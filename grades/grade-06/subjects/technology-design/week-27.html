Week 27 · Technology, Design & Tools

Trade Routes, Migration, and Exchange

How do movement and exchange transform societies?

Monday: Represent routes responsibly

Focus: Represent routes responsibly.

Learn: Digital maps encode assumptions; route lines are not proof of precise historical travel.

Practice and evidence: Design a three-stop fictional route record with sources and uncertainty labels.

Tuesday: Structure reusable datasets

Focus: Structure reusable datasets.

Learn: Consistent location, date and source fields help compare records without disguising gaps.

Practice and evidence: Draft a local CSV schema for hypothetical trade records.

Wednesday: Check map accessibility

Focus: Check map accessibility.

Learn: Text equivalents and clear labels prevent maps from relying only on color.

Practice and evidence: Build a paper or offline HTML prototype usable at narrow screen widths.

Thursday: Test uncertain records

Focus: Test uncertain records.

Learn: A reliable interface should display missing dates and unverifiable claims transparently.

Practice and evidence: Test records with incomplete provenance and document error handling.

Friday: Present an ethical route atlas

Focus: Present an ethical route atlas.

Learn: Public school work must not expose student locations or identities.

Practice and evidence: Submit a three-record offline atlas and accessibility test report. Rubric: data 6, sources 6, usability 4, privacy 4.
