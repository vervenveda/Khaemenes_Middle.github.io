Week 18 · Technology, Design & Tools

Waves, Sound, and Communication

How do waves carry energy and information?

Monday: Trace a signal pathway

Focus: Trace a signal pathway.

Learn: A digital message can follow sender, input, encoding, channel, decoding and receiver. A diagram is a simplified representation of the system.

Practice and evidence: Sketch an offline paper message-flow diagram with labels.

Tuesday: Measure simulated frequency

Focus: Measure simulated frequency.

Learn: A fictional source produces 12 cycles in 3 seconds, corresponding to 4 Hz. Units must be shown and elapsed time must be positive.

Practice and evidence: Write pseudocode for cycles divided by time and calculate the example.

Wednesday: Validate the calculation

Focus: Validate the calculation.

Learn: A signal calculator should reject zero time, negative cycle counts and missing input with explanatory feedback.

Practice and evidence: Document six tests including correct values and invalid inputs.

Thursday: Design narrow-screen access

Focus: Design narrow-screen access.

Learn: A 255px interface needs readable inputs, textual wave descriptions and a table that does not require audio or animation.

Practice and evidence: Sketch a mobile wave-frequency tool with keyboard-friendly controls.

Friday: Certify communication design

Focus: Certify communication design.

Learn: A documented design includes formula, diagram, tests, accessibility and a known model limitation.

Practice and evidence: Submit a paper prototype, tests and revision log. Rubric: accuracy 6, validation 6, access 5, documentation 3.
