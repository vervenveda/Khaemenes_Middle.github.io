Week 30 · World Languages & Global Culture

Argument, Debate, and Public Speaking

How can disagreement become disciplined learning?

Monday: Practice courteous disagreement

Focus: Practice courteous disagreement.

Learn: Forms of polite disagreement depend on language, register and social context.

Practice and evidence: Verify three target-language phrases for agreeing, questioning and disagreeing.

Tuesday: Ask for clarification

Focus: Ask for clarification.

Learn: Follow-up questions prevent a discussion from becoming an exchange of memorized lines.

Practice and evidence: Write a short modern dialogue with a clarification request and response.

Wednesday: State a reason clearly

Focus: State a reason clearly.

Learn: Simple connectors can link a view and its justification without overstating certainty.

Practice and evidence: Compose four target-language sentences expressing a preference with a reason.

Thursday: Listen for opposing views

Focus: Listen for opposing views.

Learn: Independent listening evidence should be gathered before a transcript is shown.

Practice and evidence: Listen twice to a teacher-read discussion and record each speaker's view and one reason.

Friday: Hold a respectful mini-debate

Focus: Hold a respectful mini-debate.

Learn: Communication mastery requires understandable meaning and self-correction.

Practice and evidence: Perform a 45-second bilingual exchange and note two corrections. Rubric: comprehension 6, meaning 6, politeness 4, revision 4.
