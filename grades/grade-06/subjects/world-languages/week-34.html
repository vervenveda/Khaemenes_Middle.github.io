Week 34 · World Languages & Global Culture

A++ Big Question Capstone Research

What serious question can I investigate with evidence, calculation, and design?

Monday: Choose bilingual research vocabulary

Focus: Choose bilingual research vocabulary.

Learn: Research communication relies on accurate words for question, evidence and conclusion.

Practice and evidence: Verify six terms and practical examples in the chosen target language.

Tuesday: Ask respectful questions

Focus: Ask respectful questions.

Learn: Open questions and courteous responses help gather meaning without demanding private disclosures.

Practice and evidence: Write five modern role-play interview questions with verified phrasing.

Wednesday: Summarize sourced information

Focus: Summarize sourced information.

Learn: Translation requires judgment about meaning, audience and register.

Practice and evidence: Draft two concise bilingual summaries of a fictional classroom project.

Thursday: Listen for research intent

Focus: Listen for research intent.

Learn: Independent comprehension comes before checking a transcript.

Practice and evidence: Listen to a teacher-read project invitation and identify purpose, action and audience.

Friday: Present a multilingual research pitch

Focus: Present a multilingual research pitch.

Learn: A successful pitch communicates a question and reason with accountable language choices.

Practice and evidence: Present a 45-second bilingual research pitch and source notes. Rubric: meaning 6, listening 6, clarity 4, revision 4.
