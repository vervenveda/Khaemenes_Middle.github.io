Week 36 · World Languages & Global Culture

Final Review and Grade 7 Readiness

How am I ready for seventh grade?

Monday: Revisit essential communication

Focus: Revisit essential communication.

Learn: Modern greetings, requests and directions require context-appropriate forms.

Practice and evidence: Prepare an accurate six-item target-language glossary with verified examples.

Tuesday: Strengthen practical dialogues

Focus: Strengthen practical dialogues.

Learn: Meaningful exchanges require listening, clarification and polite responses.

Practice and evidence: Write a six-line dialogue for a fictional library or community meeting.

Wednesday: Interpret translations carefully

Focus: Interpret translations carefully.

Learn: A translation may need rephrasing to preserve intention and register.

Practice and evidence: Explain two choices in a fictional bilingual notice.

Thursday: Demonstrate independent listening

Focus: Demonstrate independent listening.

Learn: Record comprehension before reviewing subtitles or a transcript.

Practice and evidence: Listen to a teacher-read invitation and identify the topic, time and action.

Friday: Reflect on language growth

Focus: Reflect on language growth.

Learn: Show independent expression and identify a next-level communication goal.

Practice and evidence: Present a 45-second dialogue and evidence log. Rubric: meaning 6, comprehension 6, clarity 4, reflection 4.
