Week 32 · World Languages & Global Culture

Health, Identity, and Responsible Choices

How do healthy routines support learning and identity?

Monday: Learn everyday wellness vocabulary

Focus: Learn everyday wellness vocabulary.

Learn: Modern language for rest, food, activity and asking for help varies by culture and context.

Practice and evidence: Verify six target-language words or phrases with examples from approved learning resources.

Tuesday: Practice courteous requests

Focus: Practice courteous requests.

Learn: A practical request for rest, clarification or assistance should be understandable and respectful.

Practice and evidence: Write two modern dialogues about asking for a break or accessible instructions.

Wednesday: Interpret meaning carefully

Focus: Interpret meaning carefully.

Learn: Literal translation of idioms or sensitive subjects can misrepresent intent.

Practice and evidence: Annotate two fictional wellness sentences and explain a translation choice.

Thursday: Listen for key information

Focus: Listen for key information.

Learn: Independent listening requires identifying meaning before seeing a transcript.

Practice and evidence: Listen to a teacher-read modern activity invitation, noting place, time and options.

Friday: Communicate a safe choice

Focus: Communicate a safe choice.

Learn: Language practice should demonstrate meaning without requiring personal health disclosure.

Practice and evidence: Present a 45-second bilingual invitation with comprehension notes. Rubric: meaning 6, listening 6, clarity 4, revision 4.
