Week 32 · World Languages & Global Culture

Health, Identity, and Responsible Choices

How do healthy routines support learning and identity?

Monday: Seminar Launch and Academic Vocabulary

Focus: translation awareness

Objective: Students strengthen world languages & global culture through translation awareness while investigating 'Health, Identity, and Responsible Choices'.

Activity: Introduce the subject focus, connect the essential question, model middle-school thinking, and define academic vocabulary.

Workshop: World Languages & Global Culture A++ workshop: create, solve, read, test, discuss, calculate, revise, or present evidence about health, identity, and responsible choices.

Model the focus skill: translation awareness. Require evidence, calculation, model, vocabulary, citation note, or clear explanation. Close with a defense or reflection: The strongest evidence in my work is ____.
