Week 03 · World Languages & Global Culture

Rates, Unit Rates, and Informational Text

How can rates and text structures explain change?

Monday: Frequency words in context

Focus: Frequency words in context.

Learn: Frequency expressions describe how often something happens. In Spanish, una vez means once, dos veces means twice, and por semana means per week. Different languages may order these words differently.

Practice and evidence: Create a bilingual chart of fictional weekly library visits: one, two and three visits per week. Translate each expression and verify meaning.

Tuesday: Numbers with units

Focus: Numbers with units.

Learn: A quantity is clearer when the unit is present. In Spanish, kilómetros por hora describes kilometers per hour, a rate; translating a rate requires both quantities.

Practice and evidence: Label a fictional trip of 12 km in 3 hours in two languages; compute 4 km/h and explain the role of por.

Wednesday: Read informational signs

Focus: Read informational signs.

Learn: Signs compress information into short labels. Spanish horario means schedule, and abierto often means open. Meaning depends on the full sentence and local use.

Practice and evidence: Create a bilingual fictional museum sign showing opening days and hours; distinguish observed text from assumed information.

Thursday: Compare frequency responsibly

Focus: Compare frequency responsibly.

Learn: Comparisons like more often or less often depend on evidence. For instance, two visits in four weeks is 0.5 visits per week, while three visits in three weeks is 1 per week.

Practice and evidence: Draft three English comparison sentences and translate into a chosen target language using verified terms. Label any uncertain expressions.

Friday: Communication portfolio

Focus: Communication portfolio.

Learn: Accurate bilingual explanations include units, context, and a clear audience.

Practice and evidence: Submit a bilingual visit-frequency table and a six-line fictional dialogue about a museum schedule; cite where words were checked. Rubric 20: vocabulary 5, rate clarity 5, contextual meaning 5, revision 5.
