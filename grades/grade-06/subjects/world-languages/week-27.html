Week 27 · World Languages & Global Culture

Trade Routes, Migration, and Exchange

How do movement and exchange transform societies?

Monday: Learn travel and exchange words

Focus: Learn travel and exchange words.

Learn: Everyday terms for directions, goods and travel vary by language and context.

Practice and evidence: Make a six-word bilingual travel glossary from approved language resources.

Tuesday: Ask for directions politely

Focus: Ask for directions politely.

Learn: Formal and informal requests differ across languages; practice modern scenarios rather than invented historical quotes.

Practice and evidence: Write two short dialogues asking for a market location.

Wednesday: Explain price and quantity

Focus: Explain price and quantity.

Learn: Numbers and polite exchanges support understandable communication.

Practice and evidence: Create and role-play a fictional market purchase with quantities and a clear price.

Thursday: Check listening comprehension

Focus: Check listening comprehension.

Learn: Listening requires recognizing meaning, not simply reading translated captions.

Practice and evidence: Listen to a teacher-read modern travel announcement and record destination, time and requested action.

Friday: Present a welcoming travel guide

Focus: Present a welcoming travel guide.

Learn: Translation should prioritize meaning and acknowledge uncertain idioms.

Practice and evidence: Deliver a 45-second bilingual welcome script with revisions and comprehension notes. Rubric: meaning 6, pronunciation 4, listening 5, reflection 5.
