Week 25 · World Languages & Global Culture

Belief Systems, Law, and Culture

How do beliefs and laws shape community life?

Monday: Translate law-and-culture words

Focus: Translate law-and-culture words.

Learn: Spanish ley means law, cultura culture and respeto respect. Legal terms can vary with jurisdiction and historical setting.

Practice and evidence: Prepare a bilingual glossary with notes distinguishing classroom examples from authoritative legal translations.

Tuesday: Use respectful comparisons

Focus: Use respectful comparisons.

Learn: Spanish tradición means tradition and costumbre means custom. Neither implies every member of a community behaves alike.

Practice and evidence: Write paired bilingual sentences comparing fictional customs without stereotyping.

Wednesday: Recognize formal register

Focus: Recognize formal register.

Learn: A request in a civic context may use formal phrasing and politeness. Legal translation demands more precision than an everyday paraphrase.

Practice and evidence: Draft a short Spanish-English hypothetical town-hall dialogue and identify words needing verification.

Thursday: Describe ambiguity

Focus: Describe ambiguity.

Learn: Words for rights, duties or belief can carry different historical meanings; direct translation may omit important context.

Practice and evidence: Annotate two terms with possible ambiguities and a strategy for checking them.

Friday: Publish a bilingual civic glossary

Focus: Publish a bilingual civic glossary.

Learn: Effective multilingual explanation names source language, intended audience and translation limits.

Practice and evidence: Submit glossary, fictional dialogue and reflection. Rubric: meaning 6, accuracy 5, respect 5, revision 4.
