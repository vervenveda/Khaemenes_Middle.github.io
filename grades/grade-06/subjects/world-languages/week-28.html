Week 28 · World Languages & Global Culture

World Languages and Cultural Communication

How does language carry culture and connection?

Monday: Practice contextual greetings

Focus: Practice contextual greetings.

Learn: Greetings vary by language, register, age and setting; no phrase is universally appropriate.

Practice and evidence: Choose an Academy-supported target language and write two verified modern greetings, noting context.

Tuesday: Build useful exchange vocabulary

Focus: Build useful exchange vocabulary.

Learn: Vocabulary should support real situations such as introductions, directions and requests.

Practice and evidence: Build a six-term bilingual glossary with sources, pronunciation notes and example sentences.

Wednesday: Compose a short conversation

Focus: Compose a short conversation.

Learn: An understandable dialogue combines greeting, question, response and polite closing.

Practice and evidence: Write and rehearse a six-line modern conversation with a partner or teacher.

Thursday: Listen independently

Focus: Listen independently.

Learn: Listening comprehension is tested by meaning, not by merely reading a translation.

Practice and evidence: Listen twice to a teacher-read or locally recorded introduction; identify speaker, purpose and one requested action before checking a transcript.

Friday: Demonstrate meaningful communication

Focus: Demonstrate meaningful communication.

Learn: Original output and self-correction show more learning than memorized isolated words.

Practice and evidence: Present a 45-second dialogue and annotate two translation decisions. Rubric: comprehension 6, expression 6, pronunciation or intelligibility 4, revision 4.
