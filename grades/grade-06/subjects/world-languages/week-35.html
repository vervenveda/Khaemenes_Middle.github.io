Week 35 · World Languages & Global Culture

Capstone Studio and Oral Defense

How can I make my evidence clear enough to defend publicly?

Monday: Prepare a bilingual summary

Focus: Prepare a bilingual summary.

Learn: Communicating a project demands verified vocabulary and understandable target-language sentences.

Practice and evidence: Write a six-term glossary and a short modern project introduction.

Tuesday: Check register

Focus: Check register.

Learn: Public introductions and requests for clarification need suitable levels of formality.

Practice and evidence: Revise two fictional phrases for a respectful audience.

Wednesday: Listen for questions

Focus: Listen for questions.

Learn: Independent listening evidence comes before a written transcript is revealed.

Practice and evidence: Listen to a teacher-read project question and note topic and requested answer.

Thursday: Rehearse a response

Focus: Rehearse a response.

Learn: An effective answer addresses a question and can politely request repetition.

Practice and evidence: Perform a short bilingual mock question-and-answer exchange.

Friday: Present meaningful communication

Focus: Present meaningful communication.

Learn: Oral or alternative accessible presentation should demonstrate comprehension and self-correction.

Practice and evidence: Give a 45-second summary, glossary and revision note. Rubric: meaning 6, listening 6, clarity 4, reflection 4.
