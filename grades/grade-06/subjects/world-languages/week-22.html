Week 22 · World Languages & Global Culture

Earth Systems and Water

How do Earth systems interact and affect human life?

Monday: Learn water vocabulary

Focus: Learn water vocabulary.

Learn: Spanish agua means water, lluvia rain, río river and nube cloud. Labels should distinguish weather terms from landforms.

Practice and evidence: Create a checked bilingual water-cycle glossary and draw a fictional labeled landscape.

Tuesday: Describe weather and place

Focus: Describe weather and place.

Learn: Spanish evaporación and condensación name processes; a translation must preserve the difference between a process and a place.

Practice and evidence: Write four bilingual captions connecting evaporation, condensation and rain in a model.

Wednesday: Clarify environmental statements

Focus: Clarify environmental statements.

Learn: A river and rainfall can matter differently to communities; avoid presenting invented cultural traditions as historical facts.

Practice and evidence: Draft a respectful dialogue asking how a fictional town protects drinking water.

Thursday: Use geographic directions

Focus: Use geographic directions.

Learn: Spanish arriba means above and abajo below. A diagram's arrow labels should name whether water moves as vapor, precipitation or runoff.

Practice and evidence: Annotate a bilingual water-cycle schematic and explain two arrow directions.

Friday: Present a bilingual water poster

Focus: Present a bilingual water poster.

Learn: Effective environmental communication uses checked vocabulary, readable labels and honest scientific limitations.

Practice and evidence: Submit a bilingual diagram, glossary and short explanation. Rubric: language 6, science 6, accessibility 4, revision 4.
