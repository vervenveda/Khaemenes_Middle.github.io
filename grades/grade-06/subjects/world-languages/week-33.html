Week 33 · World Languages & Global Culture

Technology, Data, and Ethical Creation

How can tools strengthen evidence without replacing judgment?

Monday: Research interface vocabulary

Focus: Research interface vocabulary.

Learn: Labels for save, back, next and help require correct meaning and cultural context.

Practice and evidence: Verify six target-language interface terms and their sources.

Tuesday: Consider script direction

Focus: Consider script direction.

Learn: Interfaces should respect right-to-left, left-to-right and mixed-script text.

Practice and evidence: Sketch two fictional interface labels with appropriate reading order.

Wednesday: Translate actionable instructions

Focus: Translate actionable instructions.

Learn: Clarity matters more than word-for-word similarity in a practical instruction.

Practice and evidence: Write a polite two-step target-language tutorial for an invented app.

Thursday: Listen for a requested action

Focus: Listen for a requested action.

Learn: Independent listening checks comprehension of a modern spoken instruction.

Practice and evidence: Listen to a teacher-read setup instruction and record two actions before seeing text.

Friday: Review a multilingual prototype

Focus: Review a multilingual prototype.

Learn: Accuracy, access and self-correction demonstrate language learning.

Practice and evidence: Present a 45-second interface walkthrough with glossary and revisions. Rubric: meaning 6, listening 6, usability 4, reflection 4.
