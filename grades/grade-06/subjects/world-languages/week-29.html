Week 29 · World Languages & Global Culture

Research Writing: From Question to Thesis

How do researchers build trustworthy explanations?

Monday: Research multilingual keywords

Focus: Research multilingual keywords.

Learn: A translated academic term may vary with context or grammatical register.

Practice and evidence: Build a six-word verified research glossary in the chosen target language.

Tuesday: Evaluate bilingual references

Focus: Evaluate bilingual references.

Learn: Dictionaries explain possible meanings; a word list alone cannot prove a full sentence translation.

Practice and evidence: Compare two supported glosses and record which fits an invented source sentence.

Wednesday: Ask polite research questions

Focus: Ask polite research questions.

Learn: Question words and courteous openings enable meaningful exchanges.

Practice and evidence: Write five modern interview questions suitable for a voluntary classroom role-play.

Thursday: Practice listening independently

Focus: Practice listening independently.

Learn: Listening evidence should be collected before a written transcript is shown.

Practice and evidence: Listen twice to a teacher-read research invitation and identify purpose, topic and requested response.

Friday: Present a bilingual research summary

Focus: Present a bilingual research summary.

Learn: Meaningful communication includes accurate intent and willingness to self-correct.

Practice and evidence: Deliver a 45-second summary and glossary with two revision notes. Rubric: comprehension 6, accuracy 6, clarity 4, revision 4.
