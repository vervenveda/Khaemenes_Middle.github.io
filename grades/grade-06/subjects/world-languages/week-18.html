Week 18 · World Languages & Global Culture

Waves, Sound, and Communication

How do waves carry energy and information?

Monday: Hear and identify meaning

Focus: Hear and identify meaning.

Learn: Listening comprehension considers context, pronunciation and a listener's hearing needs. A message may be conveyed through spoken language or a written equivalent.

Practice and evidence: Create an English-Spanish glossary for sound, voice, listening, message and clarification.

Tuesday: Practice clarification

Focus: Practice clarification.

Learn: Spanish ¿Puede repetirlo? means Can you repeat it? A learner can request a slower pace or an alternative written message.

Practice and evidence: Write two original short clarification dialogues with English translations.

Wednesday: Compare sound and spelling

Focus: Compare sound and spelling.

Learn: Letters and sounds do not correspond identically across languages. A phonetic approximation should be checked and not presented as universal pronunciation.

Practice and evidence: Annotate two vocabulary items with careful spelling and a note on accent or regional variation.

Thursday: Communicate without audio

Focus: Communicate without audio.

Learn: Subtitles, transcripts and readable captions can provide access when audio is unavailable or unsuitable.

Practice and evidence: Make a bilingual plain-text transcript of a fictional three-line announcement.

Friday: Publish a bilingual communication guide

Focus: Publish a bilingual communication guide.

Learn: A useful portfolio checks meaning, respects diverse speakers and offers accessible alternatives.

Practice and evidence: Submit a vocabulary record, corrected dialogue and transcript. Rubric: meaning 6, revision 5, access 5, reflection 4.
