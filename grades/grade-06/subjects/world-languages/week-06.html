Week 06 · World Languages & Global Culture

Rational Numbers and Ancient Number Systems

How have cultures represented numbers and meaning across time?

Monday: Number words and symbols

Focus: Number words and symbols.

Learn: Number words are language-specific while written numerical symbols may travel across languages. Spanish uno, dos, tres correspond to one, two, three. Roman I, II, III represent those values through a different notation system.

Practice and evidence: Build a bilingual 1–10 number chart and add Roman numerals I through X, verifying each translation.

Tuesday: Different writing systems

Focus: Different writing systems.

Learn: Modern Arabic-Indic digits ٠١٢٣٤٥٦٧٨٩ and Western digits 0123456789 are two representations used in different contexts. Do not assume everyone in a language community uses the same style.

Practice and evidence: Compare 0, 1, 2, 3 in the two digit forms and provide a respectful note about regional variation.

Wednesday: Signed and fractional quantities

Focus: Signed and fractional quantities.

Learn: The mathematical minus sign and a negative number require context. Language learners should explain both a sign and what it applies to; −3 °C is a temperature, not a negative count of objects.

Practice and evidence: Make a bilingual fictional weather card with −3 °C and +2 °C, including verified wording and units.

Thursday: Clarify cultural meanings

Focus: Clarify cultural meanings.

Learn: Transliteration approximates pronunciation and does not replace a script. A word's exact meaning may depend on grammar and context; a dictionary is a starting point for verification.

Practice and evidence: Make three vocabulary cards identifying target script, transliteration if useful, English meaning and source confidence.

Friday: Multilingual representation portfolio

Focus: Multilingual representation portfolio.

Learn: Strong communication identifies number values, accurate words, appropriate symbols and translation limits.

Practice and evidence: Produce a bilingual number chart, a signed-temperature statement, and a short note comparing symbol systems. Rubric 20: meaning 6, symbols 5, context 5, review 4.
