Week 21 · World Languages & Global Culture

Ecosystems and Energy Flow

How do energy and matter move through ecosystems?

Monday: Name ecosystem roles

Focus: Name ecosystem roles.

Learn: Spanish ecosistema means ecosystem, productor producer, consumidor consumer and descomponedor decomposer. Scientific categories need careful translation.

Practice and evidence: Prepare a checked bilingual glossary labeling a fictional grass-rabbit-fox food chain.

Tuesday: Describe energy directions

Focus: Describe energy directions.

Learn: In a food-chain diagram arrows point from food toward the eater; the direction has meaning in either language.

Practice and evidence: Write paired bilingual captions for an invented food web with arrows correctly labeled.

Wednesday: Compare habitats respectfully

Focus: Compare habitats respectfully.

Learn: Spanish bosque is forest and río is river. Avoid inventing cultural practices when discussing environmental stewardship.

Practice and evidence: Create two habitat descriptions and mark cultural statements needing source verification.

Thursday: Communicate conservation ideas

Focus: Communicate conservation ideas.

Learn: A respectful proposal names a concrete action and explains its likely purpose without assuming every community shares identical resources.

Practice and evidence: Draft a brief bilingual fictional community dialogue on protecting a local habitat.

Friday: Present a multilingual ecosystem poster

Focus: Present a multilingual ecosystem poster.

Learn: An inclusive science poster uses verified terms, accessible text labels and scientific cautions.

Practice and evidence: Submit a bilingual food-web diagram, glossary and reflection. Rubric: language 6, science 6, access 4, revision 4.
