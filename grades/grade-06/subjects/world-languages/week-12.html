Week 12 · World Languages & Global Culture

Geometry: Area, Surface Area, and Design

How do measurements help designers plan efficient spaces?

Monday: Shape vocabulary

Focus: Shape vocabulary.

Learn: Spanish rectángulo means rectangle, triángulo triangle, and cuadrado square. Shape names alone do not specify dimensions.

Practice and evidence: Create a checked bilingual glossary and label a rectangle 4 cm by 3 cm.

Tuesday: Measurements and units

Focus: Measurements and units.

Learn: Spanish centímetro is centimeter and metro is meter. An area needs squared units: 4 cm by 3 cm has area 12 cm².

Practice and evidence: Write four bilingual labels for lengths and areas; check which unit fits each.

Wednesday: Describe position

Focus: Describe position.

Learn: Spanish encima de means above and debajo de means below in many contexts. An accurate design description states the observer's frame of reference.

Practice and evidence: Write a bilingual four-sentence description of a labeled paper net.

Thursday: Compare shapes

Focus: Compare shapes.

Learn: A rectangle 5 cm by 2 cm and a square 3 cm by 3 cm have areas 10 cm² and 9 cm². Comparisons should identify the measured quantity.

Practice and evidence: Write bilingual comparison sentences about the two shapes and verify your calculations.

Friday: Present a bilingual design

Focus: Present a bilingual design.

Learn: A clear bilingual artifact needs verified vocabulary, identical numeric meaning in both languages and a readable diagram.

Practice and evidence: Submit a bilingual net or poster, glossary and six-sentence description. Rubric: language 6, area 5, labels 5, review 4.
