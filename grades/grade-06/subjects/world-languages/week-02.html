Week 02 · World Languages & Global Culture

Evidence, Annotation, and Ratios

How do evidence and comparison help us understand complex ideas?

Monday: Cognates and careful comparison

Focus: Cognates and careful comparison.

Learn: Cognates are words in different languages with shared histories and similar meanings, such as English animal and Spanish animal. Similar spelling alone does not guarantee the same meaning.

Practice and evidence: Create a two-column list of six Spanish-English words, mark likely cognates, and verify meanings. Note a false friend: Spanish embarazada does not mean embarrassed.

Tuesday: Labels and context clues

Focus: Labels and context clues.

Learn: A label identifies a thing, while its context helps readers understand purpose. Spanish biblioteca means library, and escuela means school.

Practice and evidence: Draw and label a fictional neighborhood with escuela, biblioteca and parque. Add English meanings and explain one inference from context.

Wednesday: Comparison sentences

Focus: Comparison sentences.

Learn: Spanish más ... que expresses more ... than, and menos ... que expresses less ... than. Comparisons should describe real or clearly fictional evidence, not stereotype cultures.

Practice and evidence: Write two comparisons using fictional library opening hours; include the exact figures and English translations.

Thursday: Using sources respectfully

Focus: Using sources respectfully.

Learn: An accurate translation requires attention to region, tone, audience, and context. Dictionaries provide starting points rather than universal social rules.

Practice and evidence: Annotate two greetings and describe how formal and informal situations could change your choice. Record where a phrase was verified.

Friday: Bilingual evidence task

Focus: Bilingual evidence task.

Learn: A meaningful language response combines vocabulary, contextual accuracy, and a brief exchange.

Practice and evidence: Prepare a six-line bilingual dialogue using a greeting, a place word, and one comparison. Add a glossary and one note about uncertainty. Rubric: vocabulary 5, grammar 5, context 5, reflection 5.
