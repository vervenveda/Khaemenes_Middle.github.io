Week 17 · World Languages & Global Culture

Midyear Portfolio and Assessment

How can I prove growth across every subject?

Monday: Collect communication evidence

Focus: Collect communication evidence.

Learn: A language portfolio preserves dated learner-created phrases, vocabulary and short conversations; errors and corrections can show progress.

Practice and evidence: Choose three previously studied vocabulary themes and identify an authentic sample from each.

Tuesday: Review scientific vocabulary

Focus: Review scientific vocabulary.

Learn: Spanish masa, volumen, fuerza and movimiento correspond to mass, volume, force and motion in scientific contexts.

Practice and evidence: Construct a checked bilingual glossary and explain one ambiguity.

Wednesday: Revise a dialogue

Focus: Revise a dialogue.

Learn: A short bilingual interaction should communicate meaning and allow clarification rather than reward memorization alone.

Practice and evidence: Revise a fictional dialogue about directions, fixing two translation errors.

Thursday: Reflect with respect

Focus: Reflect with respect.

Learn: Cultural comparisons should distinguish specific evidence from assumptions about groups or communities.

Practice and evidence: Write two respectful comparison observations and cite the classroom materials that support them.

Friday: Assemble a language portfolio

Focus: Assemble a language portfolio.

Learn: An accessible midyear portfolio combines glossary, dialogue, corrected wording and a learner reflection.

Practice and evidence: Submit selected samples and reflection. Rubric: meaning 6, revision 5, communication 5, evidence 4.
