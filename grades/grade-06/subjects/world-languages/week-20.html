Week 20 · World Languages & Global Culture

Cells, Systems, and Scientific Models

How do small structures support living systems?

Monday: Name cellular structures

Focus: Name cellular structures.

Learn: Spanish célula means cell, núcleo nucleus and membrana membrane. Cognates can help learning but their context must be checked.

Practice and evidence: Create a bilingual cell glossary with five accurate labels and sources for verification.

Tuesday: Identify roots

Focus: Identify roots.

Learn: The prefix micro- means small and bio- relates to life. Scientific vocabulary can cross languages without having identical everyday meanings.

Practice and evidence: Annotate microscope, microbiology and biology with root explanations.

Wednesday: Describe cell models

Focus: Describe cell models.

Learn: A fictional plant cell diagram may show a cell wall and chloroplasts. Not all cells have those structures.

Practice and evidence: Write paired English-Spanish captions stating the cell type and model limits.

Thursday: Clarify a science term

Focus: Clarify a science term.

Learn: A helpful clarification request asks whether a term names a structure or its function. Translation should not erase uncertainty.

Practice and evidence: Draft a bilingual student dialogue about the function of a cell membrane.

Friday: Publish a multilingual diagram

Focus: Publish a multilingual diagram.

Learn: Clear multilingual science uses accurate labels, alt-text, checked meanings and measured qualifications.

Practice and evidence: Submit a bilingual cell diagram and glossary. Rubric: terms 6, science 6, access 4, revision 4.
