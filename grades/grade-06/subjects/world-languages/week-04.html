Week 04 · World Languages & Global Culture

Percent, Proportion, and Civic Decisions

How can percentages help a community make informed decisions?

Monday: Percent vocabulary

Focus: Percent vocabulary.

Learn: Percentages describe parts of a whole. Spanish porcentaje means percentage, mitad means half, and de cada cien means out of every hundred. Check local usage when translating.

Practice and evidence: Prepare a bilingual glossary and describe 25%, 50%, and 75% of a fictional class survey using numbers and clear units.

Tuesday: Survey questions in context

Focus: Survey questions in context.

Learn: A neutral survey question allows a person to answer freely. Spanish ¿Cuál prefieres? asks which you prefer. Always identify the response choices and avoid assumptions about people's cultures.

Practice and evidence: Write a bilingual fictional survey about preferred library activities with clear, optional response categories.

Wednesday: Read a results table

Focus: Read a results table.

Learn: In a simulated survey of 20 people, 12 choose reading, 5 choose music, and 3 choose art: 60%, 25%, and 15%. Translation should preserve totals and meaning.

Practice and evidence: Create a labeled bilingual results table and confirm the percentages sum to 100%.

Thursday: Respectful cultural comparison

Focus: Respectful cultural comparison.

Learn: One class survey cannot characterize an entire language community. A respectful comparison names its sample and avoids statements like everyone in a country prefers a specific activity.

Practice and evidence: Correct two exaggerated statements about fictional survey findings; explain why the sample limits the conclusion.

Friday: Bilingual survey report

Focus: Bilingual survey report.

Learn: A strong report pairs accurate numerical evidence with useful language, audience awareness, and an honest limitation.

Practice and evidence: Submit a six-line bilingual survey presentation with a three-row results table and a note about the fictional sample. Rubric: language 6, accuracy 5, interpretation 5, reflection 4.
