Khaemenes Academy · Grade 6 Mathematics · Week 27

Trade Routes, Migration, and Exchange: Statistics

How do measures of center and spread support careful comparisons?

Learning goals

Calculate mean, median, mode, and range, compare small data distributions, create displays, and distinguish claims supported by a sample from claims about all historical exchange. All route data are invented for practice.

Monday — Mean of route data

A fictional trading study lists travel times of 4, 6, 8, 10 and 12 days. The mean is (4+6+8+10+12)/5=8 days. A mean summarizes the sample but does not describe each trip.

Practice: calculate the mean of 3, 5, 7, 9, 11; describe the unit and sample.

Tuesday — Median and mode

A simulated exchange survey lists 2, 3, 3, 5, 12 items. Its median is 3 and mode is 3; its mean is 5. The high value of 12 pulls the mean upward. Order values before locating the median.

Practice: find mean, median, mode of 4, 4, 6, 8, 18; compare the measures.

Wednesday — Range and spread

Two hypothetical routes have travel times A: 5, 6, 7, 8, 9 and B: 2, 4, 7, 10, 12. Both have median 7 but ranges 4 and 10 respectively. Spread matters when planning travel reliability.

Practice: compute the range of 3, 5, 8, 11, 13 and describe why range alone is limited.

Thursday — Distribution and fair comparisons

Suppose Route A has 4, 6, 8, 10 days and Route B has 7, 7, 7, 7. Both means are 7 days, but Route B has no variation in this fictional sample. A data table or dot plot can reveal patterns that averages conceal.

Practice: compare medians and ranges, draw dot plots, and state a limitation of small samples.

Friday — Evidence defense

A: mean of 4, 6, 8. B: median of 2, 3, 5, 9, 20. C: range of 3, 7, 12, 15. D: explain why samples with equal means may have different ranges and what that means for a fictional route.

Paper exercises remain formative; the independent randomized verified examination records mastery.

Answer key

Reveal after practice

Monday: 7 days. Tuesday: mean 8, median 6, mode 4. Wednesday: 10 days. Thursday: both medians 7; Route A range 6, Route B range 0. Friday: mean 6, median 5, range 12.
