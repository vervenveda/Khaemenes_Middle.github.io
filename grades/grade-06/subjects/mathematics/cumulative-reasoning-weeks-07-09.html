Week 07 · cumulative retrieval

Coordinate planes and geography

Read ordered pairs as (x, y), use signed coordinates, and reason carefully about quadrants, reflections, and map scale.

Write a method, units where appropriate, and a check in your own notebook. Return after two days and seven days to test retention.

Challenge 1

Where is the point (−4, 3)? Enter quadrant I, II, III, or IV.

Show worked reasoning after trying

Negative x and positive y place the point in Quadrant II.

Challenge 2

Reflect the point (3, −5) over the x-axis. Enter the new y-coordinate.

Show worked reasoning after trying

A reflection across the x-axis changes the sign of y but leaves x unchanged: (3, 5).

Challenge 3

Point A is (−2, −1). Point B is (4, −1). How many units apart horizontally?

Show worked reasoning after trying

The x-coordinate changes by 4 − (−2) = 6. Both points share y = −1.

Challenge 4

From (−3, 2), move four units right and five down. What is the new x-coordinate?

Show worked reasoning after trying

Horizontal movement changes x: −3 + 4 = 1; the full resulting point is (1, −3).

Challenge 5

A map uses 1 coordinate unit = 2 km. Two points have the same y-value and x-values 1 and 6. How many km apart?

Show worked reasoning after trying

Their horizontal separation is 5 coordinate units, so 5 × 2 = 10 km.

Week 08 · cumulative retrieval

Expressions, variables, and equivalence

Translate words into expressions, evaluate with substitution, and recognize expressions that remain equal for every variable value.

Write a method, units where appropriate, and a check in your own notebook. Return after two days and seven days to test retention.

Challenge 1

Evaluate 3x + 4 when x = 5.

Show worked reasoning after trying

Substitute x = 5: 3 × 5 + 4 = 19.

Challenge 2

Expand 4(y + 3). What is the constant term in the equivalent expression?

Show worked reasoning after trying

Distribute: 4y + 12. Multiplying only y by four would be incorrect.

Challenge 3

Simplify 2a + 5a − 3. What is the coefficient of a?

Show worked reasoning after trying

Combine like terms: 2a + 5a = 7a; the constant −3 is separate.

Challenge 4

Evaluate 2x² when x = −3.

Show worked reasoning after trying

Square −3 first: (−3)² = 9, then multiply by 2 to get 18.

Challenge 5

A taxi charges $4 plus $2 per mile. How much for 7 miles? Enter dollars.

Show worked reasoning after trying

Use 4 + 2m with m = 7: $4 + $14 = $18.

Show worked reasoning after trying

Reflection across y-axis changes the sign of x, yielding (2, 4).

Week 09 · cumulative retrieval

Equations and balanced arguments

Solve by inverse operations and test every solution in the original equation, with a short written defense.

Write a method, units where appropriate, and a check in your own notebook. Return after two days and seven days to test retention.

Challenge 1

Solve x + 9 = 23.

Show worked reasoning after trying

Subtract 9 from both sides. Check: 14 + 9 = 23.

Challenge 2

Solve 5x = 45.

Show worked reasoning after trying

Divide both sides by 5. Check: 5 × 9 = 45.

Challenge 3

Solve y/4 = 7.

Show worked reasoning after trying

Multiply both sides by 4. Check: 28 ÷ 4 = 7.

Challenge 4

Solve 3 + x = −2.

Show worked reasoning after trying

Subtract 3 from both sides: x = −5. Check: 3 + (−5) = −2.

Challenge 5

A student solves 2x + 6 = 20 as x = 10. Is the answer correct? Enter yes or no.

Show worked reasoning after trying

Subtract six first: 2x = 14, then divide by two: x = 7. Checking 2(10)+6 gives 26, not 20.

Earlier-learning retrieval 6

Retrieval from Week 08: Evaluate 2a + 3 when a = −4.

Show worked reasoning after trying

2(−4) + 3 = −8 + 3 = −5.
