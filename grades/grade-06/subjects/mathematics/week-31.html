Khaemenes Academy · Grade 6 Mathematics · Week 31

Arts, Media, and Message Design: Rational Numbers

How do signed quantities describe changes in a designed composition?

Learning goals

Calculate signed integer, decimal, and fraction changes; model number-line distance; average signed deviations; and distinguish model values from real-world physical measurements. Examples are hypothetical classroom design settings.

Monday — Signed creative adjustments

A fictional image editor sets brightness at −3 units, then raises it by 7: −3+7=4. A negative setting is relative to the chosen zero, not a negative amount of light.

Practice: −5+8, 4−9, and explain both answers on a number line.

Tuesday — Decimal changes

A sound-mixing exercise starts a fictional level at −2.5 units and raises it by 4.0, reaching +1.5. Find signed change using final minus initial.

Practice: compute change from −3.5 to +2.5 and from +1.5 to −2.0.

Wednesday — Fractions on the number line

A layout offset starts at −3/4 of a grid unit and shifts +1/2: −3/4+1/2=−1/4. Convert fractions to fourths and justify the signed result.

Practice: −2/3+1/6 and +3/4−5/4.

Thursday — Average variation

Five hypothetical soundtrack adjustments are −2, +1, +4, −1, −2 units. Their sum is 0, so average adjustment is 0. This does not mean no individual track was adjusted.

Practice: calculate mean of −3,+2,+1,+4, and explain why a mean hides variation.

Friday — Independent defense

A: −4.5+7. B: distance from −3.5 to +2.5. C: −3/4+1/2. D: find mean of −2,+1,+3,−2 and state a limitation of interpreting the mean.

The review is formative; the separate saved randomized examination controls verified mastery.

Answer key

Check after practicing

Monday: +3 and −5. Tuesday: +6.0 and −3.5. Wednesday: −1/2 and −1/2. Thursday: mean +1 unit. Friday: 2.5; 6; −1/4; 0.
