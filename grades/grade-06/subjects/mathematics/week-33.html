Khaemenes Academy · Grade 6 Mathematics · Week 33

Technology, Data, and Ethical Creation: Expressions

How can algebra help us test and critique a computational model?

Learning goals

Translate algorithm descriptions into expressions, evaluate variables, distribute multiplication, combine like terms, and test competing models. All timing data are fictional; calculations do not replace real usability or accessibility testing.

Monday — Variables in algorithms

A fictional accessibility tool spends 4 seconds checking each of n files and 6 seconds initializing: T=4n+6. For n=5, T=26 seconds. Variables represent changing inputs and coefficients represent rates.

Practice: write 3 seconds per file plus 8 startup seconds, then evaluate for 7 files.

Tuesday — Equivalent expressions and debugging

A program's hypothetical time estimate is 3(x+4). Distribute correctly: 3x+12, not 3x+4. Check equivalence by substituting x=5: both correct forms yield 27.

Practice: expand 4(y+3) and 2(3a+5). Explain how substitution detects a bug.

Wednesday — Combining like terms

A fictional processing log has 5a+2a−3 units. The variable terms combine to 7a−3. Do not combine a variable term with a constant.

Practice: simplify 6p+3p+4 and 8m−3m+2+5.

Thursday — Compare data-tool models

Two simulated export processes take 7t+12 and 5t+20 seconds. They tie for t=4, but differ at other input sizes. Compare at t=3 and t=6; identify units and assumptions instead of claiming any algorithm is always faster.

Practice: construct a labeled comparison table and explain why one matching data point is insufficient.

Friday — Independent model review

A: express 5 seconds per record plus 9 seconds setup. B: expand 4(x+2). C: simplify 6a+2a−5. D: compare 3n+8 with 5n+2 at n=4 and identify a limitation.

This review is formative. The separate randomized assessment controls verified progression.

Answer key

Review after completing the practice

Monday: 3f+8; 29 seconds. Tuesday: 4y+12 and 6a+10. Wednesday: 9p+4 and 5m+7. Thursday: at t=3, 33 versus 35 seconds; at t=6, 54 versus 50 seconds. Friday: 5r+9; 4x+8; 8a−5; 20 versus 22.
