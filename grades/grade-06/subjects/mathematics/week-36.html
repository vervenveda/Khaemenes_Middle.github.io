Khaemenes Academy · Grade 6 Mathematics · Week 36

Final Review and Grade 7 Readiness: Statistics

What mathematical evidence demonstrates readiness for seventh grade?

Learning goals

Calculate and interpret mean, median, mode and range; compare distributions; revisit ratio, rational-number, equation and geometric models; and prepare a defensible learning portfolio. These fictional practice results are not individual ability labels.

Monday — Mean and median review

A fictional class records practice times 4, 6, 8, 10 and 12 minutes. Mean is 40÷5=8 minutes, and median is the middle value 8. These measures describe the sample, not every learner.

Practice: find mean and median for 3, 5, 7, 9, 11 and label units.

Tuesday — Mode, range and outliers

A review set 2, 3, 3, 5, 12 has median and mode 3, mean 5, and range 10. The outlying 12 influences the mean more than the median.

Practice: find mean, median, mode, and range for 4, 4, 6, 8, 18.

Wednesday — Compare readiness data fairly

Fictional practice groups A: 5, 6, 7, 8, 9 and B: 2, 4, 7, 10, 12 both have median 7, while their ranges are 4 and 10. The greater spread shows different variability, not that one group is more capable.

Practice: draw two labeled dot plots; find both means and ranges and explain one limitation of the comparison.

Thursday — Cumulative Grade 6 bridge

Review: 3:5 equivalent to 12:20; 25% of 80 = 20; −3+8 = 5; solve 3x+6=21 to obtain x=5; the volume of 2×3×4 prism is 24 cubic units. Explain how a number line, proportion, inverse operation or labeled solid checks an answer.

Practice: create one original problem for ratios, rational numbers, equations and geometry; solve and check each.

Friday — Grade 7 readiness defense

Present a brief portfolio with one accurate statistical comparison, one algebraic solution, one geometry calculation, a corrected past mistake, and a plan for further practice. Independently complete the saved weekly mastery examination and, when eligible, the separate cumulative closed-book final.

Portfolio reflection is formative and cannot substitute for verified weekly mastery or the cumulative final exam.

Answer key

Reveal after completing practice

Monday: 7 minutes for both. Tuesday: mean 8, median 6, mode 4, range 14. Wednesday: both means 7; ranges 4 and 10. Thursday: 12:20, 20, 5, 5, 24 cubic units.
