Week 19 · retrieval and transfer

Visual-media ratios and rates

Write your method, identify units and model limitations, and revisit after two and seven days.

Challenge 1

30 light pixels and 20 dark: simplified light:dark ratio?

Worked reasoning after attempting

Divide both parts of 30:20 by ten.

Challenge 2

18 red and 12 blue pixels: fraction red out of total?

Worked reasoning after attempting

18/30 simplifies to 3/5. Do not confuse red:blue with red:total.

Challenge 3

A 4 cm × 6 cm image doubles each dimension. New width in cm?

Worked reasoning after attempting

The original width is 6 cm. Double it to 12 cm; both axes must scale.

Challenge 4

A 90-frame clip lasts 3 seconds. Average frames per second?

Worked reasoning after attempting

90 ÷ 3 = 30 frames/s.

Challenge 5

Does a cropped graph alone prove a claim about all images? yes/no

Worked reasoning after attempting

A crop can conceal context and sample selection.

Prior learning 6

Recall Week 18: values −3,0,3 have what range?

Worked reasoning after attempting

Maximum minus minimum is 3−(−3)=6.

Week 20 · retrieval and transfer

Cell-sample percentages

Write your method, identify units and model limitations, and revisit after two and seven days.

Challenge 1

24 marked cells of 80 sampled: what percent?

Worked reasoning after attempting

24/80=0.30=30%, describing the sample only.

Challenge 2

What is 15% of 200 cells?

Worked reasoning after attempting

0.15×200=30.

Challenge 3

Sample A has 12/40 and B has 18/90 selected. Which has a larger share, A or B?

Worked reasoning after attempting

A is 30%, B is 20%. Compare proportions, not counts.

Challenge 4

45% of 60: how many?

Worked reasoning after attempting

0.45×60=27.

Challenge 5

Does a 20-cell image alone establish the share in an entire organism? yes/no

Worked reasoning after attempting

A selected or small sample may not be representative.

Prior learning 6

Recall Week 19: simplify 20:30; give first term.

Worked reasoning after attempting

20:30 simplifies to 2:3.

Week 21 · retrieval and transfer

Fraction division and portions

Write your method, identify units and model limitations, and revisit after two and seven days.

Challenge 1

How many 1/4-liter portions fit in 3 liters?

Worked reasoning after attempting

3÷(1/4)=12.

Challenge 2

How many 1/8-liter portions fit in 3/4 liter?

Worked reasoning after attempting

3/4=6/8 so six portions fit.

Challenge 3

Compute 2/3 ÷ 1/6.

Worked reasoning after attempting

2/3×6=4; check 4×1/6=2/3.

Challenge 4

A 2.5-liter container fills 1/2-liter cups. How many full cups?

Worked reasoning after attempting

2.5÷0.5=5.

Challenge 5

Can a fractional quotient alone establish energy transfer in an ecosystem? yes/no

Worked reasoning after attempting

The division is a mathematical model, not causal ecological evidence.

Prior learning 6

Recall Week 20: 25% of 80?

Worked reasoning after attempting

One quarter of 80 is 20.
