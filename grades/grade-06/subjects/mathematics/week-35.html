Khaemenes Academy · Grade 6 Mathematics · Week 35

Capstone Studio and Oral Defense: Surface Area and Volume

How can geometry defend the design and material choices of a prototype?

Learning goals

Calculate rectangle and triangle area, rectangular- and triangular-prism volume, and surface area of closed and open-top structures. Prepare a concise oral defense with labeled nets, units, verification, and limitations. All dimensions are fictional practice models.

Monday — Build a dimensioned prototype

A fictional presentation display has rectangular base 6 cm by 4 cm and height 3 cm. The footprint area is 24 cm², while interior volume is 72 cm³. Area uses square units; volume uses cubic units.

Practice: find footprint and volume of a 5×3×4 cm model, label units and sketch all dimensions.

Tuesday — Surface area and nets

A closed model box 4×3×2 cm needs 2(12+8+6)=52 cm² of exterior material. Draw six faces and identify three pairs of congruent rectangles.

Practice: calculate exterior area for a closed 5×2×3 cm prism and sketch a net.

Wednesday — Triangular-prism displays

A right-triangular base has legs 3 cm and 4 cm, so its area is 6 cm². With prism length 10 cm, the volume is 60 cm³.

Practice: calculate volume of a triangular prism with base 6 cm, triangle height 4 cm and prism length 5 cm.

Thursday — Critique a prototype assumption

A fictional open-top rectangular container 6×4×2 m has volume 48 m³ but external face area excluding top 24+24+16=64 m². Ask which faces need material and whether thickness, waste or supports have been omitted.

Practice: calculate open-top surface area and volume for a 5×3×2 m model; propose a design revision.

Friday — Oral mathematical defense

Explain a model's dimensions, formula choices, square versus cubic units, one worked surface-area calculation and one worked volume calculation. Verify a numerical result by an alternative method or a face-by-face net.

This presentation and paper work are formative. The separate randomized examination determines verified mastery.

Answer key

Review after independent work

Monday: 15 cm² footprint; 60 cm³ volume. Tuesday: 62 cm². Wednesday: 60 cm³. Thursday: 47 m² of outer faces excluding top; 30 m³ capacity. Friday: answers depend on the chosen model; require correctly labeled calculations and a defended assumption.
