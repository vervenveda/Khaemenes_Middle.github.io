Week 25 · Equations, inequality and fair constraints

Practice without the key, show reasoning, and review after two days and seven days.

Reasoning challenge 1

Solve 3x+5=23.

Show worked reasoning after trying

Subtract five then divide by three; verify 3×6+5=23.

Reasoning challenge 2

Solve x−7=12.

Show worked reasoning after trying

Add seven to both sides.

Reasoning challenge 3

For x≤4, does 4 satisfy the condition? yes/no

Show worked reasoning after trying

The inclusive boundary permits equality.

Reasoning challenge 4

Solve −2x>8. What is the excluded boundary?

Show worked reasoning after trying

Dividing by a negative reverses the sign, producing x<−4.

Reasoning challenge 5

A budget allows at most 30 units, with 6 fixed costs. Max variable units?

Show worked reasoning after trying

6+x≤30, so x≤24.

Week 26 · Surface area and volume of designs

Practice without the key, show reasoning, and review after two days and seven days.

Reasoning challenge 1

Floor 8 m by 5 m: area in m²?

Show worked reasoning after trying

Multiply the two planar dimensions.

Reasoning challenge 2

Prism 8×5×3 m: volume in m³?

Show worked reasoning after trying

Multiply all three lengths: 8×5×3.

Reasoning challenge 3

Closed box 2×3×4 cm: total surface area cm²?

Show worked reasoning after trying

2(6+8+12)=52 cm².

Reasoning challenge 4

Open-top 2×3×4 box: surface area cm²?

Show worked reasoning after trying

Subtract top face 2×3=6 from 52.

Reasoning challenge 5

Triangular prism with triangle area 12 cm² and length 5 cm: volume cm³?

Show worked reasoning after trying

12×5=60 cubic centimeters.

Week 27 · Center, spread and fair sample claims

Practice without the key, show reasoning, and review after two days and seven days.

Reasoning challenge 1

Mean of 4,6,8,10,12?

Show worked reasoning after trying

Sum 40 divided by five is 8.

Reasoning challenge 2

Median of 2,3,9,10,20?

Show worked reasoning after trying

The third ordered observation is 9.

Reasoning challenge 3

Range of 5,7,7,15?

Show worked reasoning after trying

Maximum minus minimum = 15−5.

Reasoning challenge 4

Mode of 2,4,4,5,6?

Show worked reasoning after trying

Four occurs more often than any other value.

Reasoning challenge 5

Does a selected sample of five people prove every person's experience? yes/no

Show worked reasoning after trying

A small or biased sample cannot justify a universal statement.
