Show worked reasoning after trying

At 5 cards per minute, cards in 8 minutes?

Week 29 · Percent comparisons and research claims

Practice without the key, show reasoning, and review after two days and seven days.

Reasoning challenge 1

18 of 24 citations checked: percent?

Show worked reasoning after trying

18÷24=0.75=75%.

Reasoning challenge 2

12 of 20 sources checked: percent?

Show worked reasoning after trying

12÷20=0.60.

Reasoning challenge 3

Sample A 15/30, sample B 16/40: which share higher, A or B?

Show worked reasoning after trying

50% exceeds 40% even though B has more selected items.

Reasoning challenge 4

Price rises from $40 to $50: percent increase?

Show worked reasoning after trying

Increase is 10/40=25%, not 10/50.

Reasoning challenge 5

Rate changes from 20% to 30%: change in percentage points?

Show worked reasoning after trying

30−20=10 percentage points, distinct from relative percentage change.

Week 30 · Fraction division and argumentation

Practice without the key, show reasoning, and review after two days and seven days.

Reasoning challenge 1

3 minutes divided into 1/2-minute speaking turns: how many?

Show worked reasoning after trying

3÷1/2=6 equal turns.

Reasoning challenge 2

2/3 ÷ 1/6?

Show worked reasoning after trying

Multiply 2/3 by the reciprocal 6.

Reasoning challenge 3

1 1/2 minutes divided into 1/4-minute turns: how many?

Show worked reasoning after trying

1.5÷0.25=6.

Reasoning challenge 4

2 1/4 liters divided into 1/2-liter units: how many full units?

Show worked reasoning after trying

Exact quotient is 4.5, so only four full units fit.

Reasoning challenge 5

Does 3/4 ÷ 1/8 equal 6? yes/no

Show worked reasoning after trying

3/4=6/8, so six eighths fit.
