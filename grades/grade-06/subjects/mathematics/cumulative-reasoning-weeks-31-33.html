Week 31 · Signed rational values in creative models

Explain every method and correct misconceptions. Repeat practice after two and seven days.

Challenge 1

A brightness setting begins −3 and increases by 7. Result?

Show worked reasoning after trying

−3+7=4, relative to the chosen reference.

Challenge 2

A parameter moves from +2.5 to −1.5. Signed change?

Show worked reasoning after trying

Final minus initial = −1.5−2.5=−4.

Challenge 3

Evaluate −3/4 + 1/4. Enter a fraction.

Show worked reasoning after trying

Combine fourths: −2/4 = −1/2.

Challenge 4

Mean of signed adjustments −3,1,2?

Show worked reasoning after trying

Sum is zero; average zero does not mean there was no variation.

Challenge 5

Which is greater, −0.6 or −0.2?

Show worked reasoning after trying

−0.2 lies right of −0.6 on the number line.

Earlier-learning retrieval 6

Recall Week 30: 1 ÷ 1/8?

Show worked reasoning after trying

Eight eighth-size portions fit in one.

Return to Week 31

Week 32 · Planning-grid coordinate reasoning

Explain every method and correct misconceptions. Repeat practice after two and seven days.

Challenge 1

What quadrant contains (3,−4)? Roman numeral.

Show worked reasoning after trying

Positive x and negative y define Quadrant IV.

Challenge 2

Reflect (−2,5) across x-axis: new y?

Show worked reasoning after trying

Flip y sign only.

Challenge 3

Reflect (−2,5) across y-axis: new x?

Show worked reasoning after trying

Flip x sign only.

Challenge 4

From (−4,2) to (3,2), horizontal distance?

Show worked reasoning after trying

Absolute difference |3−(−4)|=7.

Challenge 5

Does a coordinate on a fictional planning grid represent medical diagnosis? yes/no

Show worked reasoning after trying

The exercise represents arbitrary positions, not diagnostic evidence.

Earlier-learning retrieval 6

Recall Week 31: −2.5+1.5?

Show worked reasoning after trying

Combine signed changes to obtain −1.

Return to Week 32

Week 33 · Algorithmic expressions and debugging

Explain every method and correct misconceptions. Repeat practice after two and seven days.

Challenge 1

A tool takes 4 seconds per file plus 6 setup seconds. Time for 5 files?

Show worked reasoning after trying

T=4n+6; at n=5, T=26 seconds.

Challenge 2

Expand 3(n+2): constant term?

Show worked reasoning after trying

Distribute 3 across n and 2.

Challenge 3

Simplify 5x+2x−4: coefficient of x?

Show worked reasoning after trying

Collect like terms, giving 7x−4.

Challenge 4

Evaluate 2x² at x=−3.

Show worked reasoning after trying

2×(−3)²=2×9=18.

Challenge 5

Models A=3n+8 and B=5n. For n=5, which is faster (smaller), A or B?

Show worked reasoning after trying

At n=5, A=23 and B=25; A is the smaller modeled time.

Earlier-learning retrieval 6

Recall Week 32: reflect (−4,3) across y-axis; new x?

Show worked reasoning after trying

Reflection changes the sign of x.
