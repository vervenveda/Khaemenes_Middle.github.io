Khaemenes Academy · Grade 6 Mathematics · Week 24

Ancient River Civilizations: Algebraic Expressions

How can expressions describe and compare simplified resource models?

Learning goals

Translate verbal rules into expressions, evaluate variables, apply the distributive property, combine like terms, and critique mathematical assumptions. Scenarios are fictional models and not historical claims.

Monday — Variables for river planning

A fictional settlement stores 4 baskets for each of n farms, plus 6 shared baskets. Write 4n+6. For n=5, storage is 26 baskets. Variables name changing quantities; coefficients scale them, and constants stay fixed.

Practice: write an expression for 3 containers per household plus 8 communal containers; evaluate at 7 households.

Tuesday — Equivalent expressions

A hypothetical irrigation model uses 3(x+4) water units. Distribute to obtain 3x+12. Both expressions produce 27 when x=5. A model's equivalence is algebraic; it does not prove historical measurements.

Practice: expand 4(y+3) and 2(3a+5). Verify one with substitution.

Wednesday — Combine like terms

A pretend granary receives 5a sacks then 2a sacks and uses 3 sacks: 5a+2a−3=7a−3. You can combine a-terms, but a constant cannot be added as if it were a-quantity.

Practice: simplify 6p+3p+4 and 8m−3m+2+5.

Thursday — Translate and critique models

A fictional transport route charges 7 units per trip plus a 12-unit fixed cost, represented by 7t+12. A second plan 5t+20 costs the same at t=4, but differs for other trip counts. An expression describes assumptions, not a verified account of ancient river civilizations.

Practice: evaluate both at t=3 and t=6; compare and explain.

Friday — Independent reasoning

A (2 points): write 5 baskets per family plus 9 common baskets. B (2 points): expand 4(x+2). C (3 points): simplify 6a+2a−5. D (3 points): compare 3n+8 and 5n+2 when n=4, showing calculations and assumptions.

Ten-point formative work does not unlock lessons; only the separate saved randomized examination verifies mastery.

Answer key

Open after trying each problem
