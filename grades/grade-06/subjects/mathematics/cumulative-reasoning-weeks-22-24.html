Week 24 · retrieval and transfer

Challenge 1

Worked reasoning after attempting

4×5+6=26.

Challenge 2

Worked reasoning after attempting

Distribute 3 to both x and 4: 3x+12.

Challenge 3

Simplify 2a+5a−3: coefficient of a?

Worked reasoning after attempting

Combine only like terms to obtain 7a−3.

Challenge 4

Worked reasoning after attempting

Challenge 5

Worked reasoning after attempting

6×4+2=26.
