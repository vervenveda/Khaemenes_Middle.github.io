Khaemenes Academy · Grade 6 Mathematics · Week 28

World Languages and Cultural Communication: Ratios and Rates

How can proportional comparisons describe communication-project data?

Learning goals

Simplify part-to-part and part-to-whole ratios, build equivalent ratios, calculate unit rates, compare proportions and identify limits of fictional samples. All counts represent imaginary classroom activities, not judgments about languages or people.

Monday — Language sample ratios

A fictional language club recorded 18 greeting cards with drawings and 12 without. Drawings-to-no-drawings is 18:12 = 3:2; drawings-to-total is 18:30 = 3:5. Ratios require a named numerator and denominator.

Practice: compare 24 translated labels with 16 untranslated labels using part-to-part and part-to-whole ratios.

Tuesday — Equivalent ratios

A bilingual classroom display contains 3 pictograms for every 5 text labels. With 12 pictograms, the equivalent number of labels is 20 because 3:5 = 12:20. Multiplying both quantities preserves the ratio.

Practice: scale 2:7 to a first quantity of 10, and explain why adding the same number to both terms generally fails.

Wednesday — Unit rates

A fictional transcription team labels 48 items in 6 minutes, an average of 8 items per minute. At an unchanged rate it could label 80 in 10 minutes. Unit rates assume sustained conditions, so predictions should be qualified.

Practice: compute 63 labels in 7 minutes and the prediction for 12 minutes.

Thursday — Compare communication models

Fictional practice group A accurately matches 18 of 24 cards (75%); group B matches 21 of 35 (60%). Comparing successes alone favors B, but comparing proportions favors A. These exercises do not judge real languages or cultures.

Practice: compare 15 of 20 and 18 of 30; identify fair denominators and limits.

Friday — Formative explanation

Solve: 20:30 in simplest terms; 4:7 = 20:?; 56 cards/7 minutes; and compare 12/16 with 15/25. Explain the quantities, assumptions, and one limitation of the model.

Formative review supports readiness only. The saved randomized examination records verified mastery.

Answer key

Open after attempting the practice

Monday: 3:2 and 3:5. Tuesday: 10:35. Wednesday: 9 labels/min; 108 labels. Thursday: 75% versus 60%. Friday: 2:3; 35; 8 cards/min; 75% versus 60%.
