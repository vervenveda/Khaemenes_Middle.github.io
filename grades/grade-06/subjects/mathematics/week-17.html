Khaemenes Academy · Grade 6 Mathematics · Week 17

Midyear Portfolio and Mathematical Defense

How can I prove growth using calculations, evidence, and corrected work?

Learning targets

Retrieve work from the first sixteen weeks, defend mathematical choices, revise earlier errors, and demonstrate readiness for later cumulative assessment. This is a portfolio checkpoint, not a substitute for the separate course midterm.

Monday — Select portfolio evidence

Choose one piece of work from ratios, signed numbers, expressions, or equations and explain the initial question, method, and corrected reasoning. For example, 3:5 scales to 12:20 by multiplying both terms by four. An answer alone is not sufficient evidence of learning.

Practice: select two older problems, label the skill, and produce a correction note with calculations.

Tuesday — Geometry and measurement defense

A box with dimensions 6 cm × 4 cm × 3 cm has volume 72 cm³, but its closed surface area is 2(24+18+12)=108 cm². Defend why square units and cubic units cannot be substituted. A triangle with base 8 cm and perpendicular height 5 cm has area 20 cm².

Practice: sketch a prism, calculate both measures, and explain one common mistake.

Wednesday — Statistical and graphical reasoning

For observations 2, 4, 4, 6, 9, mean is 5, median is 4, mode is 4 and range is 7. Give a defensible statistical claim, and describe the limits of a small sample. A graph must use labeled, equally spaced axes.

Practice: defend why a single mean does not describe every value; sketch a labeled plot.

Thursday — Revisit applied equations and models

A cart moving at a modeled 5 m/s for 7 seconds travels 35 meters. An inequality such as 12 + 6t ≤ 48 limits whole-number trays to at most six. Explain what is measured, what is modeled, and when only whole numbers are sensible.

Practice: compare the motion equation and the budget inequality; include substitution checks.

Friday — Portfolio defense and formative review

Complete a ten-point review: ratio equivalence (2 points), a signed-number equation (2 points), a labeled area/volume comparison (3 points), and a statistical or rate claim supported by numerical evidence (3 points). For each, show an earlier attempt, a correction or validation, and clear units.

Teacher-reviewed evidence is formative. Week 17's saved assessment retains its separate 80% mastery requirement; the course midterm remains a separate examination controlled by the existing program schedule.

Self-check

Answers and notes

Ratios 3:5 and 12:20 are equivalent. Prism surface area is 108 cm²; volume is 72 cm³. Dataset 2,4,4,6,9 has mean 5, median 4, mode 4 and range 7. Distance at 5 m/s for 7 s is 35 m. Budget inequality 12 + 6t ≤ 48 yields t ≤ 6 whole trays.
