Khaemenes Academy · Grade 6 Mathematics · Week 26

Classical Civilizations and Civic Ideas: Surface Area and Volume

How can geometric models help us estimate space and materials?

Learning goals

Distinguish area from volume, apply rectangular- and triangular-prism formulas, reason with nets, and model open versus closed structures. Architectural contexts are hypothetical and not claims about actual ancient measurements.

Monday — Area versus volume

A fictional public hall floor measures 8 m by 5 m. Its floor area is 40 m². If the hall is 3 m tall, the rectangular interior has a volume of 8×5×3=120 m³. Square units describe a surface; cubic units describe space.

Practice: calculate floor area and volume of a 6×4×3 m model room; explain the units.

Tuesday — Nets and surface area

A closed rectangular model box measures 4 cm by 3 cm by 2 cm. Its surface area is 2(4×3+4×2+3×2)=52 cm². Draw its six faces and label equal opposite rectangles.

Practice: find surface area of a closed 5×2×3 cm prism; explain why each face is counted twice.

Wednesday — Triangular and rectangular prisms

A triangular-prism model has a right-triangle base with legs 3 cm and 4 cm, giving base area 6 cm². With prism length 10 cm, volume is 60 cm³. For a triangular prism V=base area×length.

Practice: find the volume when a triangle's base is 6 cm, height 4 cm, and prism length 5 cm.

Thursday — Building material models

An open-top rectangular model reservoir measures 6 m by 4 m by 2 m. Its capacity is 48 m³. Its exterior area excluding the top is 6×4+2(6×2)+2(4×2)=64 m². Specify whether a structure is open or closed before calculating materials.

Practice: calculate an open-top 5×3×2 m box's volume and surface area; distinguish area from capacity.

Friday — Evidence defense

A: calculate volume of 4×3×5 cm prism. B: find surface area of closed 2×3×4 cm prism. C: volume of triangular prism with triangle base 8 cm, height 3 cm, and length 6 cm. D: compare a model's surface area with its volume and explain why the numbers use different units.

Formative review does not award mastery; the separate randomized assessment checks verified mathematics and overall proficiency.

Answer key

Reveal after solving

Monday: 24 m² and 72 m³. Tuesday: 62 cm². Wednesday: 60 cm³. Thursday: 30 m³ and 47 m². Friday: 60 cm³; 52 cm²; 72 cm³; square units versus cubic units.
