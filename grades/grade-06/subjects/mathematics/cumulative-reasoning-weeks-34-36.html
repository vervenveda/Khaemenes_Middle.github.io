Week 34 · Capstone equations and inequalities

Explain every method and correct misconceptions. Repeat practice after two and seven days.

Challenge 1

Solve 4x+7=27.

Show worked reasoning after trying

Subtract seven and divide by four. Verify 4×5+7=27.

Challenge 2

Budget 35 units, setup 7, each item 4. Max whole items?

Show worked reasoning after trying

7+4n≤35 gives n≤7.

Challenge 3

Does n=6 satisfy n<6? yes/no

Show worked reasoning after trying

Strict inequality excludes equality.

Challenge 4

Solve −3x≥12: largest boundary x?

Show worked reasoning after trying

Divide by −3 and reverse sign: x≤−4.

Challenge 5

Project price 12 each and 5 setup. Total for 3?

Show worked reasoning after trying

12×3+5=41.

Week 36 · Final statistics and Grade 7 bridge

Explain every method and correct misconceptions. Repeat practice after two and seven days.

Challenge 1

Show worked reasoning after trying

Challenge 2

Median of 3,4,6,9,20?

Show worked reasoning after trying

Middle ordered value is 6.

Challenge 3

Mode of 2,2,3,5,8?

Show worked reasoning after trying

Two appears twice.

Challenge 4

Range of −2,3,6,10?

Show worked reasoning after trying

Challenge 5

Show worked reasoning after trying
