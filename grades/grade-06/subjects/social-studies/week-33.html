Week 33 · Social Studies & Civics

Technology, Data, and Ethical Creation

How can tools strengthen evidence without replacing judgment?

Monday: Inspect historical databases

Focus: Inspect historical databases.

Learn: Digitized archives are selected records, not complete representations of the past.

Practice and evidence: Describe a fictional archive's inclusion rules and missing voices.

Tuesday: Evaluate source provenance

Focus: Evaluate source provenance.

Learn: Source date, creator, context and translation affect historical meaning.

Practice and evidence: Create metadata cards for two instructor-provided records.

Wednesday: Analyze mapped data

Focus: Analyze mapped data.

Learn: A map can make locations visible but may hide disputed borders and uncertain dates.

Practice and evidence: Annotate a fictional historical data map with uncertainty notes.

Thursday: Examine digital power

Focus: Examine digital power.

Learn: Who controls a database can influence visibility and privacy.

Practice and evidence: Compare fictional policies for public access and learner protection.

Friday: Curate a fair digital exhibit

Focus: Curate a fair digital exhibit.

Learn: Historical exhibits should not treat missing evidence as proof of absence.

Practice and evidence: Prepare two source cards and an ethical-access statement. Rubric: sources 6, context 6, inclusion 4, clarity 4.
