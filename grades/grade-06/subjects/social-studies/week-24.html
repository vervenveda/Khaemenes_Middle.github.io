Week 24 · Social Studies & Civics

Ancient River Civilizations

How did rivers support early civilizations and complex societies?

Monday: Locate early river regions

Focus: Locate early river regions.

Learn: Early urban societies developed in different regions, including Mesopotamia near the Tigris and Euphrates and ancient Egypt along the Nile. Regions and dates should be verified from historical sources.

Practice and evidence: Create a labeled outline map of the Nile, Tigris and Euphrates with a source note.

Tuesday: Compare Indus settlements

Focus: Compare Indus settlements.

Learn: Sites associated with the Indus civilization show planned streets and water-management features. Archaeological interpretations remain incomplete, particularly because the Indus script is undeciphered.

Practice and evidence: Make a two-column chart distinguishing visible archaeological features from hypotheses.

Wednesday: Study early China

Focus: Study early China.

Learn: Communities in the Yellow River region were among foundations of early Chinese civilization; cultural and political developments differed across places and periods.

Practice and evidence: Prepare a timeline with source questions about Neolithic settlements and later states, avoiding invented exact dates.

Thursday: Examine labor, writing and trade

Focus: Examine labor, writing and trade.

Learn: Agriculture, crafts, trade and early systems of recordkeeping interacted with social organization. Surviving records do not represent everyone equally.

Practice and evidence: Compare source types such as inscriptions, artifacts and settlement plans, identifying whose perspective might be missing.

Friday: Present a four-region comparison

Focus: Present a four-region comparison.

Learn: Historical comparison needs accurate geography, qualified chronology, multiple social perspectives and honest source boundaries.

Practice and evidence: Submit a four-region evidence matrix and 180-word comparative brief. Rubric: geography 6, sourcing 6, fairness 4, clarity 4.
