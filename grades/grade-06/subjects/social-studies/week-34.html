Week 34 · Social Studies & Civics

A++ Big Question Capstone Research

What serious question can I investigate with evidence, calculation, and design?

Monday: Choose a civic inquiry

Focus: Choose a civic inquiry.

Learn: Research questions should identify time, place, groups and a feasible evidence base.

Practice and evidence: Frame a fictional public-space access policy investigation.

Tuesday: Evaluate stakeholders

Focus: Evaluate stakeholders.

Learn: A solution should represent differently affected people without claiming one group is uniform.

Practice and evidence: Build a stakeholder-and-needs matrix using fictional accounts.

Wednesday: Distinguish source from interpretation

Focus: Distinguish source from interpretation.

Learn: Policy records establish stated intent, not every outcome.

Practice and evidence: Annotate a simulated historical or civic document with provenance and uncertainty.

Thursday: Assess tradeoffs

Focus: Assess tradeoffs.

Learn: Public choices involve resource constraints, access and competing priorities.

Practice and evidence: Write evidence-based pros, cons and an equity safeguard for a proposal.

Friday: Defend civic context

Focus: Defend civic context.

Learn: A capstone should make its evidence, missing voices and ethical assumptions visible.

Practice and evidence: Present a sourced civic research brief and counterargument. Rubric: context 6, evidence 6, inclusion 4, reasoning 4.
