Week 27 · Social Studies & Civics

Trade Routes, Migration, and Exchange

How do movement and exchange transform societies?

Monday: Locate exchange networks

Focus: Locate exchange networks.

Learn: Historical routes changed through time and connected communities across land and sea.

Practice and evidence: Create a sourced map showing two networks and clearly dated geographic boundaries.

Tuesday: Describe goods and ideas

Focus: Describe goods and ideas.

Learn: Exchange can move objects, technologies, languages and ideas through numerous intermediaries.

Practice and evidence: Trace an example commodity and distinguish verified links from uncertain routes.

Wednesday: Examine diverse migration motives

Focus: Examine diverse migration motives.

Learn: Migration can involve opportunity, environmental pressures, coercion, conflict and family connections.

Practice and evidence: Compare three fictional migration narratives without assuming everyone moved voluntarily.

Thursday: Interpret consequences and inequalities

Focus: Interpret consequences and inequalities.

Learn: Trade may benefit some groups while imposing costs or risks on others.

Practice and evidence: Create a stakeholder matrix showing merchants, workers and residents with mixed outcomes.

Friday: Publish a contextual trade exhibit

Focus: Publish a contextual trade exhibit.

Learn: A responsible exhibit includes dates, sourcing and voices beyond elites.

Practice and evidence: Present three panels on a selected historic network with two reliable sources, an uncertainty note and reflection. Rubric: context 6, evidence 6, inclusion 4, reasoning 4.
