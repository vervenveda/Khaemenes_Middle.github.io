Week 08 · Social Studies & Civics

Expressions and Algebraic Thinking

How can symbols represent ideas in math, language, and culture?

Monday: Symbols and cultural context

Focus: Symbols and cultural context.

Learn: Symbols can express authority, identity, trade, or belief, but their meanings depend on place and source context. Modern classroom symbols must not be presented as authentic historical artifacts.

Practice and evidence: Design a clearly fictional trading-post sign and identify the symbols it uses without assigning unsupported meanings to a culture.

Tuesday: Model a historical quantity

Focus: Model a historical quantity.

Learn: A variable can represent an unknown quantity in an invented historical-style accounting scenario. If each of four fictional caravans carries b baskets, total baskets are 4b.

Practice and evidence: Make a table of totals for b=2,3,5, labeling the situation a simulation.

Wednesday: Evidence versus simulation

Focus: Evidence versus simulation.

Learn: An algebraic model can illuminate a question without proving historical events occurred. Genuine historical claims require records and reliable interpretation.

Practice and evidence: Write a paragraph distinguishing an invented 4b trade model from what would need authentic documentary evidence.

Thursday: Compare representations

Focus: Compare representations.

Learn: A written ledger, drawing, tally marks, and an expression can depict the same quantity in different forms. Their usefulness depends on context and documentation.

Practice and evidence: Represent three equal groups and two extra objects as 3n+2 using drawing, text and symbols.

Friday: Historian's evidence brief

Focus: Historian's evidence brief.

Learn: Responsible historical explanation identifies source status, represents quantities accurately, and admits gaps in evidence.

Practice and evidence: Create a one-page fictional trading-ledger example with 4b, a contextual symbol legend and two historical questions that need research. Rubric: algebra 6, source honesty 6, representation 4, reflection 4.
