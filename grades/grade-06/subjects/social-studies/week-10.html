Week 10 · Social Studies & Civics

Inequalities and Decision Rules

How do constraints shape solutions and decisions?

Monday: Rules and civic responsibilities

Focus: Rules and civic responsibilities.

Learn: A rule sets a condition for behavior, but a public law must have a source, jurisdiction, and enforceable context. A fictional library policy is not automatically an actual legal requirement.

Practice and evidence: Design a fictional reading-room policy with a clear capacity limit and identify whose authority would need to be checked in real life.

Tuesday: Interpret eligibility constraints

Focus: Interpret eligibility constraints.

Learn: An invented grant has a maximum fictional project budget of 100 units: b≤100. This mathematical condition does not imply any real program has that rule.

Practice and evidence: Give three eligible and three ineligible simulated budgets, explaining whether the boundary 100 is included.

Wednesday: Rights and tradeoffs

Focus: Rights and tradeoffs.

Learn: An occupancy policy may advance safety but can also affect access. Decision makers should assess need, fairness, disability access, staffing and workable exceptions.

Practice and evidence: Write a comparison chart with two potential benefits and two concerns for a fictional room limit.

Thursday: Distinguish sources and claims

Focus: Distinguish sources and claims.

Learn: A policy draft, a meeting note, and an actual enacted law are different sources. Claims about legal requirements need verification against official texts.

Practice and evidence: Create three simulated source cards and annotate what each can and cannot prove.

Friday: Balanced policy brief

Focus: Balanced policy brief.

Learn: Responsible civic recommendations explain the rule, affected groups, numerical test cases, rights considerations, and gaps in evidence.

Practice and evidence: Write a one-page fictional reading-room policy brief with b≤100 or capacity≤20, an alternative, and a source-verification note. Rubric: reasoning 6, constraint 5, fairness 5, sourcing 4.
