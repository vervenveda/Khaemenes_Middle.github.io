Week 36 · Social Studies & Civics

Final Review and Grade 7 Readiness

How am I ready for seventh grade?

Monday: Review historical sourcing

Focus: Review historical sourcing.

Learn: Dates, creators, translations and audiences influence historical interpretation.

Practice and evidence: Create provenance cards for two fictional documents from different periods.

Tuesday: Compare exchange and civic institutions

Focus: Compare exchange and civic institutions.

Learn: Trade, migration and law have different effects across places and communities.

Practice and evidence: Build a historical comparison chart without assuming all societies were identical.

Wednesday: Include multiple perspectives

Focus: Include multiple perspectives.

Learn: Surviving records may exclude people with less political or institutional power.

Practice and evidence: Identify one missing voice and a question that could guide further research.

Thursday: Connect evidence to civic reasoning

Focus: Connect evidence to civic reasoning.

Learn: Present-day proposals need transparent tradeoffs, not invented historical precedents.

Practice and evidence: Write a fictional community policy brief with source qualifications.

Friday: Defend historical understanding

Focus: Defend historical understanding.

Learn: Readiness includes fair use of sources and accountable interpretation.

Practice and evidence: Submit two sourced comparisons and a reflection on evidence limits. Rubric: context 6, sources 6, inclusion 4, reasoning 4.
