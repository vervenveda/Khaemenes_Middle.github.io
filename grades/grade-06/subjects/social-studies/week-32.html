Week 32 · Social Studies & Civics

Health, Identity, and Responsible Choices

How do healthy routines support learning and identity?

Monday: Study public health in context

Focus: Study public health in context.

Learn: Community approaches to health and safety differ over time and place; historical sources have limited viewpoints.

Practice and evidence: Compare two sourced historical descriptions of community sanitation with dates.

Tuesday: Consider access differences

Focus: Consider access differences.

Learn: Public services and safe spaces may be distributed unevenly across communities.

Practice and evidence: Create an invented stakeholder chart for access to a public walking path.

Wednesday: Identify evidence limits

Focus: Identify evidence limits.

Learn: A policy document states intended rules, not necessarily how every resident experienced them.

Practice and evidence: Separate a fictional policy's text from assumptions about compliance.

Thursday: Discuss civic responsibility

Focus: Discuss civic responsibility.

Learn: A fair policy balances shared safety, dignity, privacy and feasible accommodations.

Practice and evidence: Draft a proposal for an inclusive public recreation space.

Friday: Exhibit contextual research

Focus: Exhibit contextual research.

Learn: Respectful public-history work acknowledges inequality without stereotyping groups.

Practice and evidence: Submit two sourced cards and an inclusion analysis. Rubric: context 6, sourcing 6, equity 4, clarity 4.
