Week 06 · Social Studies & Civics

Rational Numbers and Ancient Number Systems

How have cultures represented numbers and meaning across time?

Monday: Historic notation

Focus: Historic notation.

Learn: Roman numerals use combinations such as I, V, X and L; XII represents 12. Not all historical number systems used identical grouping or place values.

Practice and evidence: Make a table for I, V, X, XII and XX, listing decimal equivalents and one limitation of the examples.

Tuesday: Place value and chronology

Focus: Place value and chronology.

Learn: The widely used decimal positional system developed through multiple cultures and historical exchanges, especially important advances in South Asia and transmission through other regions. Timelines need trustworthy sources.

Practice and evidence: Write a careful short timeline of number-notation transmission, labeling any dates that need independent research rather than inventing them.

Wednesday: Trade and record keeping

Focus: Trade and record keeping.

Learn: Historical merchants used tools and conventions suitable to their communities. A fictional ledger entry of XII items is a classroom simulation, not proof of a particular merchant's bookkeeping.

Practice and evidence: Translate three simulated ledger quantities between Roman and decimal representations and label the simulation clearly.

Thursday: Compare number systems

Focus: Compare number systems.

Learn: Historical comparisons should consider symbols, grouping, place value and source availability without ranking societies as more or less intelligent.

Practice and evidence: Write a source-aware compare/contrast note describing one advantage and one limitation for each notation in a specific classroom task.

Friday: Cultural history portfolio

Focus: Cultural history portfolio.

Learn: Historical inquiry combines accurate examples, contextual caution, and proper source distinctions.

Practice and evidence: Submit a Roman-decimal comparison chart, a clearly fictional trade example, and a paragraph explaining what further historical sources you would verify. Rubric: notation 6, chronology 4, context 5, limits 5.
