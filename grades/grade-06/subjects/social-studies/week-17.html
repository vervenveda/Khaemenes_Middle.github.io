Week 17 · Social Studies & Civics

Midyear Portfolio and Assessment

How can I prove growth across every subject?

Monday: Select geographical and civic evidence

Focus: Select geographical and civic evidence.

Learn: A humanities portfolio can include an annotated map, a balanced policy brief and a historical-source comparison. Each should name what is factual versus invented.

Practice and evidence: Choose two past social-studies assignments and add dates, topics and evidence captions.

Tuesday: Evaluate sources

Focus: Evaluate sources.

Learn: A firsthand account, a later interpretation and a fictional source card carry different evidentiary value. Reliable claims should note provenance and possible limits.

Practice and evidence: Create a two-column source-credibility comparison including the type of evidence needed.

Wednesday: Revisit maps and quantitative claims

Focus: Revisit maps and quantitative claims.

Learn: A fictional travel route of 12 km in 3 h averages 4 km/h. That computation alone cannot establish affordability or real historical travel conditions.

Practice and evidence: Annotate a prior fictional route map with its calculation and two open civic questions.

Thursday: Write a balanced explanation

Focus: Write a balanced explanation.

Learn: Civic decisions can require weighing cost, access, safety and environmental effects; a reasoned response acknowledges competing priorities.

Practice and evidence: Revise a fictional community planning paragraph so it includes an opposing concern and qualified claim.

Friday: Produce a midyear civic evidence brief

Focus: Produce a midyear civic evidence brief.

Learn: Strong portfolio work distinguishes simulated scenarios from real sources and links a specific claim to an appropriate artifact.

Practice and evidence: Submit a source table, annotated map and 150-word reflection. Rubric: evidence 6, context 5, fairness 5, accuracy 4.
