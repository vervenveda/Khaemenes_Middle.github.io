Week 35 · Social Studies & Civics

Capstone Studio and Oral Defense

How can I make my evidence clear enough to defend publicly?

Monday: Establish historic context

Focus: Establish historic context.

Learn: A civic claim must identify its sources, period, participants and missing voices.

Practice and evidence: Prepare provenance cards for two fictional historical records.

Tuesday: Compare viewpoints

Focus: Compare viewpoints.

Learn: Public decisions affect people differently and no single account represents all participants.

Practice and evidence: Build a stakeholder matrix showing agreement and disagreement.

Wednesday: Check fairness of claims

Focus: Check fairness of claims.

Learn: Interpretations should distinguish what a document says from presumed impacts.

Practice and evidence: Revise an overly broad claim into one grounded by evidence.

Thursday: Practice moderated questions

Focus: Practice moderated questions.

Learn: Respectful public inquiry welcomes disagreement while protecting dignity.

Practice and evidence: Plan a short moderated exchange with accessible speaking or writing options.

Friday: Present a civic defense

Focus: Present a civic defense.

Learn: A credible civic proposal includes counterarguments, context and equity implications.

Practice and evidence: Submit a referenced mini-exhibit and Q&A sheet. Rubric: context 6, sources 6, inclusion 4, reasoning 4.
