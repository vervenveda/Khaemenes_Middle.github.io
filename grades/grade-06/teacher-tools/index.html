Sixth Grade Middle School A++ Mentor Toolkit

High-standard, printable, local, and organized for subject-based instruction, transition support, capstone defense, and Grade 7 readiness.

Middle School Transition Map

02

Grade 6 Literacy Guide

Support textual evidence, theme/central idea, point of view, argument analysis, text structure, vocabulary, grammar, speaking/listening, and disciplinary literacy.

03

Grade 6 Math Progression Map

Teach ratios/rates, percent, fraction division, rational numbers, coordinate planes, expressions/equations, inequalities, geometry, and statistics.

04

05

06

World Language and Culture Guide

Use greetings, cognates, scripts, pronunciation, language geography, cultural comparison, translation awareness, and respectful communication.

07

Technology, Data, and Design Guide

Use files, spreadsheets, charts, source notes, algorithms, debugging, accessibility, ethical media, and presentation design.

08

Portfolio, Capstone, and 80% Certification

Track weekly mastery, midterm, final, capstone work, revisions, reflections, Grade 7 readiness, and adult portfolio approval as separate gates.
