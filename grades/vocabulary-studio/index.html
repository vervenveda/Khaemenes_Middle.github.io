Six-term vocabulary practice

Read the six term cards first. Answer six mixed questions: some ask for a definition and others ask you to identify the term from its meaning. Then check your answers. You may use the cards and lesson while practicing. Practice target: 80%; this result is formative only.

Need help? Read the definition on the matching card, compare the meaning of similar words, and return to the weekly Language Arts lesson. If your assignment mentions a source not available on this page, use the lesson or ask an educator; do not invent evidence.
