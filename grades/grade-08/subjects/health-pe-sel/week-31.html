World Languages, Diplomacy, and Cultural Protocol

How can language and cultural knowledge prevent misunderstanding?

Monday: Seminar Launch and High School Vocabulary

Focus: stress regulation

Objective: Students strengthen health, pe & sel through stress regulation while investigating 'World Languages, Diplomacy, and Cultural Protocol'.

Activity: Introduce the subject focus, connect the essential question, model high-school-readiness thinking, and define academic vocabulary.

Workshop: Health, PE & SEL A++ workshop: create, solve, read, test, discuss, calculate, revise, or present evidence about world languages, diplomacy, and cultural protocol.
