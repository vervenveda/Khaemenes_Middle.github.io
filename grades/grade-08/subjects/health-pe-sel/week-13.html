Week 13 · Health, PE & SEL

Literary Theme, Perspective, and Modern Connections

How do authors transform old patterns into new meanings?

Monday — Identify stress signals

Learn and model: Stress may affect attention, sleep and mood differently for different people. Recognizing personal signs can support healthy planning, but a worksheet is not a medical diagnosis.

Practice and evidence: Draft a fictional school-day scenario naming two possible stress signals and two appropriate supports.

Tuesday — Practice regulation choices

Learn and model: A pause, manageable task list, gentle movement or a supportive conversation may help some students. Choose accessible activities; stop any movement that causes pain, and seek trusted help for persistent or concerning symptoms.

Practice and evidence: Make a personal choice menu with seated and standing options and a way to ask for help.

Wednesday — Evaluate a claim

Learn and model: An online post says 'This breathing exercise removes all anxiety in 30 seconds.' The absolute claim lacks evidence and ignores individual differences. A safer message acknowledges uncertainty and avoids promising treatment.

Practice and evidence: Identify the unverified claim, rewrite cautiously, and list two source-quality questions.

Thursday — Plan executive-function support

Learn and model: Chunking an assignment into reading, notes, first draft and review reduces ambiguity. For a fictional 60-minute block, allocate 15, 10, 25 and 10 minutes respectively; the sum should be checked.

Practice and evidence: Create an adaptable weekly study plan with breaks, priorities, and an alternative for low-energy days.

Friday — Independent wellness reflection

Learn and model: Prepare a one-page evidence-aware planning guide distinguishing general wellbeing strategies from professional support and identifying safe, accessible choices.

Practice and evidence: Rubric (20): accuracy 6, thoughtful planning 5, accessibility 5, evidence critique 4. Do not record private health disclosures in public portfolios.
