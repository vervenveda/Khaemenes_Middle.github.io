Week 08 · Language Arts & Literacy

Dilations, Similarity, and Scale

How can scale preserve shape while changing size?

Monday: Seminar Launch and High School Vocabulary

Focus: evidence-based explanations of similarity and scale

Objective: Students strengthen language arts & literacy through evidence-based explanations of similarity and scale while investigating 'Dilations, Similarity, and Scale'.

Activity: Introduce the subject focus, connect the essential question, model high-school-readiness thinking, and define academic vocabulary.

Workshop: Language Arts & Literacy A++ workshop: create, solve, read, test, discuss, calculate, revise, or present evidence about dilations, similarity, and scale.

Teacher script: Name the subject: Language Arts & Literacy. Connect to the essential question: How can scale preserve shape while changing size? Model the focus skill: evidence-based explanations of similarity and scale. Require evidence, calculation, model, vocabulary, citation note, or high-school-level explanation. Close with a defense or reflection: The high-school-ready part of my work is ____.

Evidence: Student work sample, teacher observation, oral explanation, correction/revision, and weekly subject mastery evidence.
