Week 28 · Language Arts & Literacy

Expansion, Reform, and Conflict

How do growth and reform create opportunity and conflict?

Monday: Seminar Launch and High School Vocabulary

Focus: strongest textual evidence

Objective: Students strengthen language arts & literacy through strongest textual evidence while investigating 'Expansion, Reform, and Conflict'.

Activity: Introduce the subject focus, connect the essential question, model high-school-readiness thinking, and define academic vocabulary.

Workshop: Language Arts & Literacy A++ workshop: create, solve, read, test, discuss, calculate, revise, or present evidence about expansion, reform, and conflict.

Teacher script: Name the subject: Language Arts & Literacy. Connect to the essential question: How do growth and reform create opportunity and conflict? Model the focus skill: strongest textual evidence. Require evidence, calculation, model, vocabulary, citation note, or high-school-level explanation.
