Week 23 · Arts, Music & Media

Genetics, Inheritance, and Bioethics

How do inheritance, environment, and ethics intersect?

Monday: Seminar Launch and High School Vocabulary

Focus: performance critique

Objective: Students strengthen arts, music & media through performance critique while investigating 'Genetics, Inheritance, and Bioethics'.

Activity: Introduce the subject focus, connect the essential question, model high-school-readiness thinking, and define academic vocabulary.

Workshop: Arts, Music & Media A++ workshop: create, solve, read, test, discuss, calculate, revise, or present evidence about genetics, inheritance, and bioethics.

Teacher script: Name the subject: Arts, Music & Media. Connect to the essential question: How do inheritance, environment, and ethics intersect? Model the focus skill: performance critique. Require evidence, calculation, model, vocabulary, citation note, or high-school-level explanation. Close with a defense or reflection: The high-school-ready part of my work is ____.

Evidence: Student work sample, teacher observation, oral explanation, correction/revision, and weekly subject mastery evidence.
