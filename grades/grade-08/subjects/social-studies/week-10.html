Volume, Cylinders, Cones, and Spheres

How do formulas help us model three-dimensional space?

Monday: Seminar Launch and High School Vocabulary

Focus: founding principles

Objective: Students strengthen social studies, civics & history through founding principles while investigating 'Volume, Cylinders, Cones, and Spheres'.

Activity: Introduce the subject focus, connect the essential question, model high-school-readiness thinking, and define academic vocabulary.

Workshop: Social Studies, Civics & History A++ workshop: create, solve, read, test, discuss, calculate, revise, or present evidence about volume, cylinders, cones, and spheres.

Model the focus skill: founding principles. Require evidence, calculation, model, vocabulary, citation note, or high-school-level explanation. Close with a defense or reflection: The high-school-ready part of my work is ____.
