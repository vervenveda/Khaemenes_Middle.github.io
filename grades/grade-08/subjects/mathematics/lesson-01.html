01Remember · Expressions and Equations

Before learning a new idea, retrieve what you know. Try without looking at a worked example.

Quick retrieval

1. Expand 2(x + 3).

2. Solve 2x + 5 = 17.

3. Explain how an expression differs from an equation.

Your explanation

2(x + 3) = 2x + 6; the solution of 2x + 5 = 17 is x = 6. An equation asserts equality; an expression does not.

02Discover · Constant Rate of Change

A linear relationship changes by a constant amount for every equal increase in the input. Explore y = 3x + 2: each increase of 1 in x adds 3 to y. The starting value is 2.

Compare the pattern

Input x0123
Output y25811

Think like a mathematician

What stays constant in this table? Why is the starting value different from the rate of change?

03Learn · Worked Model & Guided Work

Worked example

A relationship is modeled by y = 3x + 2. To find the output when x = 4, substitute: y = 3(4) + 2 = 14. The coefficient 3 is the rate of change; 2 is the starting value.

Guided problem

For y = 3x + 2, what is y when x = 4?

Reasoning prompt

Explain why multiplying x by 3 before adding 2 models the table.
