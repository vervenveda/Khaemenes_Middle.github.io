Linear relationships · Learn and represent

Vocabulary in context: Name the quantities, relationships, domain and units used in the questions.

Worked mathematical model: In y=mx+b, m is the constant change per input unit and b is the baseline at x=0. Today's model uses a=5 and b=4; explain how the example applies to this week's evidence theme.

Misconception checkpoint: A nonzero intercept does not stop a relationship from being linear, but it stops it being proportional.

Independent reasoning challenges:

  1. For y=5x+4, find y at x=2.
  2. Identify slope in y=5x+4.
  3. Find y-intercept in y=5x+4.
  4. In y=5x+4, increase x by 3: how much does y change?

Independent self-check: Recompute using inverse operations, a substitution, an alternative diagram or unit analysis before opening the key.
