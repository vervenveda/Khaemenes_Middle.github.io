Interpret slope and intercept · Mathematics laboratory

Vocabulary in context: Define the key operations, units and variables in the worked example; explain their mathematical meaning.

Worked mathematical model: For y=−3x+8, slope is −3 and y-intercept is 8; the line falls 3 per step.

Misconception checkpoint: A negative slope describes direction of change, not a negative starting amount.

Independent reasoning challenges:

  1. Find slope of y=4x−7.
  2. Find intercept of y=4x−7.
  3. Evaluate y=−3x+8 at x=2.
  4. Find x when y=−3x+8 equals −1.

Independent self-check: Verify the result by substitution, inverse operations or a second mathematical representation before viewing the solutions.

Teacher solution key (open after attempting)
  1. 4
  2. −7
  3. 2
  4. 3

Calculate slope from coordinates · Mathematics laboratory

Vocabulary in context: Define the key operations, units and variables in the worked example; explain their mathematical meaning.

Worked mathematical model: Slope between (1,4) and (4,10) is (10−4)/(4−1)=2.

Misconception checkpoint: Subtract coordinates in the same point order for numerator and denominator.

Independent reasoning challenges:

  1. Find slope between (0,3) and (2,9).
  2. Find slope between (−1,5) and (3,−3).
  3. Find slope between (2,4) and (6,4).
  4. Find slope between (3,1) and (3,8).

Independent self-check: Verify the result by substitution, inverse operations or a second mathematical representation before viewing the solutions.

  3. 0

Critique graphs and claims · Mathematics laboratory

Vocabulary in context: Define the key operations, units and variables in the worked example; explain their mathematical meaning.

Worked mathematical model: A graph with the vertical axis cut at 90 may exaggerate a change from 95 to 100.

Misconception checkpoint: A graph's visual steepness depends on scale; use actual values before drawing conclusions.

Independent reasoning challenges:

  1. Find numerical change from 95 to 100.
  2. Find percent increase from 95 to 100 to two decimals.
  3. Find y at x=3 for y=2x+1.
  4. Find slope between (1,3) and (4,9).

Independent self-check: Verify the result by substitution, inverse operations or a second mathematical representation before viewing the solutions.

Find intersection and break-even · Mathematics laboratory

Vocabulary in context: Define the key operations, units and variables in the worked example; explain their mathematical meaning.

Worked mathematical model: A(x)=2x+9 and B(x)=5x intersect when 2x+9=5x, so x=3 and y=15.

Misconception checkpoint: An intersection is a pair satisfying both rules, not merely an equal slope.

Independent reasoning challenges:

  1. Solve 3x+7=5x+1.
  2. At x=3, evaluate 3x+7.
  3. At x=3, evaluate 5x+1.
  4. Find y-intercept of A(x)=2x+9.

Independent self-check: Verify the result by substitution, inverse operations or a second mathematical representation before viewing the solutions.

Explain linear evidence · Mathematics laboratory

Vocabulary in context: Define the key operations, units and variables in the worked example; explain their mathematical meaning.

Worked mathematical model: For y=5x−2, each unit increase in x adds 5. A table and equation should agree.

Misconception checkpoint: Two sample points support a line but do not prove real data are perfectly linear.

Independent reasoning challenges:

  1. Evaluate y=5x−2 at x=4.
  2. Find change from x=1 to x=5 for slope 5.
  3. Find slope between (1,3) and (4,18).
  4. Find x when 5x−2=23.

Independent self-check: Verify the result by substitution, inverse operations or a second mathematical representation before viewing the solutions.

  2. 20
  3. 5
  4. 5
