Identify linear relationships · Mathematics laboratory

Vocabulary in context: Define the important quantities and units in the worked model; explain them in your own words.

Worked mathematical model: The rule y=3x+2 produces y-values 2,5,8 for x=0,1,2; each step rises by 3.

Misconception checkpoint: Constant change means linear, but nonzero starting values mean not proportional.

Independent reasoning challenges:

  1. For y=2x+5, find y at x=0.
  2. For y=2x+5, find y at x=3.
  3. Find slope of y=2x+5.
  4. Find the y-intercept of y=2x+5.

Independent self-check: Verify by substitution, inverse operations, unit comparison or a second representation before inspecting the answer key.

Build linear tables · Mathematics laboratory

Vocabulary in context: Define the important quantities and units in the worked model; explain them in your own words.

Worked mathematical model: For y=4x−1, points at x=0,1,2 have y=−1,3,7, a constant increase of 4.

Misconception checkpoint: The first listed output need not be zero for a linear pattern.

Independent reasoning challenges:

  1. For y=4x−1 find y at x=3.
  2. Find y at x=−1.
  3. Find output change when x increases by 2.
  4. State the slope of the rule.

Compare slopes and intercepts · Mathematics laboratory

Vocabulary in context: Define the important quantities and units in the worked model; explain them in your own words.

Worked mathematical model: A=3x+4 and B=5x+1: A starts higher, but B increases faster.

Misconception checkpoint: Compare both initial amounts and rates before declaring a plan cheaper for all inputs.

Independent reasoning challenges:

  1. Compute A(0) for A=3x+4.
  2. Compute B(0) for B=5x+1.
  3. Find A(3).
  4. Find B(3).

Independent self-check: Verify by substitution, inverse operations, unit comparison or a second representation before inspecting the answer key.

Solve simple linear models · Mathematics laboratory

Vocabulary in context: Define the important quantities and units in the worked model; explain them in your own words.

Worked mathematical model: If y=5x+7 and y=27, subtract 7 and divide by 5 to find x=4.

Misconception checkpoint: Reversing the operations in the wrong order can yield a false x-value.

Independent reasoning challenges:

  1. Solve 2x+3=13.
  2. Solve 4x−5=15.
  3. Solve 3x+8=20.
  4. Check x=4 in 5x+7=27.

Independent self-check: Verify by substitution, inverse operations, unit comparison or a second representation before inspecting the answer key.

Defend linear reasoning · Mathematics laboratory

Vocabulary in context: Define the important quantities and units in the worked model; explain them in your own words.

Worked mathematical model: At y=6x+4, x=4 yields y=28; substituting verifies the model.

Misconception checkpoint: A table pattern observed for two pairs does not prove every real situation remains linear.

Independent reasoning challenges:

  1. For y=6x+4 find y at x=5.
  2. State slope for y=6x+4.
  3. State intercept for y=6x+4.
  4. Find x when y=40.

Independent self-check: Verify by substitution, inverse operations, unit comparison or a second representation before inspecting the answer key.
