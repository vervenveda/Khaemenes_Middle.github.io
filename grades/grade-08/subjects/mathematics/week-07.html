Translate points on the plane · Mathematics laboratory

Vocabulary in context: Define quantities, symbols and units from the worked model in your own words.

Worked mathematical model: Translation (x,y)→(x+3,y−2) maps (−1,4) to (2,2).

Misconception checkpoint: Translation shifts both coordinates as directed but does not rotate or resize the figure.

Independent reasoning challenges:

  1. Translate (2,3) by (+4,−1).
  2. Translate (−5,1) by (+2,+3).
  3. Translate (0,−3) by (−2,+5).
  4. What translation vector maps (1,1) to (4,−1)?

Independent self-check: Verify the result with substitution, inverse operations, units, a coordinate sketch or a second model before opening the teacher key.

Teacher solution key (open after attempting)
  1. (6,2)
  2. (−3,4)
  3. (−2,2)
  4. (+3,−2)

Reflect and rotate coordinates · Mathematics laboratory

Vocabulary in context: Define quantities, symbols and units from the worked model in your own words.

Worked mathematical model: Across the x-axis, (a,b)→(a,−b). A 90° counterclockwise rotation maps (a,b)→(−b,a).

Misconception checkpoint: Reflection across y-axis negates x, not y; clockwise and counterclockwise rotations differ.

Independent reasoning challenges:

  1. Reflect (−3,5) over x-axis.
  2. Reflect (−3,5) over y-axis.
  3. Rotate (2,5) 90° counterclockwise.
  4. Rotate (2,5) 180°.

Independent self-check: Verify the result with substitution, inverse operations, units, a coordinate sketch or a second model before opening the teacher key.

Teacher solution key (open after attempting)
  1. (−3,−5)
  2. (3,5)
  3. (−5,2)
  4. (−2,−5)

Identify rigid congruence · Mathematics laboratory

Vocabulary in context: Define quantities, symbols and units from the worked model in your own words.

Worked mathematical model: Translate a 3-4-5 triangle: all corresponding sides remain 3,4,5, so the triangle is congruent.

Misconception checkpoint: A dilation by a factor other than 1 changes size and usually is not a rigid motion.

Independent reasoning challenges:

  1. Does reflection preserve side length?
  2. Does translation preserve angles?
  3. Does dilation factor 2 preserve length?
  4. Which is rigid: translation or factor-3 dilation?

Independent self-check: Verify the result with substitution, inverse operations, units, a coordinate sketch or a second model before opening the teacher key.

Teacher solution key (open after attempting)
  1. yes
  2. yes
  3. no
  4. translation

Compose geometric transformations · Mathematics laboratory

Vocabulary in context: Define quantities, symbols and units from the worked model in your own words.

Worked mathematical model: Reflect (2,3) over y-axis to (−2,3), then translate right 4 to (2,3).

Misconception checkpoint: Order matters: translating and reflecting can yield different end coordinates.

Independent reasoning challenges:

  1. Reflect (1,2) across x-axis.
  2. After reflecting (1,2) across the x-axis to (1,−2), translate the image 3 units right.
  3. Rotate (3,−1) 180°.
  4. Reflect (0,4) across y-axis.

Independent self-check: Verify the result with substitution, inverse operations, units, a coordinate sketch or a second model before opening the teacher key.

Teacher solution key (open after attempting)
  1. (1,−2)
  2. (4,−2)
  3. (−3,1)
  4. (0,4)

Explain congruence with evidence · Mathematics laboratory

Vocabulary in context: Define quantities, symbols and units from the worked model in your own words.

Worked mathematical model: If an image is reached by translations, rotations and reflections, distances and angles are preserved.

Misconception checkpoint: Similar-looking sketches without matching measures are not proof of congruence.

Independent reasoning challenges:

  1. A 5 cm segment translated: new length?
  2. A 60° angle reflected: new angle?
  3. A triangle with sides 4,5,6 rigidly moved: perimeter?
  4. A square side 3 rotated: area?

Independent self-check: Verify the result with substitution, inverse operations, units, a coordinate sketch or a second model before opening the teacher key.

Teacher solution key (open after attempting)
  1. 5 cm
  2. 60°
  3. 15
  4. 9
