Slope and rate comparisons · Concept and vocabulary

Vocabulary in context: Explain the main quantities, units, symbols and domains in the model.

Worked mathematical model: Slope is rise/run and a line's intercept is its value at x=0. For today's practice a=4 and b=3; connect the model with this week's mathematical context.

Misconception checkpoint: A negative or positive slope measures modeled direction of change, not cause.

Independent reasoning challenges:

  1. Find slope between (0,3) and (3,15).
  2. Find intercept of y=4x−3.
  3. At slope 4, x increases four: rise?
  4. For y=4x−3, calculate y(2).

Independent self-check: Verify every result with inverse operations, substitution, diagrams or units before consulting the answer key.
