Similarity and dilation · Learn and represent

Vocabulary in context: Name the quantities, relationships, domain and units used in the questions.

Worked mathematical model: A dilation about the origin multiplies both coordinates by its scale factor; corresponding angles stay equal. Today's model uses a=2 and b=4; explain how the example applies to this week's evidence theme.

Misconception checkpoint: A dilation other than scale one is not a rigid congruence.

Independent reasoning challenges:

  1. Dilate point (2,4) by factor 2 about origin.
  2. A side 2 cm scales by factor 3. New length?
  3. A shape area 4 cm² scales by factor 2. New area?
  4. A 8 cm side becomes 4 cm. What is the scale factor?

Independent self-check: Recompute using inverse operations, a substitution, an alternative diagram or unit analysis before opening the key.

Teacher solution key (open after attempting)
  1. (4,8)
  2. 6 cm
  3. 16 cm²
  4. 1/2
