Week 22 · Mathematics: Algebra I Readiness

Systems of Equations in Geologic Modeling

How does evidence from Earth history explain change over time?

Monday: Seminar Launch and High School Vocabulary

Focus: systems of equations

Objective: Students strengthen mathematics: algebra i readiness through systems of equations while investigating 'Geologic Time, Fossils, and Evolution Evidence'.

Activity: Introduce the subject focus, connect the essential question, model high-school-readiness thinking, and define academic vocabulary.

Workshop: Mathematics: Algebra I Readiness A++ workshop: create, solve, read, test, discuss, calculate, revise, or present evidence about geologic time, fossils, and evolution evidence.

Teacher script: Name the subject: Mathematics: Algebra I Readiness. Connect to the essential question: How does evidence from Earth history explain change over time? Model the focus skill: systems of equations. Require evidence, calculation, model, vocabulary, citation note, or high-school-level explanation. Close with a defense or reflection: The high-school-ready part of my work is ____.

Evidence: Student work sample, teacher observation, oral explanation, correction/revision, and weekly subject mastery evidence.
