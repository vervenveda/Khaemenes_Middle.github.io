Monday: Seminar Launch and High School Vocabulary

Focus: similarity

Objective: Students strengthen mathematics: algebra i readiness through similarity while investigating 'A++ High School Readiness Capstone Research'.

Activity: Introduce the subject focus, connect the essential question, model high-school-readiness thinking, and define academic vocabulary.

Workshop: Mathematics: Algebra I Readiness A++ workshop: create, solve, read, test, discuss, calculate, revise, or present evidence about a++ high school readiness capstone research.

Teacher script: Name the subject: Mathematics: Algebra I Readiness. Connect to the essential question: What serious question can I investigate with evidence, calculation, design, and public responsibility? Model the focus skill: similarity. Require evidence, calculation, model, vocabulary, citation note, or high-school-level explanation. Close with a defense or reflection: The high-school-ready part of my work is ____.

Evidence: Student work sample, teacher observation, oral explanation, correction/revision, and weekly subject mastery evidence.

Similarity capstone · Concept and vocabulary

Vocabulary in context: Explain the main quantities, units, symbols and domains in the model.

Worked mathematical model: A dilation scales both coordinates and changes linear size by k, area by k². For today's practice a=4 and b=4; connect the model with this week's mathematical context.

Misconception checkpoint: Equal corresponding angles mean similarity; congruence also requires equal lengths.

Independent reasoning challenges:

  1. Dilate (4,4) factor 2 about origin.
  2. Side 4 cm scaled by factor 3: new side?
  3. Area 4 cm² scaled by factor 2: new area?
  4. Side shrinks from 8 to 4: factor?

Independent self-check: Verify every result with inverse operations, substitution, diagrams or units before consulting the answer key.

Teacher solution key (open after attempting)
  1. (8,8)
  2. 12 cm
  3. 16 cm²
  4. 1/2
