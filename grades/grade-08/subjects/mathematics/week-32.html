Monday: Seminar Launch and High School Vocabulary

Focus: exponents/scientific notation

Objective: Students strengthen mathematics: algebra i readiness through exponents/scientific notation while investigating 'Technology, Algorithms, Data Ethics, and AI Readiness'.

Activity: Introduce the subject focus, connect the essential question, model high-school-readiness thinking, and define academic vocabulary.

Workshop: Mathematics: Algebra I Readiness A++ workshop: create, solve, read, test, discuss, calculate, revise, or present evidence about technology, algorithms, data ethics, and ai readiness.

Evidence: Student work sample, teacher observation, oral explanation, correction/revision, and weekly subject mastery evidence.
