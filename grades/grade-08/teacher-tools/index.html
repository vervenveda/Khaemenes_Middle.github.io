Teacher Tools

Eighth Grade A++ High School Readiness Mentor Toolkit

High-standard, printable, local, and organized for subject-based instruction, capstone defense, and high school transition.

01

High School Transition Pacing Map

Coordinate nine subject halls across 36 weeks while teaching executive function, files, study habits, accountability, advisory reflection, and high-school transition planning.

02

Grade 8 Literacy and Research Guide

Support strongest textual evidence, theme/central idea, point of view, argument evaluation, source credibility, research synthesis, grammar, speaking/listening, and disciplinary literacy.

03

Algebra I Readiness Map

Teach linear equations, functions, slope/intercept, systems, exponents, transformations, similarity, Pythagorean theorem, volume, bivariate data, and high-school-ready modeling.

04

Culminating Middle School Science Guide

Teach matter/reactions, forces, energy, waves, Earth/space systems, evolution evidence, genetics, ecosystems, sustainability, models, fair tests, and engineering design.

05

Civics and History Inquiry Guide

Use maps, timelines, primary/secondary sources, founding documents, constitutional principles, rights, reform, Reconstruction, economics, perspective, and informed action.

06

World Language and Diplomacy Guide

Use grammar patterns, cognates, scripts, language geography, cultural protocols, translation awareness, diplomacy vocabulary, and respectful communication.

07

Technology, AI, Data, and Design Guide

Use files, spreadsheets, charts, source notes, algorithms, debugging, accessibility, ethical media, privacy, bias, AI-readiness, and design thinking.

08

Portfolio, Capstone, and 80% Certification

Track weekly mastery, midterm, final, capstone work, revisions, reflections, high-school transition plan, and adult portfolio approval as separate gates.
