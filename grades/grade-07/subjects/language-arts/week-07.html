Week 07 · Language Arts & Literacy

Equations and Inequalities

How does solving an equation or inequality reveal hidden information?

Monday: Seminar Launch and Academic Vocabulary

Focus: explaining algebraic reasoning through precise informational writing

Objective: Students strengthen language arts & literacy through explaining algebraic reasoning through precise informational writing while investigating 'Equations and Inequalities'.

Activity: Introduce the subject focus, connect the essential question, model middle-school thinking, and define academic vocabulary.

Workshop: Language Arts & Literacy A++ workshop: create, solve, read, test, discuss, calculate, revise, or present evidence about equations and inequalities.

Teacher script: Name the subject: Language Arts & Literacy. Connect to the essential question: How does solving an equation or inequality reveal hidden information? Model the focus skill: explaining algebraic reasoning through precise informational writing. Require evidence, calculation, model, vocabulary, citation note, or clear explanation. Close with a defense or reflection: The strongest evidence in my work is ____.

Evidence: Student work sample, teacher observation, oral explanation, correction/revision, and weekly subject mastery evidence.
