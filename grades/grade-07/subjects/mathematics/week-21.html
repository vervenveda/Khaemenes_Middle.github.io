Week 21 · Mathematics

Signed Rational Numbers in Ecosystem Models

How do relationships in ecosystems affect survival and responsibility?

Monday: Seminar Launch and Academic Vocabulary

Focus: rational-number operations

Objective: Students strengthen mathematics through rational-number operations while investigating 'Ecosystems, Interdependence, and Human Impact'.

Activity: Introduce the subject focus, connect the essential question, model middle-school thinking, and define academic vocabulary.

Workshop: Mathematics A++ workshop: create, solve, read, test, discuss, calculate, revise, or present evidence about ecosystems, interdependence, and human impact.

Teacher script: Name the subject: Mathematics. Connect to the essential question: How do relationships in ecosystems affect survival and responsibility? Model the focus skill: rational-number operations. Require evidence, calculation, model, vocabulary, citation note, or clear explanation. Close with a defense or reflection: The strongest evidence in my work is ____.

Evidence: Student work sample, teacher observation, oral explanation, correction/revision, and weekly subject mastery evidence.

Signed rational operations · Learn and represent

Vocabulary in context: Name the quantities, relationships, domain and units used in the questions.

Worked mathematical model: Use an oriented number line and inverse operations to determine signed sums, products and quotients. Today's model uses a=3 and b=3; explain how the example applies to this week's evidence theme.

Misconception checkpoint: A negative location and a negative change are different ideas; check the operation sign.

Independent reasoning challenges:

  1. Evaluate −3+7.
  2. Evaluate 3−6.
  3. Evaluate (−3)×3.
  4. Evaluate (−6)÷2.

Independent self-check: Recompute using inverse operations, a substitution, an alternative diagram or unit analysis before opening the key.
