Week 23 · Mathematics

Equations and Inequalities in Motion

How do forces and energy interact in systems?

Monday: Seminar Launch and Academic Vocabulary

Focus: equations/inequalities

Objective: Students strengthen mathematics through equations/inequalities while investigating 'Energy, Forces, and Motion Systems'.

Activity: Introduce the subject focus, connect the essential question, model middle-school thinking, and define academic vocabulary.

Workshop: Mathematics A++ workshop: create, solve, read, test, discuss, calculate, revise, or present evidence about energy, forces, and motion systems.

Teacher script: Name the subject: Mathematics. Connect to the essential question: How do forces and energy interact in systems? Model the focus skill: equations/inequalities. Require evidence, calculation, model, vocabulary, citation note, or clear explanation. Close with a defense or reflection: The strongest evidence in my work is ____.

Evidence: Student work sample, teacher observation, oral explanation, correction/revision, and weekly subject mastery evidence.

Inequality constraints · Concept and diagrams

Vocabulary in context: Define mathematical symbols, units and constraints in your explanation.

Worked mathematical model: Balance inverse operations and reverse the inequality direction when dividing by a negative. Today's model sets a=5, b=5; show how these values support the week's mathematics.

Misconception checkpoint: Continuous bounds and admissible whole-number domains must be distinguished.

Independent reasoning challenges:

  1. Solve x+5≤10.
  2. Solve 5x≥25.
  3. Solve −5x<25.
  4. A fixed fee 5 plus 5 per visit has budget 25. Find maximum whole visits.

Independent self-check: Use an inverse, substitution, alternate diagram or units before revealing solutions.
