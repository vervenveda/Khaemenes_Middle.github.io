Week 34 · Mathematics

Circle Geometry Capstone Research

What serious question can I investigate with evidence, calculation, design, and care?

Monday: Seminar Launch and Academic Vocabulary

Focus: circles and area

Objective: Students strengthen mathematics through circles and area while investigating 'A++ Big Question Capstone Research'.

Activity: Introduce the subject focus, connect the essential question, model middle-school thinking, and define academic vocabulary.

Workshop: Mathematics A++ workshop: create, solve, read, test, discuss, calculate, revise, or present evidence about a++ big question capstone research.

Teacher script: Name the subject: Mathematics. Connect to the essential question: What serious question can I investigate with evidence, calculation, design, and care? Model the focus skill: circles and area. Require evidence, calculation, model, vocabulary, citation note, or clear explanation. Close with a defense or reflection: The strongest evidence in my work is ____.

Evidence: Student work sample, teacher observation, oral explanation, correction/revision, and weekly subject mastery evidence.

Circle capstone mathematics · Concept and vocabulary

Vocabulary in context: Explain the main quantities, units, symbols and domains in the model.

Worked mathematical model: C=2πr and A=πr² are distinct models with distinct units. For today's practice a=4 and b=4; connect the model with this week's mathematical context.

Misconception checkpoint: Diameter, radius, circumference and area are not interchangeable.

Independent reasoning challenges:

  1. Circle radius 4 cm: diameter?
  2. Circle radius 4 cm: exact circumference?
  3. Circle radius 4 cm: exact area?
  4. Semicircle radius 8 cm: area?

Independent self-check: Verify every result with inverse operations, substitution, diagrams or units before consulting the answer key.

Teacher solution key (open after attempting)
  1. 8 cm
  2. 8π cm
  3. 16π cm²
  4. 32π cm²
