01Remember · Number Sense

Before learning a new idea, retrieve what you know. Try without looking at a worked example.

Quick retrieval

1. Simplify 6:9.

2. What is 18 ÷ 3?

3. Explain the difference between 2:3 and 3:2.

Your explanation

6:9 simplifies to 2:3; 18 ÷ 3 = 6. Ratio order changes which quantity is compared to the other.

02Discover · Equivalent Ratios

Two quantities are proportional when their ratios remain equivalent. A recipe uses 2 cups of concentrate for every 5 cups of water. Scaling the recipe must scale both quantities by the same multiplier.

03Learn · Worked Model & Guided Work

Worked example

Five notebooks cost $7.50. Find the cost of one notebook: $7.50 ÷ 5 = $1.50 per notebook. At the same rate, eight notebooks cost 8 × $1.50 = $12.00.

Guided problem

Four markers cost $6. What is the cost of one marker?

Reasoning prompt

Why is total cost divided by number of items the correct operation?
