Week 32 · Mathematics

Inequalities and Diplomatic Resource Constraints

How can language build connection across difference?

Monday: Seminar Launch and Academic Vocabulary

Focus: equations/inequalities

Objective: Students strengthen mathematics through equations/inequalities while investigating 'World Languages, Culture, and Diplomacy'.

Activity: Introduce the subject focus, connect the essential question, model middle-school thinking, and define academic vocabulary.

Workshop: Mathematics A++ workshop: create, solve, read, test, discuss, calculate, revise, or present evidence about world languages, culture, and diplomacy.

Teacher script: Name the subject: Mathematics. Connect to the essential question: How can language build connection across difference? Model the focus skill: equations/inequalities. Require evidence, calculation, model, vocabulary, citation note, or clear explanation. Close with a defense or reflection: The strongest evidence in my work is ____.

Evidence: Student work sample, teacher observation, oral explanation, correction/revision, and weekly subject mastery evidence.
