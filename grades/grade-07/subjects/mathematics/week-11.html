Probability and Fair Games

How can probability help us judge risk, fairness, and prediction?

Monday: Seminar Launch and Academic Vocabulary

Focus: experimental probability and fair-game reasoning

Objective: Students strengthen mathematics through experimental probability and fair-game reasoning while investigating 'Probability and Fair Games'.

Activity: Introduce the subject focus, connect the essential question, model middle-school thinking, and define academic vocabulary.
