Week 31 · Mathematics

Algebraic Expressions in Economic Models

How do economic systems affect human choices and dignity?

Monday: Seminar Launch and Academic Vocabulary

Focus: expressions

Objective: Students strengthen mathematics through expressions while investigating 'Economics, Labor, and Global Systems'.

Activity: Introduce the subject focus, connect the essential question, model middle-school thinking, and define academic vocabulary.

Workshop: Mathematics A++ workshop: create, solve, read, test, discuss, calculate, revise, or present evidence about economics, labor, and global systems.

Teacher script: Name the subject: Mathematics. Connect to the essential question: How do economic systems affect human choices and dignity? Model the focus skill: expressions. Require evidence, calculation, model, vocabulary, citation note, or clear explanation. Close with a defense or reflection: The strongest evidence in my work is ____.

Evidence: Student work sample, teacher observation, oral explanation, correction/revision, and weekly subject mastery evidence.

Expression models · Concept and vocabulary

Vocabulary in context: Explain the main quantities, units, symbols and domains in the model.

Worked mathematical model: Substitute into a model, distribute factors through all terms and combine like terms. For today's practice a=5 and b=4; connect the model with this week's mathematical context.

Misconception checkpoint: An expression with a variable and a constant cannot combine into a single like term.

Independent reasoning challenges:

  1. For 5x+4, find output at x=3.
  2. Expand 5(x+4).
  3. Combine 5x+4x−2.
  4. Solve 5x+4=14.

Independent self-check: Verify every result with inverse operations, substitution, diagrams or units before consulting the answer key.
