Week 22 · Mathematics

Expressions and Chemical Quantity Models

How can particle models explain matter and change?

Monday: Seminar Launch and Academic Vocabulary

Focus: expressions

Objective: Students strengthen mathematics through expressions while investigating 'Matter, Atoms, and Chemical Foundations'.

Activity: Introduce the subject focus, connect the essential question, model middle-school thinking, and define academic vocabulary.

Workshop: Mathematics A++ workshop: create, solve, read, test, discuss, calculate, revise, or present evidence about matter, atoms, and chemical foundations.

Teacher script: Name the subject: Mathematics. Connect to the essential question: How can particle models explain matter and change? Model the focus skill: expressions. Require evidence, calculation, model, vocabulary, citation note, or clear explanation. Close with a defense or reflection: The strongest evidence in my work is ____.

Evidence: Student work sample, teacher observation, oral explanation, correction/revision, and weekly subject mastery evidence.
