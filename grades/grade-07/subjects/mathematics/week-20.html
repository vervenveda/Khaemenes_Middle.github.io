Week 20 · Mathematics

Percentages and Comparative Inheritance Data

How do inheritance and environment shape living things?

Monday: Seminar Launch and Academic Vocabulary

Focus: unit rates and percent

Objective: Students strengthen mathematics through unit rates and percent while investigating 'Genetics Foundations and Variation'.

Activity: Introduce the subject focus, connect the essential question, model middle-school thinking, and define academic vocabulary.

Workshop: Mathematics A++ workshop: create, solve, read, test, discuss, calculate, revise, or present evidence about genetics foundations and variation.

Teacher script: Name the subject: Mathematics. Connect to the essential question: How do inheritance and environment shape living things? Model the focus skill: unit rates and percent. Require evidence, calculation, model, vocabulary, citation note, or clear explanation. Close with a defense or reflection: The strongest evidence in my work is ____.

Evidence: Student work sample, teacher observation, oral explanation, correction/revision, and weekly subject mastery evidence.

Percentage models · Learn and represent

Vocabulary in context: Name the quantities, relationships, domain and units used in the questions.

Worked mathematical model: Convert a percent to a multiplier; use the original amount to calculate relative percentage change. Today's model uses a=2 and b=5; explain how the example applies to this week's evidence theme.

Misconception checkpoint: Percent change and percentage-point change use different reference quantities.

Independent reasoning challenges:

  1. Find 25% of 16.
  2. Increase 100 by 10%.
  3. Decrease 40 by 20%.
  4. What percentage is 15 of 60?

Independent self-check: Recompute using inverse operations, a substitution, an alternative diagram or unit analysis before opening the key.
