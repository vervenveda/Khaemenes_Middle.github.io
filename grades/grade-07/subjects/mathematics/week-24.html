Week 24 · Mathematics

Scale Drawings and Earth Science Models

How do Earth processes shape the planet and human decisions?

Monday: Seminar Launch and Academic Vocabulary

Focus: scale drawings

Objective: Students strengthen mathematics through scale drawings while investigating 'Earth Systems, Plate Tectonics, and Hazards'.

Activity: Introduce the subject focus, connect the essential question, model middle-school thinking, and define academic vocabulary.

Workshop: Mathematics A++ workshop: create, solve, read, test, discuss, calculate, revise, or present evidence about earth systems, plate tectonics, and hazards.

Teacher script: Name the subject: Mathematics. Connect to the essential question: How do Earth processes shape the planet and human decisions? Model the focus skill: scale drawings. Require evidence, calculation, model, vocabulary, citation note, or clear explanation. Close with a defense or reflection: The strongest evidence in my work is ____.

Evidence: Student work sample, teacher observation, oral explanation, correction/revision, and weekly subject mastery evidence.

Scale and spatial reasoning · Concept and diagrams

Vocabulary in context: Define mathematical symbols, units and constraints in your explanation.

Worked mathematical model: Scale lengths by k and areas by k²; retain correct measurement units. Today's model sets a=2, b=3; show how these values support the week's mathematics.

Misconception checkpoint: Proportional scale drawings do not certify real routes or actual site dimensions.

Independent reasoning challenges:

  1. At 1 cm:2 m, a line of 3 cm models which distance?
  2. At 1 cm:2 m, a real 8 m line appears how many cm?
  3. If lengths scale by 2, what is area scale factor?
  4. An area of 3 cm² scales by 2. Find new area.

Independent self-check: Use an inverse, substitution, alternate diagram or units before revealing solutions.

Teacher solution key (open after attempting)
  1. 6 m
  2. 4 cm
  3. 4
  4. 12 cm²
