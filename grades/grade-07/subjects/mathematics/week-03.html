Add signed rational numbers · Mathematics laboratory

Vocabulary in context: Define the important quantities and units in the worked model; explain them in your own words.

Worked mathematical model: −3/4+1/2 equals −3/4+2/4=−1/4.

Misconception checkpoint: An addition involving a negative need not produce a negative result; magnitude matters.

Independent reasoning challenges:

  1. Find −2/3+1/6.
  2. Find −1.5+2.25.
  3. Find 3/4−5/4.
  4. Find −4+9.

Independent self-check: Verify by substitution, inverse operations, unit comparison or a second representation before inspecting the answer key.

Teacher solution key (open after attempting)
  1. −1/2
  2. 0.75
  3. −1/2
  4. 5

Use signed rational numbers in context · Mathematics laboratory

Vocabulary in context: Define the important quantities and units in the worked model; explain them in your own words.

Worked mathematical model: A balance changes from −$8.50 to +$3: the signed difference is +$11.50.

Misconception checkpoint: A balance below zero is not the same as the signed change between balances.

Independent reasoning challenges:

  1. Find change from −5 to +4.
  2. Find change from +7 to −2.
  3. Find distance from −5 to +4.
  4. Calculate −1.25+0.5.
