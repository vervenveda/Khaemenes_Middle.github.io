Week 12 · Science & Engineering

Statistics and Inference

How can samples help us make claims about populations?

Monday: Seminar Launch and Academic Vocabulary

Focus: biological variation and sampling uncertainty

Objective: Students strengthen science & engineering through biological variation and sampling uncertainty while investigating 'Statistics and Inference'.

Activity: Introduce the subject focus, connect the essential question, model middle-school thinking, and define academic vocabulary.

Workshop: Science & Engineering A++ workshop: create, solve, read, test, discuss, calculate, revise, or present evidence about statistics and inference.

Teacher script: Name the subject: Science & Engineering. Connect to the essential question: How can samples help us make claims about populations? Model the focus skill: biological variation and sampling uncertainty. Require evidence, calculation, model, vocabulary, citation note, or clear explanation. Close with a defense or reflection: The strongest evidence in my work is ____.

Evidence: Student work sample, teacher observation, oral explanation, correction/revision, and weekly subject mastery evidence.

Week 12 Evidence Crosswalk · Day 1: Define the central biological variation and sampling uncertainty question, label the quantities or source claims, and distinguish an observation from an inference.

Evidence check: Explain the example, identify an assumption and correct one error. This formative exercise does not change official assessment scores.
