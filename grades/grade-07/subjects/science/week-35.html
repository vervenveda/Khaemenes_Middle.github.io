Week 35 · Science & Engineering

Capstone Studio and Oral Defense

How can I defend my evidence clearly, respectfully, and publicly?

Monday: Seminar Launch and Academic Vocabulary

Focus: weather/climate

Objective: Students strengthen science & engineering through weather/climate while investigating 'Capstone Studio and Oral Defense'.

Activity: Introduce the subject focus, connect the essential question, model middle-school thinking, and define academic vocabulary.

Workshop: Science & Engineering A++ workshop: create, solve, read, test, discuss, calculate, revise, or present evidence about capstone studio and oral defense.

Teacher script: Name the subject: Science & Engineering. Connect to the essential question: How can I defend my evidence clearly, respectfully, and publicly? Model the focus skill: weather/climate. Require evidence, calculation, model, vocabulary, citation note, or clear explanation. Close with a defense or reflection: The strongest evidence in my work is ____.

Evidence: Student work sample, teacher observation, oral explanation, correction/revision, and weekly subject mastery evidence.
