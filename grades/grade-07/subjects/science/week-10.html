Week 10 · Science & Engineering

Surface Area, Volume, and Modeling

How can three-dimensional models help solve real-world problems?

Monday: Seminar Launch and Academic Vocabulary

Focus: biological surface-area and volume models

Objective: Students strengthen science & engineering through biological surface-area and volume models while investigating 'Surface Area, Volume, and Modeling'.

Activity: Introduce the subject focus, connect the essential question, model middle-school thinking, and define academic vocabulary.

Workshop: Science & Engineering A++ workshop: create, solve, read, test, discuss, calculate, revise, or present evidence about surface area, volume, and modeling.

Teacher script: Name the subject: Science & Engineering. Connect to the essential question: How can three-dimensional models help solve real-world problems? Model the focus skill: biological surface-area and volume models. Require evidence, calculation, model, vocabulary, citation note, or clear explanation. Close with a defense or reflection: The strongest evidence in my work is ____.

Evidence: Student work sample, teacher observation, oral explanation, correction/revision, and weekly subject mastery evidence.

Week 10 Evidence Crosswalk · Day 1: Define the central biological surface-area and volume models question, label the quantities or source claims, and distinguish an observation from an inference.

Evidence check: Explain the example, identify an assumption and correct one error. This formative exercise does not change official assessment scores.
