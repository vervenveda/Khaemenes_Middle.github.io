Grade 05 → Grade 06 Bridge

Advanced learners can move ahead without being mislabeled.

Grade 05 already establishes the academic foundation for Middle School. The bridge adds challenge where Grade 06 becomes more abstract or introduces a new strand.

Math

Fractions, decimals, coordinates, volume, and multi-step reasoning expand into ratios, rates, percent, rational numbers, expressions, equations, inequalities, and statistics.

Literacy

Accurate quotation, essays, research, and revision expand into formal citation, argument analysis, disciplinary literacy, and longer evidence-based writing.

Science

Matter, Earth systems, ecosystems, engineering, and models expand into systems science, data analysis, forces, waves, cells, weather/climate, and CER.

Global

Grade 06 introduces a dedicated World Languages & Global Culture hall, making it a useful advanced-preview strand.

Research

Grade 05 source notes and capstones become more independent Middle School research notebooks, citation systems, project planning, and oral defense.

Rule

Preview access never creates a Grade 06 formal record. Promotion remains an explicit Academy/family placement action.

Advanced preview mode: use Grade 06 curriculum for enrichment, diagnostic challenge, and preparation. Formal Grade 05 records remain authoritative until the Academy Family Registry deliberately changes placement.

Grade 08 → High School

Readiness is evidence. Placement is a decision.

Grade 08 adds a formal transition-plan gate to its certificate. Completing Middle School demonstrates readiness but does not automatically rewrite the learner as a High School student.

36
